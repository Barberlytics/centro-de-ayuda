# Switch roles

**In short:** if you're an owner and you also work as a barber, your account can have two roles, which the app calls profiles. You'll see them in the account menu and choose which one you work with. You don't have to sign in again.

## Steps

1. Tap your account icon, at the top right.
2. In the menu you'll see your name once for each role, with the role name below it, for example **Business** and
   **Barber comission**. The role you're using now has a checkmark.
   ![The account menu with both roles](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/cambiar-de-rol/menu-con-roles.png)
3. Tap the role you want to work with.

## What happens when you switch

- The screen takes you to **Calendar** if you switch to **Barber comission**, and to **Metrics** if you go back to **Business**.
- The menu changes: with **Barber comission**, you won't see **Services & Products**, **Payroll** or **Transactions**.
- In **Settings**, only **Time format** and **Delete account** are left.

The details are in [I have two profiles: owner and barber](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/tienes-dos-perfiles).

## Frequently asked questions

**I don't see two roles in my menu.**
Then your account has just one role.

**What can each role do?**
The owner sets this in **Settings › Permissions**. There's a role for the Location administrator, the Commission barber, the Rent barber, the Receptionist and the Owner. Owner accounts have full access. See the [permissions table](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol).
