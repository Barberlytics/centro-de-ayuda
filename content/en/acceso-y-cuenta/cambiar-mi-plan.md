# Change your Barberlytics plan

**In short:** the **Change plan** button is in **My profile**, in the **Current plan** section. Before you tap it, check your plan name, its price and the **Next renewal** date right there.

## Steps

1. Tap your account icon, at the top right.
2. Tap **My profile**. The screen says “Manage your account, plan and payment methods.”
   ![The My profile screen with the Current plan section and the Change plan button](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/mi-perfil/pantalla.png)
3. Find the **Current plan** section. There you'll see the plan you have (for example, **Annual**), its price and the **Next renewal**.
4. Tap **Change plan**.

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows after you tap **Change plan** (the available plans, when the new one starts being charged, whether there's a prorated charge) hasn't been seen yet. This article will be completed once that step has been walked through. If you have questions before changing, write to us.

## What to check first

In **Settings › Billing details and invoices** you'll find everything your account pays:

- **Billing details**: your plan, the period, the amount and the address, with **Edit billing details**.
- **Default payment method**: the card that gets charged, with **Manage payment methods**.
- **List of invoices and fees**: each invoice with its **Type**, **Total** and **Date**, and a note about when the next one will be ready (“Your next invoice will be available on AUG 28, 2027”).

![The Billing details and invoices screen](/assets/es/configuracion/detalles-de-facturacion-y-facturas/pantalla.png)

The details are in [Billing details and invoices](/ayuda/configuracion/detalles-de-facturacion-y-facturas).

## Frequently asked questions

**When is the plan charged?**
In **My profile**, the **Current plan** section shows the **Next renewal** date. It's charged to the card marked **DEFAULT** ([Manage the card you pay Barberlytics with](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/metodos-de-pago-de-tu-plan)).

**Does this change what I charge my clients?**
No. Your plan is what you pay Barberlytics. What you charge your clients is in **Transactions**.

**Where do I see how much I've paid so far?**
In the **Invoice history** in **My profile** or in the “List of invoices and fees” in Settings ([Download your Barberlytics invoices](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/descargar-tus-facturas)).
