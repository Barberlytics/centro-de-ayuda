# Sign out

**In short:** tap your account icon, at the top right, and choose **Sign out**.

## Steps

1. At the top right, tap your account icon.
   ![The account menu with Sign out at the bottom](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/cerrar-sesion/menu-de-cuenta.png)
2. At the bottom of the menu, tap **Sign out**.

To use Barberlytics again, you'll have to sign in again.

> [!TIP]
> If you use a computer that several people at the barbershop share, sign out when you're done.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where is dark mode?**
In the same menu, above **Sign out**. See [Language and dark mode](/ayuda/navegacion/idioma-y-modo-oscuro).

**I have two roles. Do I have to sign out to switch between them?**
No. See [Switch roles](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/cambiar-de-rol).
