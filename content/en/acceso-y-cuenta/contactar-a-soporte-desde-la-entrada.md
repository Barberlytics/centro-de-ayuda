# Contact support from the sign-in screen

**In short:** if you can't get in, tap the help button at the bottom right, fill out the
**Contact us** form and tap **Send**.

## Where it is

On every sign-in screen there's a black circle with a question mark, at the bottom right.
![The help button, at the bottom right of the sign-in screen](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/contactar-a-soporte-desde-la-entrada/boton-de-ayuda.png)

You also get to the same form from **I can't receive the email**, on the
[Forgot your number](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/olvidaste-tu-numero) screen.

## When to write

- You see “The user has been blocked for security reasons” ([what to do](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/usuario-bloqueado-por-seguridad)).
- The code doesn't arrive even though you tap **Resend code**.
- You don't remember your number and you don't get the email from **Forgot your number?** either.

## Steps

1. Tap the help button. **Contact us** opens: “Fill in the following information so we can help you as soon as
   possible.”
   ![The Contact us form](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/contactar-a-soporte-desde-la-entrada/formulario.png)
2. Fill in the five fields. All of them are required:
   - **Full name\***
   - **Company\***: the name of your barbershop.
   - **Email\***: the email where you want us to reply.
   - **Phone\***: choose your country with the little flag, just like on the sign-in screen.
   - **Tell us what happened\***: explain the problem in your own words.
3. Tap **Send**.

To go back, tap **← Back**.

## Errors you may see

| What the screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| “This field is required” | You left a field empty. It appears below each field that's missing. | Fill it in. All five fields are required. |

## Frequently asked questions

**What do I put in “Tell us what happened”?**
Anything that helps us find your account: the number you're trying to sign in with, since when it's been happening and what you see on the screen.
