# Download your Barberlytics invoices

**In short:** your invoices are in two places: in **My profile**, in the **Invoice history** section, and in **Settings › Billing details and invoices**, in the “List of invoices and fees.” You can download them from both.

## From My profile

1. Tap your account icon, at the top right.
2. Tap **My profile**.
3. In **Invoice history** you see each invoice with its date and amount.
4. Use the download icon on the invoice you want.

## From Settings

1. Go to **Settings › Billing details and invoices**.
2. Scroll down to the “List of invoices and fees.” Each row shows the **Type**, the **Total** and the **Date**.
3. Tap **Download** on the invoice you want.

To find an invoice, filter the list by **Type**, **Year** and **Month**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are these invoices for my clients' appointments?**
No. They're the invoices for what you pay Barberlytics. What you charge your clients is in **Transactions**.

**How do I find an invoice from several months ago?**
In **Settings › Billing details and invoices**, filter by **Year** and by **Month**.

**How do I change the details that appear on my invoice?**
In **Settings › Billing details and invoices**, with the **Edit billing details** button.
