# Choose your phone's country

**In short:** the **Phone** field starts with **United States (+1)**. Tap the little flag on the
left to change it to **Colombia** or **Venezuela**.

## Steps

1. On the sign-in screen, tap the little flag at the start of the **Phone** field.
2. A short list of the available countries opens: **United States**, **Colombia** and **Venezuela**.
   ![The country list for the Phone field](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/elegir-el-pais-del-telefono/lista-de-paises.png)
3. Tap your country. The field changes the prefix to that country's.
4. Enter your number without the prefix and tap **Sign In**.

## Frequently asked questions

**My country isn't on the list.**
Right now only the United States, Colombia and Venezuela are available. If your number is from another country, use the help button at the bottom
right to write to us.

**It says “This field is too short.”**
Check that you chose the right country and entered the full number. See
[Sign in with your phone number](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/ingresar).
