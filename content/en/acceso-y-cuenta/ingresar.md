# Sign in with your phone number

**In short:** on the sign-in screen you enter your **Phone** and tap **Sign In**. Then you confirm
it's you with a code. There's no password to remember.

![The Barberlytics sign-in screen with the Phone field and the Sign In button](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/ingresar/pantalla-de-entrada.png)

## Steps

1. Open **dash.barberlytics.com**. You'll see the message “Start managing, measuring and maximizing your barbershop.” and the
   **Phone\*** field.
2. If your number isn't from the United States, choose your country with the little flag on the left of the field. See
   [Choose your phone's country](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/elegir-el-pais-del-telefono).
3. Enter your number. The field only accepts numbers.
4. Tap **Sign In**.
5. Continue with [Confirm your number with the code](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/confirmar-el-codigo).

## Errors you may see

| What the screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| “This field is required” | You tapped **Sign In** without entering your number. | Enter your number and tap the button again. |
| “This field is too short” | The number is missing digits. | Check that you entered the full number, with the right country. |

![The “This field is required” message below the Phone field](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/ingresar/campo-vacio.png)

## Frequently asked questions

**Why doesn't it ask for a password?**
There's no password on this screen: you sign in with your phone and confirm it's you with a code that arrives on your phone.

**Can I create my account from here?**
The sign-in screen doesn't have a button to create an account, but sign-up does exist: it opens with a separate link.
See [Register your barbershop on Barberlytics](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/registrar-tu-barberia). If you're part of a
barbershop's team, ask the owner to add you in **Team**.

**I type letters and nothing happens.**
That's normal: the **Phone** field only accepts numbers.
