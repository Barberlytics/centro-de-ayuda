# Manage the card you pay Barberlytics with

**In short:** your card appears in **My profile**, in the **Payment methods** section, with the **Manage** button. You can also get there from **Settings › Billing details and invoices**, with **Manage payment methods**.

> [!NOTE]
> This card is the one you use to pay for your Barberlytics plan. It has nothing to do with the payments your clients make to you.

## See your card

1. Tap your account icon, at the top right.
2. Tap **My profile**.
3. Find the **Payment methods** section. There you see your card with the last four digits. The one marked **DEFAULT** is the one charged by default.

## Get to your payment methods

You have two ways:

- In **My profile**, tap **Manage**.
- In **Settings › Billing details and invoices**, look at the “Default payment method” section and tap **Manage payment methods**.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens after you tap these buttons (adding a new card, changing the default one) isn't documented in this help center yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this where I set up how my clients pay me?**
No. This is only the card you pay your Barberlytics plan with.

**How do I know which card is my default?**
It's the one marked **DEFAULT** in the **Payment methods** section.

**Where do I see when my plan renews?**
In **My profile**, in the **Current plan** section, next to **Next renewal**.
