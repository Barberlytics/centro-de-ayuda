# My profile: your account, your plan and your payment methods

**In short:** in **My profile** you see your account details, the plan you pay for Barberlytics, the card
you pay it with and your invoices. It opens from your account icon, at the top right.

## How to get there

1. Tap your account icon, at the top right.
2. Tap **My profile**.

The screen says: “Manage your account, plan and payment methods.”

![The My profile screen with its four sections](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/mi-perfil/pantalla.png)

## What's in each section

- **Account information:** your name, your phone, the language (for example, EN) and your time zone.
- **Current plan:** the plan you have (for example, **Annual**), its price and the **Next
  renewal** date. The **Change plan** button lets you pick another one.
- **Payment methods:** your card, with the last four digits and the **DEFAULT** mark on the one
  charged by default. The **Manage** button opens the management of your payment methods.
- **Invoice history:** each invoice with its date and amount. The icon on the right downloads it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this where my clients' appointments get paid?**
No. This is only what you pay Barberlytics. What you charge your clients is in
[Transactions](/ayuda/navegacion/las-secciones-de-barberlytics).

**Where do I change my billing address?**
In **Settings › Billing details and invoices**, with the **Edit billing details** button.
