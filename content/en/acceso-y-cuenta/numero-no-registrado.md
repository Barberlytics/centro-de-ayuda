# What to do if your number isn't registered

**In short:** to sign in, your phone has to be saved in your barbershop: the owner or the administrator enters it when they add you in **Team** (**Phone\*** field). If you enter a different number, you won't be able to receive the code.

## What to check

1. That the country is the right one on the sign-in screen (**+57** Colombia, **+1** United States, **+58** Venezuela).
   ![The country list on the sign-in screen](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/elegir-el-pais-del-telefono/lista-de-paises.png)
2. That the number is the same one you gave your barbershop.
3. If you changed phones, ask the owner to update it in **Team › Actions › Edit** ([Edit a team member](/ayuda/equipo/editar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo)).
4. If you don't remember which one they registered you with, use **Forgot your number?** ([Forgot your number](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/olvidaste-tu-numero)).

> [!NOTE]
> The exact message the app shows when the number doesn't exist isn't documented yet: it will be checked with a real phone.

## Frequently asked questions

**I'm the owner and I can't sign in with my number.**
Write to support from **Contact us** on the sign-in screen ([Contact support from the sign-in screen](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/contactar-a-soporte-desde-la-entrada)).

**Can I sign in with an email instead of a phone?**
No. Signing in is only with a phone and a code.
