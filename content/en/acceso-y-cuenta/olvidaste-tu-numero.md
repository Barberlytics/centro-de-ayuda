# Forgot your number

**In short:** on the sign-in screen tap **Forgot your number?**, enter the email you're registered with and
tap **Send email**. You'll get an email with your phone number or with the option to change it.

## Steps

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot your number?**, below the **Phone** field.
   ![The Forgot your number? link below the Phone field](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/olvidaste-tu-numero/enlace.png)
2. **Enter your email address** opens. Type your email in the **Email\*** field: the one registered with
   Barberlytics.
   ![The Enter your email address screen](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/olvidaste-tu-numero/pantalla.png)
3. Tap **Send email**.

The screen explains what you'll receive: “an email to the email address registered with Barberlytics
with your phone number or the option to change your phone number.”

## If you can't receive the email

Tap **I can't receive the email**. The contact form opens so you can tell us what happened: enter the email you think you have registered and, if you know it, a phone number you've signed in with before. See
[Contact support from the sign-in screen](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/contactar-a-soporte-desde-la-entrada).

To go back, tap **← Back**.

## Errors you may see

| What the screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| “This field is required” | You tapped **Send email** without entering your email. | Enter your email and tap the button again. |
| “This field has an invalid format” | What you entered doesn't look like an email: the @ or the domain is missing. | Check it and enter it in full, for example `name@email.com`. |

![The “This field has an invalid format” message below the Email field](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/olvidaste-tu-numero/correo-invalido.png)

## Frequently asked questions

**I don't remember which email I was registered with.**
Ask whoever gave you access to the barbershop. If you don't know, write to us from the contact form.

**Do I change my number here?**
The email we send you has the option to change it. From this screen you only ask for that email.
