# Privacy policy and terms of service

**In short:** when you sign in you accept Barberlytics' conditions. The full document is at the
**Privacy policy and terms of service** link, at the bottom of the sign-in card.

## Where to find it

1. On the sign-in screen, look for the text “By signing in you are accepting our.”
2. Tap the blue **Privacy policy and terms of service** link, right below it.
   ![The Privacy policy and terms of service link at the bottom of the card](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/politica-de-privacidad-y-terminos/enlace.png)

The **Terms and Conditions** page opens at `dash.barberlytics.com/terms/terms-and-conditions`.

## What you need to know

- **The document is in English** (“Terms of Service”) and its last update says **March 16, 2025**.
- Among other topics, it covers: Barberlytics' services, payments, accounts and closing them, loyalty programs,
  communications, use and limits of the service, privacy and security.
- This page only tells you where it is and what it's about. To know exactly what it says, read it in full.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I sign in without accepting the terms?**
The text on the screen says that by signing in you are accepting them.
