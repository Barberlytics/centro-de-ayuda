# Register your barbershop on Barberlytics

**In short:** sign-up is a form called **Get started**. You open it with this link:
**https://dash.barberlytics.com/authenticate/signup**. You fill in eight fields and tap **Send**.

![The Get started form with its eight fields and the Send button](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/registrar-tu-barberia/formulario.png)

> [!NOTE]
> For now there's no button on the sign-in screen that takes you to sign-up. You have to open the link directly.

## Before you start

Have these on hand:
- Your first and last name.
- The name of your barbershop.
- An email you can open.
- Your phone. It's the number you'll use to sign in later ([Sign in with your phone number](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/ingresar)).
- How many barbers your team has.

## Steps

1. Open **https://dash.barberlytics.com/authenticate/signup**. You'll see “Get started” and “Enter this information to sign up.”
2. Fill in the fields. All of them have an asterisk and are required:
   - **First name\***
   - **Last name\***
   - **Company name\***: the name of your barbershop.
   - **Email\***
   - **Phone\***: if your number isn't from the United States, change the little flag before entering it ([Choose your phone's country](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/elegir-el-pais-del-telefono)).
   - **How many barbers does your team have\***
   - **Country\***: right now you can choose **Colombia** or **United States of America**.
   - **Time zone\***: for example, **America/Bogota** for Colombia
3. Tap **Send**.

To go back, tap **← Back**. At the top right, the globe switches the language between **ES** and **EN**.

## Errors you may see

![The eight fields with the “This field is required” message below each one](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/registrar-tu-barberia/campos-obligatorios.png)

| What the screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| “This field is required” | You left a field empty. It shows in red below each one that's missing. | Fill it in. All eight fields are required. |
| “User already exists” | It shows in a red notice above the **Send** button, with all the fields filled in. There's already an account with that information. | Don't sign up again: sign in with your phone ([Sign in with your phone number](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/ingresar)). If you don't remember which number, see [Forgot your number](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/olvidaste-tu-numero). |

## After you sign up

Once your account is created, you always sign in with your phone and a code, not a password. See
[Sign in with your phone number](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/ingresar) and
[Confirm your number with the code](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/confirmar-el-codigo).

## Frequently asked questions

**Why can't I find a “Create account” button?**
It doesn't exist yet on the sign-in screen. Sign-up only opens with the link above.

**Which country and time zone do I choose?**
Your barbershop's. The time zone sets the times of your appointments.

**It says “User already exists.”**
That means that sign-up has already been done. Sign in with your phone and the code. If you never signed up yourself, or you can't get in, write to us with the help button ([Contact support from the sign-in screen](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/contactar-a-soporte-desde-la-entrada)).

**I made a mistake in one field and I already tapped Send.**
Write to us with the help button, at the bottom right ([Contact support from the sign-in screen](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/contactar-a-soporte-desde-la-entrada)).
