# I have two profiles: owner and barber

**In short:** if you're an owner and you also work as a barber, your account can have two profiles: **Business** for managing and **Barber comission** for serving clients. It's the same account, with the same phone.

## What you see in each one

| | Business | Barber comission |
|---|---|---|
| When you switch, it takes you to | Metrics | Calendar |
| Menu | All the sections | No Services & Products, Payroll or Transactions |
| Calendar | All barbers | Only your column |
| Metrics | The whole team, five tabs | Your numbers, three tabs |
| Settings | All the settings | Only **Time format** and **Delete account** |

## How to switch

1. Tap your account icon, at the top right.
2. Tap the profile you want to switch to.

It doesn't ask you to sign in again.

> [!TIP]
> Use the barber profile to see the barbershop the way your team sees it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do my clients and my data change when I switch profiles?**
No. What you see and what you can do changes. The barbershop is the same.

**Why does my barber account only see my calendar?**
Because the barber profile only shows that person's schedule.
