# Add another appointment to the same booking

**In short:** on **Confirm appointment**, the **Add appointment** button opens **Service/Barber** again so you can add one more appointment. Each appointment has its own service, barber, time and price, and they are all booked together with the same button.

![Confirm appointment with two no-preference appointments: Men's Haircut at 10:30 AM and Signature Service at 11:00 AM](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/agregar-otra-cita/dos-citas.png)

## Steps

1. On **Confirm appointment**, tap **Add appointment**.
2. Choose the service and the barber (or **No preference**) and tap **Next**.
   ![Adding another service to the booking](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente/agregar-cita.png)
3. Choose the date and time and tap **Next**.
4. You're back on **Confirm appointment** with both cards, one below the other.
5. Repeat to add more, or continue with **Book now!** ([Pay for the booking](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/pagar-la-reserva)).

If your client changes their mind halfway, they tap **Discard and go back to summary**: they return to **Confirm appointment** without the new appointment.

## Edit or delete an appointment

Each card has two buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Edit** | Opens **Service/Barber** with that appointment already selected, so they can change the service, the barber or the time. **Discard and go back to summary** leaves the appointment as it was. |
| **Delete** | Removes the appointment from the booking right away, without asking. |

![Edit: the appointment's service already selected, with Next and Discard and go back to summary](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/agregar-otra-cita/editar.png)

If they delete all of them, the screen says “No appointments yet” · “Start a new appointment to continue.” with the **Start over** button.

![Confirm appointment with no appointments: No appointments yet and Start over](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/agregar-otra-cita/sin-citas.png)

## Frequently asked questions

**Can it be used to book for two people?**
All the appointments are saved under the client who entered their phone number. For another person, it's best that they book with their own phone number.

**Does the client see the total for all the appointments?**
On **Confirm appointment** they see the price of each one. The total appears on **Pay $…** and in **Your order**, after booking ([The confirmed appointment](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/cita-confirmada)).

**Can they set both appointments at the same time?**
Yes, if there are free barbers. For example, two appointments with **No preference** at 10:00 AM can end up with two different barbers.
