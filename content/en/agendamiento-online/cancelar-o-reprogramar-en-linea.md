# How late a client can cancel or reschedule online

**In short:** in **Settings › Online booking**, the “Online cancellation and rescheduling” block sets how many hours before their appointment a client can cancel or move it on their own, how much is charged if they cancel late, and how many times they can cancel online. Past that limit, the client has to call you.

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Online booking**, “Online cancellation and rescheduling” block: “Set how far in advance clients can cancel or reschedule; after this period, clients must call to change their appointment.”

![The Online cancellation and rescheduling block with the notice, the late cancellation fee and the maximum cancellations](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/cancelar-o-reprogramar-en-linea/bloque.png)

## The three settings

| Setting | What you choose |
|---|---|
| **Clients can cancel or reschedule online** | The limit: “Up to 24 hours in advance,” 12, 6, 3, 2 or 1 hour, or “Up to 30 minutes in advance.” |
| **Late cancellation fee\*** | The percentage charged if they cancel within that limit. |
| **Maximum number of online cancellations allowed\*** | How many times they can cancel online. The screen warns: “Once the client reaches this limit, they can no longer cancel online. To cancel, they must contact the barbershop directly.” |

Tap **Save** at the bottom of the page.

## Example

With “Up to 3 hours in advance,” a fee of `10` and a maximum of `5`:

- Laura has an appointment at 5:00 p.m. At 1:30 p.m. she can cancel or move it herself.
- At 2:30 p.m. she can no longer do it online: she has to call the barbershop. If she cancels, the policy says 10% is charged.
- If Laura has already canceled online 5 times, the sixth time she has to call.

These values also fill in the policy text your client sees. See [Booking and cancellation policies](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/politicas-de-reserva).

> [!NOTE]
> Whether the late cancellation fee is charged automatically or has to be charged by hand, and whether the cancellation count resets, hasn't been checked yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where does the client cancel or reschedule?**
In the **Client portal**. The portal screen says: “Rescheduling and canceling respect the minimum times you've set in Online booking.” See [Client portal](/ayuda/configuracion/portal-del-cliente).

**And if I cancel the appointment from the calendar?**
These limits are for what the client does online. From the calendar you can still cancel. See [Cancel an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cancelar-una-cita).
