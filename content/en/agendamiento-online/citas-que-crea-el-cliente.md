# Appointments the client creates: when they arrive and how you receive them

**In short:** when your client books on their own, through the link or widget in **Settings › Online booking**, the appointment goes into the calendar like any other and counts in **Metrics** as an **Online appointment**. What exactly shows up in the calendar and in the alert when that happens hasn't been checked yet with a real appointment.

## How they arrive

Your client books through one of the options you turn on in **Settings › Online booking** (“Online booking”):

- **Side panel**: a floating “Book now” button on your website.
- **Open from link**: a button or link of yours opens booking.
- **Embedded booking**: booking inside your page.
- **Direct booking link**: a link to share on Instagram or WhatsApp.

These are explained in [Online booking: what it is](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/que-es) and, for the link, [Direct booking link and UTM](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/enlace-directo-y-utm).

## How to tell them apart

- **In the appointment history.** Open the appointment and tap **History**. Under **APPOINTMENT ACTIVITY** (“Cannot be edited”), an appointment created by your team says “Appointment created · At the barbershop · Laura · 25/SEP/2026, 10:16 am.”
  ![The appointment's History tab, with the line Appointment created · At the barbershop](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/historial.png)
- **In Metrics › Business.** The **Booking method** donut separates **Internal** (booked by your team), **Online appointment** (booked by the client) and **Walk-in**. See [Booking method and appointments](/ayuda/metricas/metodo-de-agendamiento-y-citas).
- **In Metrics › Clients.** The **Online bookings** card tells you what share of your appointments the client booked. See [Online bookings and walk-in rate](/ayuda/metricas/clientes-reservas-online-y-rate-walk-in).
- **In the alert.** The bell at the top opens **Notifications**; calendar alerts start with **Agenda:**, for example “Agenda: The client Laura booked an appointment.” See [See your notifications](/ayuda/navegacion/notificaciones).

> [!NOTE]
> What the **History** of a client-made appointment says, how its card looks in the calendar, whether someone needs to confirm it and what the client receives hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed with a test online appointment.

## What the screen says

| Text | What it means |
|---|---|
| “Appointment created · At the barbershop” | The appointment was created by someone on your team from the app |
| **Online appointment** (Metrics) | Appointments the client booked on their own |
| **Internal** (Metrics) | Appointments your team booked |
| **Agenda:** (Notifications) | The alerts that come from the calendar |

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to accept the appointment the client made?**
It hasn't been checked yet. In the calendar walkthrough, no approval step was seen.

**Can the client choose a barber?**
It depends on each barber's **Visibility for booking**, in their record. How it looks for the client is covered in [Online bookings: how your client sees them](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente).

**Can the client cancel on their own?**
Yes, if **Clients can cancel appointments** is turned on in the barber's record, **Options** tab, with the time limit in **Hours** and **Minutes**.
