# If it's their first time: Let's get to know you

**In short:** if the phone number the client enters isn't in your barbershop, booking shows **Let's get to know you** and asks for **First name**, **Last name** and **Email**. When they tap **Continue**, their profile is created in **Clients** and they move on to choosing a location or service.

![The Let's get to know you screen with the phone number, First name, Last name and Email](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/cliente-nuevo/conozcamonos.png)

## What they see

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Title | **Let's get to know you** · “We'll use this information to create your profile and make booking even easier.” |
| **Phone number** | The phone number they entered, with its country. It can't be changed here: to fix it, tap the arrow at the top and enter it again. |
| **First name** | “Enter your first name” |
| **Last name** | “Enter your last name” |
| **Email** | “Enter your email” |
| Note | “We'll never share your information.” |
| **Continue** | It turns on once all three fields have something in them. |

The arrow at the top left goes back to the phone number screen.

## What happens when they tap Continue

1. The client is created in your barbershop, with the phone number, first name, last name and email they entered.
2. If you have more than one location, they go on to choose a **Location**. If you have only one, they go straight to **Service/Barber** ([Choose location, service and barber](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/elegir-sede-servicio-y-barbero)).

The next time they book with that phone number, the page already recognizes them and offers to repeat their last visit ([If they're already a client: repeat their last visit](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/cliente-que-vuelve)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Do they have to create a password?**
No. Just their phone number and these three details.

**The client has already been to the barbershop, but the page treats them as new.**
The phone number they entered doesn't match the one you have in their record. Check the number and the country in **Clients**.

> [!NOTE]
> This was walked through with a test number without tapping **Continue**, so as not to create an extra client. Whether the page checks the email format or warns when that email already exists is still to be confirmed.
