# If they're already a client: repeat their last visit

**In short:** when the phone number is already in your barbershop, the page greets the client by name and has their last visit ready: the same location, the same service and the same barber. They only pick the day and time and tap **Continue**.

![The welcome with the last visit preselected and the available times](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente/bienvenida.png)

## What they see

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Greeting | “Welcome back, Laura.” and “We remember your last visit so you can book faster. Pick your date and time to continue.” |
| **Choose something different** | Button to go to the full flow and pick something else |
| **WE PRESELECTED YOUR LAST VISIT** | Three cards, each with **Change**: **Location**, **Your last service** (with duration and price, for example “30 min • $50.00”) and **Your last barber** (with their level) |
| **Pick the time of your appointment** | “Select a day and an available time that works for you.” Three days (for example “Fri Sep 25,” “Sat Sep 26,” “Sun Sep 27”) and **More** to see others |
| Times | “Available times for Fri, Sep 25” with the free times for their barber and arrows to see more |
| Buttons | **← Back** and **Continue** (turns on once they pick a time). **Continue** goes to **Confirm appointment**. |

**More** opens the full **Date and time** step, with the month calendar, the times grouped as **Morning**, **Afternoon** and **Evening**, and the **No preference** option ([Choose date and time](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/elegir-fecha-y-hora)).

![What More opens: the month, the day and their barber's times by Morning and Afternoon](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/cliente-que-vuelve/mas.png)

## To change just one thing

They tap **Change** on the card they want to change (for example, another barber) and leave the others as they were. If they want to start from scratch, **Choose something different**.

## Frequently asked questions

**How does the page know who they are?**
By their phone number. If that number is already in **Clients**, the page recognizes it and uses their last appointment.

**What if their usual barber has no times that day?**
They'll only see that barber's free times. They can tap **Change** on **Your last barber** or pick another day. See [The client can't find available times](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/no-hay-horas-disponibles).

**What does a new client see?**
First it asks for their first name, last name and email. See [If it's their first time: Let's get to know you](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/cliente-nuevo).
