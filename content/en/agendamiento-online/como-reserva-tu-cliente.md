# How your client books, step by step

**In short:** your client books from a public Barberlytics page, without downloading anything or creating an account. They enter their phone number; if you already know them, the page offers to repeat their last visit; if not, they choose **Location**, **Service/Barber**, **Date and time** and **Confirm**. The appointment goes into your calendar as an **Online appointment**.

## How they get to the page

- Through your **Direct booking link**, the one you share on WhatsApp or Instagram ([The direct booking link](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/enlace-directo-y-utm)).
- Through the **Book now** button or the booking embedded in your website ([Floating button](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/panel-lateral), [From your own button](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/abrir-desde-un-enlace), [Embedded in a page](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/embebido)).

## 1. Enter their phone number

![The first screen: the phone number and the barbershop card](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente/paso-1.png)

- **Book your appointment** · “Enter your phone number to get started. We'll use it to find your profile and send you updates about your appointment.”
- **Phone number** with the country (**+57**, **+1**, **+58**) and the note “Only for updates about your appointment.”
- Your location's card: logo, name, “Open today: 08:00 AM to 11:50 PM” and “Need help? Call …” with the location's phone number.
- **Continue** turns on when they enter the number and shows “Loading...” while it looks up the client.

On a phone it looks the same, in a single column.

![The booking page on a phone](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente/celular.png)

## 2. If they're already a client, it offers to repeat

“Welcome back, Laura.” with their last location, service and barber already chosen: they only pick a day and time. It's covered in [If they're already a client: repeat their last visit](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/cliente-que-vuelve).

If it's their first time, it first asks for their first name, last name and email ([If it's their first time: Let's get to know you](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/cliente-nuevo)).

## 3. Choose location, service and barber

At the top they see the steps **Location › Service/Barber › Date and time › Confirm**. They choose the location, the service and the barber (or **No preference**), and see each barber's price. It's covered in [Choose location, service and barber](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/elegir-sede-servicio-y-barbero).

## 4. Choose date and time

The days of the month and the barber's free times, under **Morning**, **Afternoon** and **Evening**. With **No preference** they see the times of any barber ([No preference: how the barber is assigned](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/sin-preferencia-al-reservar)). It's covered in [Choose date and time](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/elegir-fecha-y-hora).

## 5. Confirm

They review the appointment and can **Edit**, **Delete** or **Add appointment** ([Add another appointment](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/agregar-otra-cita)). It's covered in [Confirm the booking](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/confirmar-la-reserva).

## 6. Book

If the location only charges at the barbershop, they tap **Book now and pay at the shop** and that's it. If it has other ways to pay, they tap **Book now!**, choose **Pay at the shop** and tap **Pay $…**. It's covered in [Book and pay at the barbershop](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/pagar-la-reserva).

## 7. Confirmed

They see their appointments with **Add to calendar**, the total and **Book another appointment**. It's covered in [The confirmed appointment](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/cita-confirmada).

## Afterward

The appointment shows up in your calendar and the history says how it came in. It's covered in [Appointments the client creates](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/citas-que-crea-el-cliente).

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the client need an account?**
No. Just their phone number. The page says it uses it “to find your profile and send you updates about your appointment.”

**How long does it take?**
The page promises “Takes less than 1 minute.” A returning client only picks a day and time.

**What do I control of what they see?**
Which services and barbers appear, their prices, the free times and the closed days. See [What decides what your client sees](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/que-ve-tu-cliente).
