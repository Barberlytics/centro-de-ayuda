# Confirm the booking

**In short:** on **Confirm appointment** the client reviews their appointment (service, name, date and time, barber and price) and taps **Book now!**. Before that, they can **Edit**, **Delete** or **Add appointment** to add another service.

![The confirmation with the service, the client, the date, the barber and the price](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente/confirmar.png)

## What they see

- **Confirm appointment** · “Check that your appointment information is correct.”
- One card per appointment with the service, its name, the date and time (“Sep 25, 2026 at 04:00 PM (30 min)”), “With <barber>” and the price.
- **Edit**: choose the service and barber again.
- **Delete**: removes that appointment from the booking.
- **Add appointment**: adds another appointment to the same booking ([Add another appointment to the same booking](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/agregar-otra-cita)).
- The button to finish, which changes depending on how the client can pay.

## The button to finish

| The button says | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Book now and pay at the shop** | The client can only pay at the barbershop. | Books the appointment and goes to **Confirmed**. |
| **Book now!** | The location has other ways to pay. | Opens the **Pay** screen to choose the way to pay. The appointment is booked there, with **Pay $…**. |

It's covered in [Book and pay at the barbershop](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/pagar-la-reserva). What they see afterward is in [The confirmed appointment](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/cita-confirmada).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can they book a haircut and a beard trim in the same visit?**
Yes: with **Add appointment** they add the second appointment before booking.

**Where do I see the appointment they booked?**
In your calendar, in the barber's column. See [Appointments the client creates](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/citas-que-crea-el-cliente).
