# Choose location, service and barber

**In short:** in the full flow, the steps **Location › Service/Barber › Date and time › Confirm** show at the top. The client taps their location, then a service and then a barber (or **No preference**), and moves on with **Next**.

## Location

![Choosing a location, with one card per location](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente/elegir-diferente.png)

One card per location with its name, its address, its hours (“08:00 to 23:50”) and **Location details**. Tapping one moves to the next step. If your barbershop has only one location, they still see it.

## Service

![The list of services by category, with duration and price](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente/servicios.png)

- Two tabs: **Services** and **Barbers**. **Barbers** is turned off, with the hint “Select a service,” until they pick one.
- Category filter: **All** and each category of your services.
- Each service shows its duration (“0 h 30 min”) and its “from” price (“$50.00 &UP”): the final price depends on the barber.

## Barber

![The list of barbers with each one's price for the chosen service](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente/barberos.png)

- First, **No preference**: they get whichever barber is free, based on your order in **Team** ([No preference: how the barber is assigned](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/sin-preferencia-al-reservar)). If only one barber does the service, the page picks them automatically.
- Then one card per barber with their photo, name, level and **their price for that service**. The price can change from one barber to another (for example $50.00, $60.00, $65.00), based on each one's own prices ([Create a service: who does it](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-servicio-equipo)).
- **View** opens the barber's profile: **Description**, **Level**, the **Services** they do and their **Gallery**, with the **Select this barber** button.
  ![A barber's profile with Select this barber](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente/ver-barbero.png)
- At the bottom it says “Select a barber”; when they pick one, **Next** appears.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does the service price say “&UP”?**
Because it's the price it starts from. The exact price shows up when they choose the barber.

**What happens if they choose No preference?**
On the next step they see the times when any barber is free. The barber is assigned when they book, using your **No preference** order in Team. See [No preference: how the barber is assigned](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/sin-preferencia-al-reservar).

**A service or a barber doesn't show up.**
See [A service or a barber doesn't show up when booking](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/no-aparece-un-servicio-o-barbero).
