# The direct booking link and UTM parameters

**In short:** the **Direct booking link** is the address you share so your clients can book on their own, without needing a website. You copy it with **Copy** and paste it into WhatsApp, your Instagram bio or wherever you like. With “UTM tracking (optional)” you can tag the link to know which channel each booking came from.

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Online booking**. The card says: “Adjust how clients can book and manage their appointments online.” On the “Online booking” screen, scroll down to the “Online booking link for your website” block.

## Copy the link

The screen explains it like this: “Share this link with your clients so they can book online directly. You can add UTM parameters for marketing tracking.”

1. Under **Direct booking link**, tap **Copy**.
   ![The Online booking link block with the Direct booking link, the Copy button and UTM tracking](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/enlace-directo-y-utm/enlace.png)
2. Paste it where your clients will see it: the WhatsApp welcome message, your Instagram bio, an ad.

The link looks like this, where TU-ENLACE is the part that belongs to your barbershop. Always copy it from the screen with **Copy**:

```text
https://fd.barberlytics.com/book/TU-ENLACE
```

## Tag the link with UTM

UTM parameters are a few words added to the end of the link to say where it comes from: for example, one link for Instagram and another for WhatsApp. That way, when a client books, you know which channel they came from. In **Metrics › Marketing** those bookings are split by channel. See [Marketing: channels and campaigns](/ayuda/metricas/marketing-canales-y-campanas).

To use them, open “UTM tracking (optional),” fill in what it asks for and copy the resulting link.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it the same link for all my locations?**
The screen says these are general settings, but that they can be set up for each location. Where that's done hasn't been checked yet.

**Where do I see how many clients book through the link?**
In **Metrics › Business**, the **Booking method** donut counts the appointments marked **Online appointment**. See [Booking method and appointments](/ayuda/metricas/metodo-de-agendamiento-y-citas).

**Do I need UTM for the link to work?**
No. The link works as it is. UTM is optional and only helps you know where each booking comes from.
