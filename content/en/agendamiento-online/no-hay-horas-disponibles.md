# The client can't find available times

**In short:** the page only shows the times when the barber is working, has no blocks or other appointments, and the barbershop isn't closed. If there are none, check these four things or tell the client to accept **No preference**.

## What to check

1. **The barber's schedule that day.** In **Team › Working hours**, their row should have a shift and not “Not working.”
2. **Their blocks.** Lunch, a meeting or a vacation take up those hours ([Block a barber's calendar](/ayuda/calendario/bloquear-el-calendario)).
3. **Their schedule.** If they already have back-to-back appointments, there's no gap for the length of the service.
4. **Closures.** **Settings › Business closure periods** and the **Non-working holidays** under **Business details**. “Online bookings can't be made while your business is closed.”

## What the client can do

- Pick another day on the strip of days (or **More**).
- Accept **No preference** (“Any available barber”): they see the times of all free barbers.
- Call the phone number on the location's card (“Need help? Call …”).

## Frequently asked questions

**The barber is free in the calendar and doesn't show up in booking.**
Check that the service the client chose is one that barber does, and that their **Visibility for booking** is turned on.

**Can I open more times just for online booking?**
The times come from the barber's work schedule; there's no separate schedule for the internet in the screens we reviewed.
