# The client portal's direct link

**In short:** if you don't want to touch your website, share the **Portal link**. You copy it in **Settings › Client portal** with **Copy**. Before sending it to your clients, open it yourself to check that it loads.

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Client portal**, “Direct link” block: “If you'd rather not touch your site, share this link: the portal is hosted by Barberlytics and comes with your account already set up.”

![The Direct link block with the Portal link and the Copy button](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/portal-del-cliente-enlace-directo/enlace.png)

## Share it

1. Under **Portal link**, tap **Copy**.
2. Open it on your phone to check that it loads.
3. Paste it where your clients will find it: the confirmation message you send on WhatsApp or your Instagram bio, next to the booking link.

The link looks like this, where TU-ENLACE is the part that belongs to your barbershop:

```text
https://fd.barberlytics.com/portal/TU-ENLACE
```

## Example

Mi Barbería sends this message after every booking: “All set! If you need to move or cancel your appointment, do it here:” and pastes the **Portal link**.

> [!WARNING]
> On September 30, 2026, when the **Portal link** was opened, the page showed “404 · Page not found.” Until it's fixed, use the portal inside your website ([Put the portal on your website](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/portal-del-cliente-en-tu-sitio)) or contact support.

## Errors you may see

| What the screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| “404 · Page not found · The page you're looking for doesn't exist or has been moved.” | The portal link isn't loading. | Don't share it yet. Use the snippet on your site and let support know. |

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it the same link as the booking one?**
No. The booking link ends in `/book/…` and is for booking. The portal link ends in `/portal/…` and is for viewing, moving or canceling appointments. See [The direct booking link](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/enlace-directo-y-utm).
