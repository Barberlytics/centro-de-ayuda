# Client portal: what your client can do

**In short:** the portal is the screen where your client signs in with their phone number and a code, with no password, and sees their appointments. On the upcoming ones they can reschedule, using their barber's real availability, or cancel. Always within the times you set in **Online booking**.

## Where it's explained

**Settings › Account settings › Client portal**, “What your client can do” block.

![The What your client can do block on the Client portal screen](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/portal-del-cliente-que-es/que-puede-hacer.png)

## What your client does

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sign in | “They sign in with their phone number and a verification code, without creating a password.” |
| View | “They see their upcoming, past and canceled appointments.” |
| Reschedule | On an upcoming appointment, they pick another time “with their barber's real availability.” |
| Cancel | On an upcoming appointment, they cancel it. |

The screen warns: “Rescheduling and canceling respect the minimum times you've set in Online booking.”

## Example

Laura has a haircut with Carlos on Thursday at 6:00 p.m. On Wednesday a trip comes up. She opens the portal from the “My appointments” link on your website, enters her phone number and the code, and moves the appointment to Saturday at 11:00 a.m., a time when Carlos is free. She didn't have to call you. If your limit is “Up to 3 hours in advance,” on Thursday at 4:00 p.m. she could no longer do it online.

## Frequently asked questions

**How does my client get to the portal?**
Through your website or through the direct link. See [Put the portal on your website](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/portal-del-cliente-en-tu-sitio) and [The portal's direct link](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/portal-del-cliente-enlace-directo).

**How do I stop last-minute cancellations?**
With **Online cancellation and rescheduling**. See [How late a client can cancel or reschedule online](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/cancelar-o-reprogramar-en-linea).

**Do I find out when a client cancels or reschedules?**
It hasn't been checked how the app alerts you. The appointment changes in the calendar.
