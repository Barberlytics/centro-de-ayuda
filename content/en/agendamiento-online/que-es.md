# Online booking: what it is and how it works

**In short:** online booking is how your clients book on their own, without calling or texting. You turn it on in **Settings › Online booking**: you can put booking inside your website or share a direct link on WhatsApp and social media. Every appointment that comes in this way lands in your calendar and counts in **Metrics** as an **Online appointment**.

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Online booking**. The card says: “Adjust how clients can book and manage their appointments online.” The screen is called “Online booking” and explains: “Adjust how clients can book and manage their appointments online. These are general settings, but they can be set up individually for each location.”

![The Online booking screen with the three ways to embed booking and the direct link](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/que-es/pantalla.png)

## The two ways to receive bookings

**On your website.** The “Add bookings to your website” block gives you a code snippet to paste into your page. There are three ways, and all three load the same script; the only thing that changes is how booking opens:

| Way | What the client sees | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Side panel** | A floating “Book now” button at the bottom right; booking opens in a panel over your page. | You have a site that already exists. See [Bookings on your website: floating “Book now” button](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/panel-lateral). |
| **Open from link** | No floating button: booking opens from a button or link you already have, like the “Book appointment” in your menu. | You already have your own button. See [Bookings on your website: open from your own button](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/abrir-desde-un-enlace). |
| **Embedded booking** | Booking embedded inside the page, as one more section. | You have a page dedicated to booking. See [Bookings on your website: embed in a page](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/embebido). |

The screen warns: “For the next option, if you don't have technical experience, you may need a developer's help to integrate it correctly.”

**With a link.** The “Online booking link for your website” block gives you the **Direct booking link**, to share on WhatsApp, Instagram or wherever you like. You don't need a website. See [The direct booking link and UTM parameters](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/enlace-directo-y-utm).

## What controls what the client can book

- **Private / Public services and products.** Each service, category, combo and product has a **Private / Public** switch. Only what's **Public** shows up when the client books. See [Public or private service](/ayuda/servicios/servicio-publico-o-privado) and [Public or private product](/ayuda/productos/producto-publico-o-privado).
- **Closure periods.** The app warns: “Online bookings can't be made while your business is closed.” See [Business closure periods](/ayuda/configuracion/periodos-de-cierre).
- **Blocked clients.** A blocked client loses online booking. See [Block a client](/ayuda/clientes/bloquear-a-un-cliente).

## How those appointments look

An appointment the client made goes into the calendar like any other. In **Metrics › Business**, the **Booking method** donut counts it as an **Online appointment**, separate from **Internal** (your team booked it) and **Walk-in**. See [Booking method and appointments](/ayuda/metricas/metodo-de-agendamiento-y-citas).

> [!NOTE]
> What the client sees when booking (the steps, how they choose a barber and time, what confirmation they receive) hasn't been checked yet with a real booking. Neither has where this setting is adjusted for a single location.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a website to receive online bookings?**
No. Share the **Direct booking link**. A website is only needed for the three ways under “Add bookings to your website.”

**A service doesn't show up when the client books.**
Check that the service and its category are **Public**. If they're **Private**, only your team sees them from the calendar.

**Can I close online booking for a few days?**
Yes, with a closure period. While it lasts, the app doesn't allow online bookings.
