# What decides what your client sees when booking

**In short:** the booking page shows what you set up in the app. These are the settings that change it and where each one is.

| What changes in booking | Where you set it | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Which services show up | Each service, **Private / Public** switch (“This option makes your service visible in online booking”) | [Public or private service](/ayuda/servicios/servicio-publico-o-privado) |
| Which services each location offers | **Services › Actions › Activate services** | [Activate services in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-servicios-en-una-sucursal) |
| The order of the categories | **Services › Actions › Booking order** (“Service order for online bookings”) | [The order of services when booking](/ayuda/servicios/orden-de-agendamiento) |
| Which barbers can be chosen | Barber's record › **Visibility for booking** | [Visibility for booking](/ayuda/equipo/visibilidad-para-agendamiento) |
| The price shown next to each barber | **Services › Edit › Team** (own prices) | [Create a service: who does it](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-servicio-equipo) |
| The barber's description, level and gallery | Barber's record › Profile | [A barber's record: profile](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-perfil) |
| The free times | **Team › Working hours**, blocks and appointments | [The team's working hours](/ayuda/equipo/horas-de-trabajo-del-equipo) |
| Who serves them if they choose **No preference** | **Team › Actions › Order for walk-in and no-preference booking** | [Order for clients with no preference](/ayuda/equipo/orden-para-clientes-sin-preferencia) |
| Days without bookings | **Settings › Business closure periods** (“Online bookings can't be made while your business is closed.”) | [Business closure periods](/ayuda/configuracion/periodos-de-cierre) |
| The hours and phone number on the card | Location details and **Business details** | [Edit a location's details](/ayuda/sucursales/editar-una-sucursal) |
| Who can't book | **Clients › View › Actions › Block client** (“If you block the client, they won't be able to make online appointments.”) | [Block a client](/ayuda/clientes/bloquear-a-un-cliente) |

> [!TIP]
> After changing something, open your **Direct booking link** on your phone and go through booking as if you were a client, without tapping **Book now!**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I have a service just for the barbershop and not for the internet?**
Yes: leave it **Private**. You can book it from the calendar, but the client doesn't see it when booking.

**Can I hide a new barber while they're learning?**
Turn off their **Visibility for booking**. What exactly changes when it's turned off hasn't been checked yet.
