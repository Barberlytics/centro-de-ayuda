# Waitlist: how long the client has to accept and to arrive

**In short:** when a time opens up and it's offered to a client on the waitlist, they have a set time to accept it (**Minutes to accept**) and, once accepted, a set time to get to the barbershop (**Minutes to arrive**). Both are chosen in **Settings › Online booking**.

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Online booking**, “Waitlist” block: “Set the time limit for a user to accept an appointment when they're on the waitlist. Set the time limit for a user to arrive at the barbershop once they've accepted the waitlist appointment.”

![The Waitlist block with Minutes to accept and Minutes to arrive](/assets/es/agendamiento-online/tiempos-de-la-lista-de-espera/bloque.png)

## The two times

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **Minutes to accept\*** | **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **45 minutes**, **60 minutes** |
| **Minutes to arrive\*** | **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **45 minutes**, **60 minutes** |

Choose and tap **Save** at the bottom of the page.

## Example

With 15 minutes to accept and 30 to arrive: at 3:00 p.m. an appointment is canceled and Carlos, who was on the waitlist, is offered the gap. He has until 3:15 p.m. to accept it. If he accepts at 3:10 p.m., he has until 3:40 p.m. to arrive.

> [!NOTE]
> How the client receives the alert and what happens if they don't accept or don't arrive in time hasn't been checked yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I add someone to the waitlist?**
From the calendar. See [The waitlist: put a client down when there's no opening](/ayuda/calendario/lista-de-espera).

**What time is best for me?**
If your barbershop is in an area where people arrive on foot, 15 minutes to arrive is usually enough. If your clients come by car from far away, give them more.
