# Open an appointment and see its details

**In short:** tap an appointment's card and its details open on the right, with four tabs: **Summary**, **Notes**, **Client** and **History**. At the top is the **Actions** button with the appointment's statuses.

## The header

The client's name, their phone number, their type (**VIP**…), the current status (**Pending**), the **Actions** button (≡) and **×** to close.

**Actions** offers: **Barber ready**, **Check in**, **Seated**, **Charge**, **No-show** and **Cancel**. [The statuses of an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/estados-de-una-cita) explains them.

![The Actions menu in an appointment's details](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/acciones.png)

## Summary

![The Summary tab in an appointment's details](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/resumen.png)

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **NEXT STEP** | What to do now: “Laura hasn't arrived yet.” with the **Check in · $70,000.00** button and the **Pay appointment** button |
| **CLIENT JOURNEY** | Their type, how many visits they have at this location (“31 visits at this location”, “Visit 31”) and the ladder New → Retained → Frequent → VIP |
| **APPOINTMENT · 25/SEP/2026** | Client, service with its duration, barber, **Date & Time**, **Duration** (with a button to change it), **Price** and the total (“1 services · 30 min”) |
| **LOYALTY** | How it was booked (“Registered automatically”), who manages it (“Managed by Andrés”), whether the appointment is with a different barber, who the client asked for (“Asked for Mateo”), how many times they have gone to that barber (“With Mateo 8 of 31 visits”) and whether they've “Seen other barbers” |

To change the duration, tap the **Duration** value (“Click to change the duration”).

![The button to change the duration](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/cambiar-duracion.png)

## Notes

The client's notes in three tabs: **Notes**, **Medication** and **Allergies** (each with its own counter). With **Add note** you choose the **Type\*** and write the **Description\***, then tap **Create**.

![The Notes tab](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/notas.png)

![The Add note form](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/agregar-nota.png)

## Client

![The Client tab](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/cliente.png)

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **CLIENT RHYTHM** | How often they come (“Every 7 days”, “Over 31 visits”) and the days between visits |
| **BOOK THEM FOR** | The suggested date for their next appointment (“Monday, 28/SEP”) |
| **RELIABILITY** | “Came 31 of 31 times”, “Has never missed. Reliable.” |
| **ALERTS** | For example, “Canceled 8” |
| **ALL VISITS** | Each visit with its date, service, barber and price |

## History

**APPOINTMENT ACTIVITY**: who created it, where and when (“Appointment created · At the barbershop · Mateo · 25/SEP/2026, 10:16 am”). It can't be edited.

![The History tab](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/historial.png)

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I charge from here?**
With **Pay appointment** in **NEXT STEP**, or with **Actions › Charge**. See [Charge an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cobrar-una-cita).

**Can I change the service or the barber from the details?**
There is no edit button for those details here. From the payment screen you can **Add service**.

**What is “Asked for”?**
The barber the client chose when booking. If the appointment was created with no preference, see [Clients with no preferred barber](/ayuda/calendario/clientes-sin-preferencia-de-barbero).
