# Apply a discount to an appointment

**In short:** on the payment screens we've gone through, **Pay appointment** and the **Charge** checkout, there is no discount button. What does exist is the **Discounts** card in **Metrics › Business**, which adds up what you discounted. Until we confirm where a discount is applied, this article explains what is there and what isn't.

## Where we looked

1. Open the appointment and tap **Pay appointment**. The “Summary” panel has **Services**, **Add service**, **Add products**, **Total services and products**, **Tip**, **Tax** and **Order total**. There is no discount line or button.
   ![The Pay appointment panel with its lines: services, tip, tax and order total](/assets/es/calendario/cobrar-una-cita/pagar-cita.png)
2. Tap **Open full checkout**. The “Charge” screen has **Add products**, **Add service**, **Combine appointments** and **Add client** at the top; the **Payment methods** in the middle (**Cash**, **Credit/Debit**, **Payment link**, **Split payment**); and the **Summary** and the **Order total** on the right. There's no discount there either.
   ![The Charge screen with the payment methods and the summary](/assets/es/calendario/cobrar-una-cita/checkout.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Where a discount is applied to an appointment hasn't been confirmed yet. We didn't open **Cash** (it would charge the appointment) or edit a service's price. The discount may be inside a payment method, or it may be done by changing the service's price.

## What you can see

- **Metrics › Business** has the **Discounts** card, with the amount and the percentage for the period, for example **$120,000.00 · 1.41 %**. If there are numbers there, discounts are being applied at your barbershop some way or another. [Payment distribution, refunds and discounts](/ayuda/metricas/distribucion-de-pagos-reembolsos-y-descuentos) explains it.
- In each barber's record, **Services** tab, there is a price for each service plus a **Loyalty price** and a **New client price**. Those are different prices by client type, not one-off discounts.
