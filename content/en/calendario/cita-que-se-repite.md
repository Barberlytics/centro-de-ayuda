# Create a repeating appointment

**In short:** when you create the appointment, change **Repeat** (which starts on **Does not repeat**) to **Weekly**, **Every two weeks**, **Every three weeks**, **Monthly**, **Every two months** or **Every three months**, and tap **Create**. It's for the client who always comes on the same day at the same time.

## Steps

1. In the **Calendar**, tap **Actions** and choose **Create appointment**.
2. Choose **Client**, **Service**, **Barber** and **Date & Time** like any other appointment. The date you enter is the first one in the series. [Create an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/crear-una-cita) explains how.
3. Tap **Repeat**. By default it says **Does not repeat**.
   ![The Repeat field open with its options, from Weekly to Every three months](/assets/es/calendario/crear-una-cita/repeticion.png)
4. Choose how often the client comes back: **Weekly**, **Every two weeks**, **Every three weeks**, **Monthly**, **Every two months** or **Every three months**.
5. Tap **Create**.

Example: Laura gets a haircut with Carlos every other week, on Saturdays at 10:00 AM. You create an appointment for next Saturday at 10:00 AM with **Repeat** set to **Every two weeks**.

> [!NOTE]
> How the repeated appointments look on the calendar after you tap **Create**, how far ahead they are created, and how to change or cancel just one in the series hasn't been confirmed yet.

## What the screen says

| Text | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Does not repeat** | A single appointment. It's the value the panel opens with |
| **Weekly** | The same appointment every week, on the same day and at the same time |
| **Every two weeks** / **Every three weeks** | Every 14 or 21 days |
| **Monthly** / **Every two months** / **Every three months** | Every one, two or three months |

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I repeat an appointment every day?**
No. The shortest option is **Weekly**. To block a barber's schedule every day, for example for lunch, use a block: [Block a barber's calendar](/ayuda/calendario/bloquear-el-calendario) explains how.

**Can I add the repeat to an appointment that already exists?**
**Repeat** is in the **Create appointment** panel. If the appointment already exists and you need the series, create a new one with the repeat.

**What if the client misses one of the appointments?**
Each appointment in the series is handled like any other: you open the appointment and use **Actions**. See [The client didn't show up](/ayuda/calendario/el-cliente-no-se-presento).
