# Get to know the calendar: barbers, hours and colors

**In short:** the **Calendar** (second icon in the menu) shows today with one column per barber and the hours running down. At the top are the **Team\*** filter, the date, the view and the **Actions** button. Each appointment is a card inside its barber's column.

![The calendar in the Day view, with one column per barber](/assets/es/calendario/conoce-el-calendario/pantalla.png)

## The top bar

![The calendar bar: Team, date with arrows, settings, view and Actions](/assets/es/calendario/conoce-el-calendario/barra.png)

From left to right:

| Control | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Team\*** | Choose who you see. By default it's on **Working**: only barbers with hours that day. It also offers **Whole team** and each barber by name |
| **‹** date **›** | The day you're looking at (“Friday, SEP 25”). The arrows go to the previous and next day; the icon opens a calendar so you can jump to another date. When you're not on today, the **Today** button appears |
| Settings button (sliders) | Opens **Working hours** (takes you to Team › Working hours) and **Waitlist** |
| View | **Agenda**, **Day** or **Month**. [See the day, the agenda or the month](/ayuda/calendario/ver-el-dia-la-agenda-o-el-mes) explains them |
| **Actions** | **Create appointment**, **Create quick sale**, **Walk-in sale** and **Payment list** |

![The Actions menu open](/assets/es/calendario/las-acciones-del-calendario/menu-acciones.png)

## The columns

![The barbers' columns, with their photo or initials](/assets/es/calendario/conoce-el-calendario/columnas.png)

- Each barber has their own column, with their photo (or an icon if they don't have one) and their name.
- Tapping the name opens a menu with **View barber** and **Edit barber profile**.
  ![A barber's menu opened from their column](/assets/es/calendario/conoce-el-calendario/menu-barbero.png)
- The hours run from **06:00 AM** to **11:30 PM**, in half-hour steps.
- **Shaded** stretches are hours when that barber doesn't work. The light ones are their schedule.
- A **red line** with the time (“10:12 am”) marks the current moment.

## An appointment's card

![An appointment card: client, type, service and price, time, barber and the Check in button](/assets/es/calendario/conoce-el-calendario/tarjeta-cita.png)

Each appointment looks like a card with:

- The **client's name** and, next to it, their type (**VIP**, New, Retained…).
- The **service and its price** (“Classic haircut $70,000.00”).
- The **time** (“10:30 AM to 11:00 AM”) and the **barber**.
- A warning icon when the appointment has something to check.
- The **Check in** status button, to mark that the client has arrived ([Mark that the client has arrived](/ayuda/calendario/marcar-que-el-cliente-llego)).
- On the bottom edge, a handle to **change the duration** by dragging.

Tapping the card opens the appointment's details: [Open an appointment and see its details](/ayuda/calendario/abrir-una-cita).

## Tapping an empty slot

If you tap a free slot for a barber, **Create appointment** opens with that **Barber** and that **Date & Time** already filled in. It's the fastest way to book.

![Create appointment opened from an empty slot, with barber and time already chosen](/assets/es/calendario/conoce-el-calendario/franja-vacia.png)

## When nobody is working

If no barber has hours that day, instead of the grid you see “Nobody works this day” with two buttons: **View whole team** (shows everyone, in case there are appointments) and **Set up schedules** (takes you to Team › Working hours).

## If you sign in with a barber profile

You only see your own column. The **Actions** are the same.

## Frequently asked questions

**What do the colors on the cards mean?**
Each card's border changes depending on the barber and the appointment's status. Which color goes with which status isn't documented yet.

**Why can't I see a barber?**
Either **Team\*** is on **Working** and that barber has no hours today, or they aren't activated at this location. See [A barber doesn't show up on the calendar](/ayuda/calendario/un-barbero-no-aparece-en-el-calendario).

**Can I move an appointment by dragging it?**
Cards can be dragged. [Move or reschedule an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/mover-o-reprogramar-una-cita) explains how.
