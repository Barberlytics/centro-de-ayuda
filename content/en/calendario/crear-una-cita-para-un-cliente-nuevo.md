# Create an appointment for a new client

**In short:** in the **Create appointment** panel, tap **Client**, search first by name or phone so you don't create a duplicate and, if they aren't there, tap **Create client**. If the client doesn't want to leave their details, choose **Walk in**, the first option in the list.

## Steps

1. In the **Calendar**, tap **Actions** and choose **Create appointment**. If you tap an empty slot for a barber, the panel opens with the **Barber** and the **Date & Time** already filled in.
   ![The Create appointment panel with the Client, Service, Barber and Date & Time fields](/assets/es/calendario/crear-una-cita/panel.png)
2. Tap **Client**. The “Clients” list opens.
3. Type the name or phone number in “Search N Clients”. Check that they aren't already there: the list shows the name, the phone number with asterisks and the type (**New**, **Retained**, **VIP**…).
   ![The client list with the search box, the Create client button and the Walk in option](/assets/es/calendario/crear-una-cita/cliente.png)
4. If they don't show up, tap **Create client**.
5. Fill in their details and save.
6. Continue with **Service**, **Barber** and **Date & Time**, and tap **Create**. The details of each field are in [Create an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/crear-una-cita).

> [!NOTE]
> What the **Create client** form asks for when it's opened from the calendar, and how the appointment looks after you tap **Create**, hasn't been confirmed yet.

## If the client doesn't want to register

In the “Clients” list, the first option is **Walk in**, with the phone number “000******0000” and the type OTHER. It's for serving someone without creating their profile. It's a generic client: the appointment isn't under a person's name and in **Transactions** the payment shows up as “Walk in”.

If the client showed up without booking, also turn on **Walk-in client**. [A client who walks in without an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cliente-sin-cita-previa) explains it.

> [!TIP]
> Ask for their phone number even if it's their first time. With the record created, next time you find them in a second and the app keeps count of their visits.

## What the screen says

| Text | What it means |
|---|---|
| “Search 44 Clients” | The list's search box; the number is how many clients you have |
| **Create client** | Opens the form to register someone new without leaving the appointment |
| **Walk in** · “000******0000” | A generic client, with no record of their own |
| **New** | The type given to a client with few visits |

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to go to Clients before booking?**
No. From **Client › Create client**, inside the appointment, you register them without leaving the calendar.

**I already created them and they don't show up in the list.**
Type part of the name or phone number in “Search N Clients”. If they still don't show up, check in **Clients** whether they were saved.

**What's the difference between Walk in and Walk-in client?**
**Walk in** is a client with no record. **Walk-in client** is a switch that marks that the client showed up without booking, whether or not they have a record. They can be used together.
