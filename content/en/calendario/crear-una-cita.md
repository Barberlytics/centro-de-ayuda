# Create an appointment

**In short:** tap **Actions › Create appointment** (or an empty slot on the calendar), choose **Client**, **Service**, **Barber** and **Date & Time**, and tap **Create**.

## Steps

1. In the **Calendar**, tap **Actions** and choose **Create appointment**. If you tap an empty slot for a barber, the panel opens with the barber and the time already filled in.
   ![The Create appointment panel with its four fields, the walk-in switch and Repeat](/assets/es/calendario/crear-una-cita/panel.png)
2. Tap **Client**. Search in the “Search N Clients” box and tap the client. If they're new, tap **Create client** ([Create an appointment for a new client](/ayuda/calendario/crear-una-cita-para-un-cliente-nuevo)). The first option is **Walk in**, for an unregistered client.
   ![The client list with the search box and the Create client button](/assets/es/calendario/crear-una-cita/cliente.png)
3. Tap **Service**. At the top are the categories (**All services** and each category); each service shows its duration and price. Tap one.
   ![The list of services by category, with duration and price](/assets/es/calendario/crear-una-cita/servicio.png)
4. Tap **Barber**. You can filter by level (**All barbers**, Senior, Master…). Tap the barber.
   ![The list of barbers with the level filter](/assets/es/calendario/crear-una-cita/barbero.png)
5. Tap **Date & Time**. Choose the day in the strip at the top and the time under **Morning**, **Afternoon** or **Evening** (in half-hour steps).
   ![The date and time picker, with the days of the month and the times by period](/assets/es/calendario/crear-una-cita/fecha-y-hora.png)
6. If the client is already at the barbershop and hadn't booked, turn on **Walk-in client** ([A client who walks in without an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cliente-sin-cita-previa)).
7. If the appointment repeats, change **Repeat** ([Create a repeating appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cita-que-se-repite)).
8. Tap **Create**.

## What the screen says

| Text | What it means |
|---|---|
| “Search 44 Clients” | The client search box; the number is how many you have |
| “$70,000.00 & RISING” | The service's price; it can go up depending on the barber or the client |
| “The client walked in without an appointment. Applies the barber's walk-in commission and shows in the booking reports.” | What the **Walk-in client** switch does |

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I book two services in one appointment?**
The panel lets you choose one **Service**. After you create it, you can **Add service** from the payment screen.

**What happens if I choose a time when the barber doesn't work?**
See [I can't book at this time](/ayuda/calendario/no-puedo-agendar-a-esta-hora).

**Does the client get a notice?**
Notices are set up in **Settings › Notification center**. What the client receives when the appointment is created isn't documented yet.
