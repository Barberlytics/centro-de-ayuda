# Create a quick product sale

**In short:** to sell a product to someone who has no appointment, tap **Actions › Create quick sale**, choose the products and tap **Pay order**.

## Steps

1. In the **Calendar**, tap **Actions** and choose **Create quick sale**. **New sale** opens with the phrase “Choose your product”.
   ![The New sale panel with the categories, the products and the total](/assets/es/calendario/venta-rapida/panel.png)
2. At the top are the product categories. Tap one to see its products, each with its price.
3. Tap each product the client is taking. The **Total** at the bottom keeps adding up.
4. Tap **Pay order**.

> [!NOTE]
> What comes after **Pay order** (payment methods, client, receipt) hasn't been confirmed yet. We'll fill it in with a test sale.

## Which products show up

The products in your catalog that are activated at this location and have **Availability for sale** turned on. If one is missing, see [I can't see a service or product in my location](/ayuda/sucursales/no-veo-un-servicio-o-producto-en-mi-sucursal).

## Frequently asked questions

**What if the client does have an appointment?**
Add the product to the appointment's payment with **Add products** ([Charge an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cobrar-una-cita)). That way everything ends up on one bill.

**Does a quick sale add to Metrics?**
Yes: in **Sales › Products**, **Revenue › Products** and **Product sales**.

**Does the barber get a commission?**
It depends on each barber's **Product** compensation, in their record (**Team › View › Compensation**).
