# The client didn't get the reminder

**In short:** there is no setting called “client reminder” on Barberlytics screens. What does exist: the online booking page promises “news about your appointment” to their phone, the barber's record has **Clients can cancel appointments** with the **Email / SMS / Push** channels, and the **Notification center** sets up the notices for the team.

## What to check

1. **The client's phone number and email** in **Clients › View › Profile**: if they're wrong, no notice gets through.
2. **How the appointment was created**: in the details, **History** says whether it was “At the barbershop” or an **Online appointment**. The online booking page uses the phone number “to send you news about your appointment”.
3. **The barber's options**: in **Team › View › Options**, **Clients can cancel appointments** and its channels **Email**, **SMS**, **Push**, with **Hours** and **Minutes**.
4. **The Notification center** (**Settings › Notifications**): that's where the notices to the team by **Email** and **Push** are, grouped under Clients, Products, Calendar, Metrics and Reports.

> [!NOTE]
> Exactly which messages the client receives (confirmation, reminder before the appointment, changes) and through which channel isn't documented yet: it doesn't appear on the screens we checked and has to be confirmed with the Barberlytics team.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I turn on a reminder 24 hours before?**
There's no such setting on the screens we checked. It's still to be confirmed.

**Does the client get anything when I book the appointment for them from the calendar?**
Not documented yet.
