# Filter by team: who shows up on the calendar

**In short:** the first button in the **Calendar** bar is **Team\***. By default it's on **Working**: you see the columns of the barbers who have hours that day. Change it to **Whole team** to see every active barber at the location, or choose a barber to see just their column.

## Steps

1. Open **Calendar** in the menu.
2. Tap **Team\***, the first button in the top bar. Three kinds of option open up: **Whole team**, **Working** and each active barber at the location, by name.
   ![The Team filter open, with Whole team, Working and the list of barbers](/assets/es/calendario/ver-el-dia-la-agenda-o-el-mes/equipo-filtro.png)
3. Choose what you want to see:
   - **Working**: only barbers with hours that day. It's the option the calendar opens with.
   - **Whole team**: every active barber at the location, whether or not they have hours that day.
   - A name, for example **Carlos**: just their column.
4. The grid changes right away. There's no apply button.

## If the calendar says “Nobody works this day”

With **Working**, on a day when no barber has hours, the calendar shows no columns. Instead it says “Nobody works this day” and explains: “No barber has hours on Friday, September 25, 2026. If there are booked appointments, you can see them under the whole team.” You have two buttons:

- **View whole team**: changes the filter to **Whole team** and shows the columns, with their appointments if there are any.
- **Set up schedules**: takes you to the team's working hours, so you can give someone hours that day.

## The filter in each view

- In the **Day** view the filter is **Team\***, in the top bar.
- In the **Agenda** view there's a selector of its own, **Whole team**. If there's only one option, it's disabled.
- In the **Month** view you see one barber at a time: you choose them in the selector at the top.

How to switch views is explained in [See the day, the agenda or the month](/ayuda/calendario/ver-el-dia-la-agenda-o-el-mes).

> [!NOTE]
> If you sign in with a barber profile, the calendar shows only your own column. There's no need to filter.

## What the screen says

| Text | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Working** | The barbers who have hours that day |
| **Whole team** | Every active barber at the location you've chosen |
| “Nobody works this day” | With **Working**, no barber has hours on the date you're looking at |
| “If there are booked appointments, you can see them under the whole team.” | That day's appointments are still there; switch to **Whole team** to see them |

## Frequently asked questions

**I can't see a barber's column. Was it deleted?**
Almost never. With **Working**, only those who have hours that day show up. Switch to **Whole team**. If they still don't show up, see [A barber doesn't show up on the calendar](/ayuda/calendario/un-barbero-no-aparece-en-el-calendario).

**Can I see two barbers and not the rest?**
No. The filter shows everyone, those who are working, or just one.

**Which option does the calendar open with?**
**Working**. If you want to see everyone, switch to **Whole team**.
