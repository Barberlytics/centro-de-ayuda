# The payment list: to collect and paid

**In short:** **Actions › Payment list** opens the **Ready to pay** panel with two tabs: **To collect** (the day's appointments still waiting to be paid) and **Paid** (the ones already charged).

## How to open it

1. In the **Calendar**, tap **Actions**.
2. Choose **Payment list**.
   ![The Ready to pay panel on the To collect tab](/assets/es/calendario/lista-de-cobros/por-cobrar.png)

## What it shows

- At the top, the day (“25 - September”) with an arrow to change it.
- **To collect**: “Here you'll find all your appointments pending payment”.
- **Paid**: “Here you'll find all your paid appointments”.
  ![The Paid tab](/assets/es/calendario/lista-de-cobros/pagado.png)

When there are no appointments in a tab, the panel shows only that phrase.

## What it's for

At the end of the day, check **To collect**: if anything is left, an appointment was served and nobody charged it. The **Appointments › Pending payment** card in Metrics says the same thing.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I charge one from the list?**
Open it from the list or from the calendar and follow [Charge an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cobrar-una-cita).

**Can I see another day?**
Yes, with the day arrow at the top of the panel.

**Is it the same as Transactions?**
No. This list is for the day and the calendar; **Transactions** keeps every bill with filters by date and status.
