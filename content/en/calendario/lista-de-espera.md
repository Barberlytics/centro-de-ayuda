# The waitlist: put a client down when there's no opening

**In short:** if a client wants a time that's already taken, add them to the **Waitlist**. It's in the calendar's settings button (the one with the sliders), next to **Working hours**.

## Open the list

1. In the **Calendar** bar, tap the settings button (sliders icon, to the left of the view).
   ![The settings menu with Working hours and Waitlist](/assets/es/calendario/las-acciones-del-calendario/menu-ajustes.png)
2. Choose **Waitlist**. The panel says: “Here you can see all the appointments that come in through the waitlist in scheduled order.”
   ![The Waitlist panel](/assets/es/calendario/lista-de-espera/panel.png)

## Add a client

1. Tap **Add to waitlist**.
   ![The Add to waitlist form](/assets/es/calendario/lista-de-espera/anadir.png)
2. Choose **Client**, **Service** and **Barber**.
3. Under **Date & Time 1**, tap **Select date** and **Select time** for the slot that works for the client.
4. If several slots work for them, tap **Add waitlist** to add another.
5. Tap **Confirm**.

## Serve someone on the list

Each client shows up on a card with the service, the price and the **New** label. Tap **Check in**: their appointment is created for the current time in the barber's column and it's set to **Checked in**, ready to **Seat**. Clients who sign themselves up at the entrance kiosk also end up here: see [Serve the kiosk waitlist](/ayuda/front-desk/atender-la-lista-de-espera).

> [!NOTE]
> Whether the app notifies you by itself when an opening frees up isn't documented yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it the same as the queue for no-preference clients?**
No. The waitlist is for clients waiting for an opening; the no-preference client order decides which barber serves someone who didn't choose a barber ([Clients with no preferred barber](/ayuda/calendario/clientes-sin-preferencia-de-barbero)).

**Can I see the list for other days?**
The panel shows the chosen day at the top (“25 - September”) and it can be changed.
