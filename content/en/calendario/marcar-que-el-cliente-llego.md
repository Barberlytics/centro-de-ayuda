# Mark that the client has arrived (check in)

**In short:** when the client walks in, tap **Check in**. It's in three places: on the appointment's card in the calendar, in **NEXT STEP** when you open the appointment, and in the appointment's **Actions** menu.

## From the card

1. In the **Day** view, find the appointment's card in the barber's column. It shows the client's name, the service with its price, the time (“10:30 AM to 11:00 AM”) and the barber.
2. Tap **Check in**, the button on the card.
   ![An appointment's card on the calendar, with the Check in button](/assets/es/calendario/conoce-el-calendario/tarjeta-cita.png)

## From the appointment's details

1. Tap the card to open the appointment. On the **Summary** tab, at the top, is **NEXT STEP**: it says “Laura hasn't arrived yet.” and has the **Check in · $70,000.00** button (the price is the appointment's).
   ![The appointment's details with Next step and the Check in button](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/resumen.png)
2. Tap **Check in**.

You can also tap **Actions** (the ≡ button in the header) and choose **Check in**. In that menu, in order, are: **Barber ready**, **Check in**, **Seated**, **Charge**, **No-show** and **Cancel**.

![The appointment's Actions menu open](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/acciones.png)

After **Check in**, the appointment is set to **Checked in**: **NEXT STEP** says “Laura is waiting.” and the button changes to **Seat** with the appointment's price. On the calendar card the **Seat** button appears.

> [!TIP]
> If you have a tablet at the entrance, the client can do this on their own with [Front Desk](/ayuda/front-desk/que-es).

> [!NOTE]

## What the app calls each status

The app uses different names in different places. The **Actions** menu says **Barber ready**, **Check in**, **Seated**, **Charge**, **No-show** and **Cancel**. The card shows the **Check in** button. The **Agenda** view and the appointment's header say **Pending** for an appointment that hasn't started. The **Appointment status** filter in **Transactions** uses **Scheduled**, **Arrived**, **In chair**, **Closed**, **Completed** and **Canceled**. [The statuses of an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/estados-de-una-cita) explains them.

## What the screen says

| Text | What it means |
|---|---|
| “Laura hasn't arrived yet.” | Nobody has marked this client's arrival |
| **Check in · $70,000.00** | Marks the arrival; the amount is the appointment's price |
| **Pending** | The appointment hasn't started yet |
| **Pay appointment** | Charge without going through the arrival; [Charge an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cobrar-una-cita) explains it |

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the point of marking the arrival?**
So the barber knows their client is here, and so the appointment records that the client did come.

**The client arrived and the barber is still busy.**
Mark **Check in** anyway. When the barber frees up, you move the client to the chair: [Move the client to the chair](/ayuda/calendario/pasar-al-cliente-a-la-silla) explains how.

**I made a mistake and marked another client's arrival.**
How to undo it hasn't been confirmed yet. Open the appointment and check **Actions**.
