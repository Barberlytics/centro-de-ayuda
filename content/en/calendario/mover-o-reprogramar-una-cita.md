# Move or reschedule an appointment

**In short:** in the **Day** view, you can drag the appointment's card: drop it at another time or in another barber's column. To make it last longer or shorter, drag the **Change duration** handle on the bottom edge, or open the appointment and tap **Duration** in **Summary**.

## Change the time or the barber by dragging

1. Go to the **Day** view of the **Calendar**.
2. Find the appointment's card in the barber's column. It shows the client, “Classic haircut $70,000.00”, “10:30 AM to 11:00 AM” and the barber.
   ![An appointment's card on the grid, with the Change duration handle at the bottom](/assets/es/calendario/conoce-el-calendario/tarjeta-cita.png)
3. Press and hold the card and drag it to the new time, in the same column or in another barber's.
4. Drop it.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens after you drop the card (whether it asks you to confirm, whether it notifies the client, whether it respects the shaded stretches) hasn't been confirmed yet. The grid runs in half-hour steps.

## Lengthen or shorten the appointment

There are two ways:

- **On the grid.** Below the card there's a handle that says **Change duration**. Drag it down to lengthen the appointment and up to shorten it.
- **In the details.** Tap the card. In **Summary**, inside **APPOINTMENT**, is **Duration** (“30 min”) with the text “Click to change the duration”. Tap it and choose the new duration.
  ![The appointment's details with the Duration field and the text Click to change the duration](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/cambiar-duracion.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Which options appear when you tap **Duration** and how the change is saved hasn't been confirmed yet.

## Change the day

To move the appointment to another date, in the appointment's details the **APPOINTMENT** block shows **Date & Time** (“10:30 am - 11:00 am”). Whether that field can be tapped to change the day hasn't been confirmed yet. In the meantime, the safe way is to create the appointment on the new day and cancel the old one: see [Create an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/crear-una-cita) and [Cancel an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cancelar-una-cita).

## What the screen says

| Text | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Change duration** | The handle at the bottom of the card; dragging it changes how long it lasts |
| “Click to change the duration” | In the details, the **Duration** field can be tapped |
| “1 services · 30 min” | How many services the appointment has and how long they last in total |

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I move the appointment to a barber who doesn't work at that time?**
Stretches outside the barber's hours are shaded. Whether the app lets you drop the appointment there hasn't been confirmed yet. See [I can't book at this time](/ayuda/calendario/no-puedo-agendar-a-esta-hora).
