# Move between dates

**In short:** in the **Calendar** bar, the date (“Friday, SEP 25”) has an arrow on each side and a calendar icon. The arrows change the day; the icon opens a calendar so you can jump to any date; **Today** takes you back to the current day.

## Go to the previous or next day

Tap **‹** (Previous day) or **›** (Next day).

![The date with its two arrows](/assets/es/calendario/moverte-entre-fechas/flechas.png)

## Jump to a date

1. Tap the calendar icon, to the right of the date.
2. Choose the day on the month calendar. Use the calendar's arrows to change months.
   ![The calendar for choosing a date](/assets/es/calendario/moverte-entre-fechas/selector-de-fecha.png)

## Get back to today

When you're on another day, the **Today** button appears in the bar. Tap it.

![The Today button in the bar, when on another day](/assets/es/calendario/moverte-entre-fechas/hoy.png)

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I know which day I'm looking at?**
The date in the bar tells you: “Friday, SEP 25”. If you don't see the **Today** button, you're on today.

**Can I type the date?**
No: the field can't be edited by hand. Use the calendar icon.

**Do the arrows change the week or the month in the other views?**
In our tests, the arrows are called “Previous day” and “Next day”. How they behave in **Month** hasn't been confirmed yet.
