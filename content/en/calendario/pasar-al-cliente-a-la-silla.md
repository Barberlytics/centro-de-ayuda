# Move the client to the chair

**In short:** open the appointment, tap **Actions** and choose **Seated**. It's the step that comes after **Check in** (the client arrived) and before **Charge**.

## Steps

1. In the **Calendar**, tap the appointment's card. The details open with the client's name, their type (**VIP**…), the status and the tabs **Summary · Notes · Client · History**.
2. Tap **Actions**, the ≡ button in the header.
   ![The appointment's Actions menu, with Barber ready, Check in, Seated, Charge, No-show and Cancel](/assets/es/calendario/abrir-una-cita/acciones.png)
3. Choose **Seated**.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens after you tap **Seated** (how the card and the appointment's status change, whether you can go back) hasn't been confirmed yet. Neither has what **Barber ready**, the first option in the menu, does.

## The order of the actions

The **Actions** menu shows them in this order: **Barber ready**, **Check in**, **Seated**, **Charge**, **No-show**, **Cancel**. That's an appointment's normal path: the client arrives, sits down, gets charged. **No-show** and **Cancel** are the exits when the appointment doesn't happen.

## What the app calls each status

The app uses different names in different places. In the **Actions** menu the option is **Seated**. In the **Appointment status** filter in **Transactions** it shows up as **In chair**, between **Arrived** and **Closed**. [The statuses of an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/estados-de-una-cita) explains them.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to mark Check in before Seated?**
The menu offers them in that order. Whether the app lets you skip **Check in** hasn't been confirmed yet.

**Who marks it, the barber or the front desk?**
Anyone who opens the appointment. The barber sees their column and can do it from their phone.

**Does Seated charge the appointment?**
No. Charging is a different action: **Charge** in the same menu, or **Pay appointment** in **Summary**. [Charge an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cobrar-una-cita) explains it.
