# A barber doesn't show up on the calendar

**In short:** it's almost always that the barber isn't activated at that location, or the calendar's **Team\*** filter isn't showing them. Check both.

## What to check

1. **That you're at the right location.** The calendar shows the location you chose at the top. See [Switch locations](/ayuda/navegacion/cambiar-de-sucursal).
2. **The Team\* filter.** Open it in the **Calendar** bar and check that the barber is among the ones shown.
3. **That the barber is activated at this location.**
   1. Go to **Team**.
   2. Tap **Actions**.
   3. Tap **Activate team in this location**.
   4. Find the barber on the **Barbers** tab (inside it has **Commission** and **Rent**) and turn on their switch.
   5. Tap **Save**.

The screen says: “Select the team you want to import by turning on the team switch. Click ‘Save’ once you've made your choices”.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The team is one for the whole barbershop, but each person is activated at each location where they work. The **Team** list shows only the people who are active at the location you chose.

## If the calendar says “Nobody works this day”

At a location where no barber has hours that day, the calendar shows “Nobody works this day” and explains: “No barber has hours on Friday, September 25, 2026. If there are booked appointments, you can see them under the whole team.” Below there are two buttons: **View whole team** and **Set up schedules**. Each barber's hours are changed in **Team › Working hours**.

## Frequently asked questions

**A barber works at two locations. Do I have to create them twice?**
No. They're one person, activated at both locations.

**The barber shows up in Team at another location, but not at this one.**
Activate them here with **Actions › Activate team in this location**.

**I already activated them and they still don't show up.**
Check the calendar's **Team\*** filter.
