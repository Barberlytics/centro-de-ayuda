# Walk-in sale

**In short:** **Actions › Walk-in sale** records a service for a client who showed up and was served without booking. It asks for **Date**, time, **Client**, **Service** and **Barber**, and it's created with **Create**.

## When to use it

- The client walked in without an appointment and has already been served or is in the chair.
- You want to record a service from today (or from a past date) with its barber, so it counts toward sales and commissions.

If you'd rather book it as an appointment and mark it as a walk-in, see [A client who walks in without an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cliente-sin-cita-previa).

## Steps

1. In the **Calendar**, tap **Actions** and choose **Walk-in sale**.
   ![The Walk-in sale panel: date, time, client, service, barber and Create](/assets/es/calendario/venta-sin-cita/panel.png)
2. Check the **Date** (it comes set to today; the icon opens a calendar) and the time.
3. Tap **Client** and choose them, or use **Walk in** if they aren't registered.
4. Tap **Service** and choose it.
5. Tap **Barber** and choose who served them.
6. Tap **Create**.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens after **Create** (whether it opens the payment or creates an already-served appointment) hasn't been confirmed yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**How is it different from Create quick sale?**
**Walk-in sale** is for a **service**; **Create quick sale** is for **products**.

**Does it count as a walk-in in Metrics?**
The **Booking method** card separates **Walk-in**. Whether this screen marks it that way hasn't been confirmed yet.
