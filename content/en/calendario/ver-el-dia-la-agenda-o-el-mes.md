# See the day, the agenda or the month

**In short:** the view selector in the bar offers **Agenda**, **Day** and **Month**. **Day** is the grid by barber; **Agenda** is the list of the day's appointments; **Month** shows one barber's occupancy day by day.

## How to switch views

1. Open **Calendar**.
2. Tap the selector that says **Day** (to the left of **Actions**).
3. Choose **Agenda**, **Day** or **Month**.
   ![The view selector open with Agenda, Day and Month](/assets/es/calendario/ver-el-dia-la-agenda-o-el-mes/selector.png)

## Day: the grid

One column per barber and the hours running down. It's the view for working: booking, seeing who has arrived, charging. [Get to know the calendar](/ayuda/calendario/conoce-el-calendario) describes it.

![The Day view](/assets/es/calendario/ver-el-dia-la-agenda-o-el-mes/dia.png)

## Agenda: the day's list

The day's appointments in a list, one under another: client, type (**VIP**…), service and status (**Pending**). At the top, the month and the day (“September · Friday 25”) and a team selector. At the end it says “No more appointments to show”.

![The Agenda view with the list of appointments](/assets/es/calendario/ver-el-dia-la-agenda-o-el-mes/agenda.png)

It's good for going over the day at a glance, with no columns.

## Month: one barber's occupancy

At the top you choose **one barber**. Each day of the month shows their occupancy: “Occ. 0.79 %”. Days with more appointments have a higher percentage.

![The Month view with the occupancy per day](/assets/es/calendario/ver-el-dia-la-agenda-o-el-mes/mes.png)

From the barber's name, in this view, you can also **Create time block** ([Block a barber's calendar](/ayuda/calendario/bloquear-el-calendario)).

> [!NOTE]
> In **Month** the days of the week show up as single letters (S M T W T F S). How the occupancy percentage is calculated isn't documented yet.

## The Team\* filter

In any view, **Team\*** decides who you see: **Working** (those with hours that day), **Whole team** or one barber. [Filter by team](/ayuda/calendario/filtrar-el-calendario-por-equipo) explains it.

![The Team filter open](/assets/es/calendario/ver-el-dia-la-agenda-o-el-mes/equipo-filtro.png)

## Frequently asked questions

**Which one do I use to book?**
**Day**. You tap a barber's empty slot and **Create appointment** opens with the time filled in.

**How do I see which days a barber is busiest?**
In **Month**, by choosing that barber at the top.

**Is the view remembered?**
When you come back, the calendar opens on **Day**.
