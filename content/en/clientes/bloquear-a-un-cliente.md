# Block a client

**In short:** in the client's record, tap **Actions** › **Block client**, write the reason in **Description**, and tap **Block**. The app warns you: “If you block the client, they won't be able to book appointments online.” The client moves to the **Block** tab of the list.

## When to block

Use it for a client who books online and doesn't show up, or who cancels at the last minute again and again. Once blocked, they can't book on their own: if they want to come in, they'll have to call and your team decides.

## Steps

1. Tap **Clients** in the side menu and, in the client's row, tap **Actions** › **View**.
2. At the top right of the record, tap **Actions** › **Block client**.
3. Read the warning: “If you block the client, they won't be able to book appointments online.”
4. Write the reason in **Description\***. It's required, and it helps your team later: “Three online appointments missed in September.”
5. Tap **Block**.
   ![The Block client window with the warning, the Description field and the Block button](/assets/es/clientes/bloquear-a-un-cliente/modal.png)

## What changes

- The client can no longer book appointments online.
- They show up in the **Block** tab of the **Clients** list, whose definition says: “These are the clients we've blocked for some reason. These clients can't book appointments online.”

> [!WARNING]
> Before you block, check their **No-show** and **Canceled** counters in **General information**. A **VIP** client with a single miss doesn't deserve a block.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does blocking delete the client?**
No. They stay in your list with their appointments, their payments and their notes. They only lose online booking.

**How do I unblock them?**
Where this is done hasn't been confirmed yet. Look for them in the **Block** tab, open their record and check **Actions**.

**Does the client find out they're blocked?**
The app doesn't say it notifies them. They'll notice when they try to book online.

**Can I block someone without writing the reason?**
No. **Description\*** is required.
