# Find a client

**In short:** in **Clients**, type in the **Search** field and tap the **Search** button. The table narrows down to the matching clients. To open one, tap **Actions** › **View** in their row.

## Steps

1. Tap **Clients** in the side menu.
2. Type whatever you know about the client in the **Search** field, to the right of **Client list**.
3. Tap **Search**.
   ![The Search field of the client list with its Search button](/assets/es/clientes/la-lista-de-clientes/buscador.png)
4. In the client's row, tap **Actions** and then **View** to open their record.
   ![A row's Actions button open, showing the View option](/assets/es/clientes/la-lista-de-clientes/acciones-fila.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Which details the field searches by (name only, or also phone and email) hasn't been confirmed yet. If the name doesn't work, try another detail.

## Other ways to find them

- **By type:** tap the **New**, **Retained**, **Frequent**, **VIP**, **Nearly lost**, **Lost**, **Recovered** or **Block** tab. The list narrows down to just those clients. What each one means is in [Client types](/ayuda/clientes/tipos-de-cliente).
- **Sorting the table:** tap a column name (**Name**, **# Appointments**, **LTV**…) to sort by it.
- **Page by page:** at the bottom, in **Rows per page**, go up to 30 clients per page and use the arrows.

## If they don't show up

| What to check | Why |
|---|---|
| The location at the top | The list only shows the clients of the selected location. Change it with [Switch locations](/ayuda/navegacion/cambiar-de-sucursal). |
| The **All** tab | If you're on **VIP** and the client is **New**, they won't appear. |
| The **Block** tab | A blocked client still exists; look for them there. |
| How the name is spelled | They may be saved under a different name or a different last name. |

## Frequently asked questions

**I searched and the list came up empty. Did I delete them?**
No. Check the location and the tab, and clear the search box to see the full list again.

**Can I search by phone number?**
That's still to be confirmed. In the meantime, search by name.

**Doesn't the magnifying glass at the top search clients?**
No. The magnifying glass in the header opens the **Search** screen, which looks up appointments. For clients, use the **Search** field in this list.

**How do I see the appointments of the client I found?**
Open their record and tap the **Appointments** tab: see [A client's appointments](/ayuda/clientes/citas-de-un-cliente).
