# A client's appointments

**In short:** in a client's record, the **Appointments** tab lists all their appointments, one card for each: the service, the **Date**, the **Barber**, the **Booking type**, whether it was **No preference**, and its status. The **View** button opens the appointment.

![The Appointments tab of a client with one card per appointment](/assets/es/clientes/citas-de-un-cliente/pantalla.png)

## How to get there

1. Tap **Clients** in the side menu.
2. In the client's row, tap **Actions** › **View**.
3. Tap the **Appointments** tab. You can also get there with **See more**, under **Last appointment** in **General information**.

## What each card says

| Detail | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Service | The name of the service, for example “Haircut.” |
| **Date** | The day of the appointment. |
| **Barber** | Who is serving or served them. |
| **Booking type** | **Internal**, if your team created it, or **Online appointment**, if the client booked it. |
| **No preference** | **Yes** if the client didn't ask for a barber; **No** if they chose one. |
| Status | Where it stands: **Pending**, **Completed**… |
| **View** | Opens that appointment. |

The statuses are the same as in the calendar. They're explained in [Appointment statuses](/ayuda/calendario/estados-de-una-cita).

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I know if they booked on their own or my team booked them?**
Look at **Booking type**: **Online appointment** means the client booked it, **Internal** means your team created it.

**What does “No preference” mean?**
That when booking, they didn't ask for a specific barber. See [Clients with no barber preference](/ayuda/calendario/clientes-sin-preferencia-de-barbero).

**Which barber do they usually get their cut with?**
Go through the cards and look at **Barber**. In the details of any appointment, the **Summary** tab sums it up under **LOYALTY**: see [Open an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/abrir-una-cita).

**Can I create a new appointment from here?**
Yes. Tap **Actions** › **Create appointment**, at the top right of the record.
