# Create a client

**In short:** in **Clients**, tap **Create client**. Fill in at least **First name**, **Last name**, **Phone** and **Email** (all four have an asterisk) and tap **Create**. You can leave the rest of the details for later.

## Steps

1. Tap **Clients** in the side menu.
2. Tap **Create client**, at the top right. The **Create client** page opens.
   ![The Create client form with its fields, the Online booking and payment only switch and the Create button](/assets/es/clientes/crear-un-cliente/formulario.png)
3. Fill in the required fields: **First name\***, **Last name\***, **Phone\*** and **Email\***.
4. If you have them, add the other details (what each one is is listed below).
5. Tap **Create**, at the bottom right. To leave without saving, tap **Cancel**, at the top.

## The fields

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| **First name\*** | Just the first name: “Laura.” |
| **Last name\*** | Just the last name: “Gómez.” |
| **Phone\*** | Their phone number. Pick the country with the little flag first, so the prefix (+57, +1…) comes out right. |
| **Email\*** | Their email: `correo@ejemplo.com`. |
| **Country**, **State**, **City** | Where they live. They're lists: pick the country and the state and city options fill in. |
| **Address** | Their address, in free text. |
| **Postal code** | The postal code of their address. |
| **Date of birth** | Using the field's calendar. |
| **Online booking and payment only** | A switch. If you turn it on, this client can only book and pay through online booking. |

At the top of the form there's a circle with a camera, to add a photo of the client.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Check the phone number before you tap **Create**. It's the number the client gets their reminders on, and the one you'll use to look them up later.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens right after you tap **Create** (whether it takes you to the record or back to the list) and what error the app shows if the phone number or email already exist: still to confirm. Exactly what the **Online booking and payment only** switch changes is also still to confirm.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to create the client before booking an appointment for them?**
No. From the calendar you can create the appointment and the client in the same step: see [Create an appointment for a new client](/ayuda/calendario/crear-una-cita-para-un-cliente-nuevo).

**Can I create a client without an email?**
No. **Email\*** is required, just like the phone number.

**Where do I change the details afterward?**
You'll see them in the **Profile** tab of their record: see [A client's profile](/ayuda/clientes/perfil-de-un-cliente).

**Can a barber create clients?**
The **Create client** button appears for the owner, admin and receptionist. If your role is barber and you don't see it, ask the front desk.
