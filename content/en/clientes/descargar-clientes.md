# Download the client list

**In short:** in **Settings › Permissions** there's a **Client download** row (by default only for the owner and the rent barber), but on the **Clients** screen we reviewed there's no download button.

## What you can do today

- Sort and search the list, and see up to 30 per page ([The client list](/ayuda/clientes/la-lista-de-clientes)).
- Download **Payroll** as CSV, Excel or PDF ([Download payroll](/ayuda/nomina/descargar-la-nomina)); it's the only download with a button that we saw in the app, besides your plan's invoices.

> [!NOTE]
> Where the client list is downloaded and in what format hasn't been documented yet. If your account shows a button we didn't see, let us know.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why doesn't my admin have this permission?**
By default, **Client download** is off for the admin and the receptionist. The owner can turn it on in **Settings › Permissions** or in the person's record.
