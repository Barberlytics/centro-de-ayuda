# Add family or friends to a client

**In short:** in the **Profile** tab of the record, the **Family and friends** block shows the people linked to that client: their name, the relationship (“Friend”) and their client type. How someone is added there hasn't been confirmed yet: there's no button for it under **Actions**.

## Where it is

1. Tap **Clients** in the side menu.
2. In the client's row, tap **Actions** › **View**.
3. Tap the **Profile** tab and scroll down to **Family and friends**.
   ![The Profile tab with the Family and friends block at the bottom](/assets/es/clientes/perfil-de-un-cliente/pantalla.png)

## What it shows for each person

| Detail | Example |
|---|---|
| Name | “Carlos Rodríguez” |
| Relationship | “Friend” |
| Client type | That person's **New**, **VIP**… badge |

Each person has an arrow **›** on the right to go to their record.

## What it's for

To keep track of who comes with whom. If Laura brings her son every three weeks, having him linked to her lets you book the two of them back to back and know who to message if one of them stops coming.

> [!NOTE]
> The record's **Actions** button only offers **Create note**, **Create appointment** and **Block client**: we didn't see any option to add family or friends, or a form to do it. The relationship may be created somewhere else (for example, when booking online) or it may not be possible to add it by hand. Still to confirm with the team.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I add the son my client always brings?**
For now there's no visible button for that. Create the son as a separate client with [Create a client](/ayuda/clientes/crear-un-cliente) and leave a note in the father's record.

**If I block the father, is the son blocked too?**
Nothing on the screen says so. Each client is blocked separately.

**Is the relationship shown in both records?**
Still to confirm.
