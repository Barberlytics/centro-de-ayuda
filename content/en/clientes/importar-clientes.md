# Import clients

**In short:** Barberlytics has provisions for importing clients: there's an **Import clients** permission in **Settings › Permissions** and notifications “When one of your own client imports fails later” and “When the import process finishes successfully” in the **Notification center**. But on the **Clients** screen we reviewed, there's no button to do it.

## Where what exists is

- **Settings › Permissions**: the **Import clients** row (**Active** switch) is on by default for the owner, the admin, the receptionist and the rent barber.
- **Settings › Notification center › Clients › Client import**: the two notifications, by **Email** and **Push**.
- **Clients**: the only button at the top is **Create client**.

> [!NOTE]
> Where the file is uploaded, what format it accepts and which columns it needs hasn't been documented yet. If your account shows an import button we didn't see, let us know so we can document it.

## In the meantime

Create clients one by one with **Create client** ([Create a client](/ayuda/clientes/crear-un-cliente)), or let them sign up on their own when they book online.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I move my clients over from another app?**
The app has the import permission and notifications, so the feature exists or is planned. The step-by-step is still to come.
