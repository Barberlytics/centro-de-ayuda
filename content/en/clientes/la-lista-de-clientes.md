# The client list: columns, search and pagination

**In short:** in **Clients** you have your whole clientele in one table: name, location, type, LTV, phone, email and how many appointments each one has. At the top there's a tab for each client type, the search box and the **Create client** button. To open someone's record, tap **Actions** › **View** in their row.

![The Clients screen: tabs by type, search box, client table and Create client button](/assets/es/clientes/la-lista-de-clientes/pantalla.png)

## How to get there

Tap **Clients** in the side menu. The screen says “Here you can manage your clients.” and, below it, **Client list** with the total (“43 Clients”).

The list is the one for the location you have selected at the top. If you're looking for someone from another location, switch it first: see [Switch locations](/ayuda/navegacion/cambiar-de-sucursal).

## The tabs by type

Above the list there's a tab for each client type: **All · New · Retained · Frequent · VIP · Nearly lost · Lost · Recovered · Block**. Tap one and the table narrows down to just those clients. **All** goes back to the full list.

![The All, New, Retained, Frequent, VIP, Nearly lost, Lost, Recovered and Block tabs](/assets/es/clientes/la-lista-de-clientes/pestanas.png)

When you pick a tab (other than **All**), the definition of that type appears. They're all in [Client types](/ayuda/clientes/tipos-de-cliente).

## The search box

To the right of **Client list** is the **Search** field with its **Search** button. [Find a client](/ayuda/clientes/buscar-un-cliente) walks you through it step by step.

![The client list's search box with the Search button](/assets/es/clientes/la-lista-de-clientes/buscador.png)

## What's in each column

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The initials in a circle and the client's name. For example, “LG · Laura Gómez.” |
| **Location** | The location the client belongs to. |
| **Type** | A colored badge with their type: **New**, **Retained**, **VIP**… |
| **LTV** | How much the client has spent at your barbershop in total. It's the same figure you see in their record. |
| **Phone** | Their phone number, with the middle hidden: `+57******2020`. |
| **Email** | Their email. |
| **# Appointments** | How many appointments they have. |
| **Action** | The **Actions** button, which opens the **View** option. |

Tap a column name to sort the list by it; the ▲ arrow marks which one is sorting.

## Open a client's record

In the client's row, tap **Actions** and then **View**. It's the only way into their record: it doesn't open when you tap the name. What you'll find inside is in [View a client](/ayuda/clientes/ver-un-cliente).

![A row's Actions button open, showing the View option](/assets/es/clientes/la-lista-de-clientes/acciones-fila.png)

## Change pages

Below the table you choose how many clients to see per page in **Rows per page**: 10, 15, 20, 25 or 30. Next to it you'll see where you are in the list (“1-10 of 43”) and the arrows to go forward or back.

![The list's pagination: Rows per page and 1-10 of 43](/assets/es/clientes/la-lista-de-clientes/paginacion.png)

> [!TIP]
> If your list is long, raise **Rows per page** to 30 and sort by **# Appointments**: you'll see at a glance who your regulars are.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does the phone number show asterisks?**
The list hides the middle of the phone number for every profile, the owner's included. In the client's record it isn't shown in full either.

**How many clients do I have?**
The number next to **Client list**, with the **All** tab selected. It counts the ones for the location you have at the top.

**Does a barber see the client list?**
Yes, with the same hidden phone number. What they can do inside a record depends on their role's permissions.

**How do I add a client?**
With **Create client**, at the top right. See [Create a client](/ayuda/clientes/crear-un-cliente).
