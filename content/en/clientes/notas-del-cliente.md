# Client notes: notes, medication and allergies

**In short:** in the client's record, tap **Actions** › **Create note**, choose the **Type** (**Notes**, **Medication** or **Allergies**), write the **Description** and tap **Create**. The note goes into the **Client notes** block, under the tab for its type.

## The three types

| Type | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Notes** | Anything you want to remember: “Likes a high fade,” “Always comes in with his son.” |
| **Medication** | Medicines they take that are worth knowing when you serve them. |
| **Allergies** | Products or ingredients that can't be used on them. |

## Steps

1. Tap **Clients** in the side menu and, in the client's row, **Actions** › **View**.
2. At the top right of the record, tap **Actions** › **Create note**. The **Create note** button at the bottom of **General information** works too.
3. In the window, choose the **Type\***: **Notes**, **Medication** or **Allergies**.
4. Write the **Description\***.
5. Tap **Create**.
   ![The Create note window with the Type field, the Description and the Create button](/assets/es/clientes/notas-del-cliente/crear-nota.png)

## Where they're read

In **General information**, in the **Client notes** block, with a tab for each type: **Notes**, **Medication** and **Allergies**. If there's nothing, it says “You'll find the client's notes here.”

![The Client notes block with the Notes, Medication and Allergies tabs](/assets/es/clientes/ver-un-cliente/notas.png)

The same notes appear in the details of any of the client's appointments, under its **Notes** tab: see [Open an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/abrir-una-cita). That way the barber sees them without leaving the calendar.

> [!TIP]
> Allergies always go in **Allergies**, not in **Notes**. It's the tab anyone checks first before applying a product.

> [!NOTE]
> Whether a note can be edited or deleted after you create it, and whether notes are shared between locations, is still to be confirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the client see the notes?**
No. They're internal: only your team sees them in the record and in the appointment details.

**Can I write a note from the calendar?**
Yes. In the appointment details, in the **Notes** tab, with **Add note**. It ends up in the client's record all the same.

**Who can create notes?**
Anyone who opens the record and has the **Actions** button. If your role doesn't show it, ask the front desk.
