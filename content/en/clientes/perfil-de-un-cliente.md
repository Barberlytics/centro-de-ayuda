# A client's profile: location, birthday and online-only booking

**In short:** the **Profile** tab of the record shows who the client is: their name with the type badge, the **Online booking and payment only** switch, their **Location**, their **Date of birth**, their **Creation date** and the **Family and friends** block.

![A client's Profile tab: name with VIP badge, Online booking and payment only, Location, Date of birth, Attitude, Creation date and Family and friends](/assets/es/clientes/perfil-de-un-cliente/pantalla.png)

## How to get there

1. Tap **Clients** in the side menu.
2. In the client's row, tap **Actions** › **View**.
3. Tap the **Profile** tab.

## What it shows

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Header | The photo or the circle with their initial, the name (“Laura Gómez”) and their type badge (**VIP**, **New**…). On the right, the **Online booking and payment only** switch. |
| **Location** | **Country**, **State**, **City**, **Address** and **Postal code**, as they were entered when the client was created. |
| Details | **Date of birth**, **Attitude** (shows “--” if there's nothing) and **Creation date**: the day the client was registered at your barbershop, for example “12/MAR/2025.” |
| **Family and friends** | The people linked to this client: name, relationship (“Friend”) and their client type. See [Add family or friends to a client](/ayuda/clientes/familia-y-amigos). |

The type you see in the badge is calculated by the app: see [Client types](/ayuda/clientes/tipos-de-cliente).

> [!NOTE]
> On this tab we didn't see a button to edit the details, or what exactly the **Online booking and payment only** switch does when you turn it on. What **Attitude** means and where it's filled in is also still to be confirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Since when have they been my client?**
Look at **Creation date**. It's the day they were registered, not the day of their first appointment.

**Where do I see their phone number and email?**
In the **Clients** list, in the **Phone** and **Email** columns. They don't appear in **Profile**.

**What's the use of knowing their birthday?**
To wish them a happy birthday or make them an offer that day. The app doesn't send anything on its own for birthdays.

**Can I change their type from here?**
No. The type is calculated by the app from their appointments.
