# The products a client has bought

**In short:** the **Products** tab of the record lists the products the client has bought at your barbershop and how many units of each, for example “Matte wax · 3 Units.”

![A client's Products tab with a product and its units](/assets/es/clientes/productos-de-un-cliente/pantalla.png)

## How to get there

1. Tap **Clients** in the side menu.
2. In the client's row, tap **Actions** › **View**.
3. Tap the **Products** tab.

## What it shows

One row per product, with its name and the total units you've sold them. If the client has never bought anything, the tab comes up empty.

> [!TIP]
> If Laura has bought 3 units of the same wax, you know what to offer her when she's running low: figure about six weeks per unit and book the sale with her next haircut.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do I see how much they paid for those products?**
In the **Payment methods** tab of the same record: see [A client's payments](/ayuda/clientes/pagos-de-un-cliente).

**Where do I see the products for my whole barbershop?**
This tab is for a single client. Your inventory is in **Services & Products** › **Products**, and product sales are in [Sales by service and products](/ayuda/metricas/ventas-por-servicio-y-de-productos).

**Can I sell them a product from here?**
No. Products are sold when you charge an appointment or with a quick sale from the calendar.
