# Client types: new, retained, frequent, VIP, nearly lost, lost and recovered

**In short:** Barberlytics gives each client a badge based on how many appointments they have and how long they take to come back: **New**, **Retained**, **Frequent**, **VIP**, **Nearly lost**, **Lost** or **Recovered**. **Block** is separate: those are the clients you blocked. The badge shows in the **Type** column of the list and in each client's record.

## Where to see each type's definition

1. Tap **Clients** in the side menu.
2. Tap the tab for the type you want to understand. Its definition appears below the tabs and the list narrows down to those clients.

Here are all eight, exactly as the app shows them.

## New

“New clients are those who haven't had any appointments yet or only one.”

That's Carlos, who came in for the first time last week, or the one you created who hasn't come in yet.

![The New tab with its definition](/assets/es/clientes/tipos-de-cliente/nuevo.png)

## Retained

“These clients are those who have had 2 appointments at the barbershop.”

They came back once. This is the moment to lock in the third.

![The Retained tab with its definition](/assets/es/clientes/tipos-de-cliente/retenido.png)

## Frequent

“These are the clients who have 3 or more appointments at the barbershop and are already regulars.”

![The Frequent tab with its definition](/assets/es/clientes/tipos-de-cliente/frecuente.png)

## VIP

“These are the clients who have visited us for more than 3 cycles, which means they're very loyal to the barbershop.”

Here it's no longer just the number of appointments that counts, but consistency: the client has kept up their visit rhythm for more than three cycles. What a cycle is is explained in [How a client's type changes over time](/ayuda/clientes/como-se-clasifica-un-cliente).

![The VIP tab with its definition](/assets/es/clientes/tipos-de-cliente/vip.png)

## Nearly lost

“These are the clients who have stopped coming to the barbershop for 3 frequency cycles, meaning there's a risk of losing them.”

This is the list to message today. In Metrics you'll also find them in [Risk alerts](/ayuda/metricas/alertas-de-riesgo-de-clientes).

![The Nearly lost tab with its definition](/assets/es/clientes/tipos-de-cliente/pre-perdida.png)

## Lost

“These are the clients who have stopped coming to the barbershop for 4 visit frequency cycles and have already been lost.”

![The Lost tab with its definition](/assets/es/clientes/tipos-de-cliente/perdido.png)

## Recovered

“These are the clients who were already lost but came back to the barbershop and whom we need to look after so they keep coming back often.”

![The Recovered tab with its definition](/assets/es/clientes/tipos-de-cliente/recuperado.png)

## Block

“These are the clients we've blocked for some reason. These clients can't book appointments online.”

It's not a type the app calculates: these are the clients someone on your team blocked. See [Block a client](/ayuda/clientes/bloquear-a-un-cliente).

![The Block tab with its definition](/assets/es/clientes/tipos-de-cliente/bloqueo.png)

## The types at a glance

| Type | The rule, in short |
|---|---|
| **New** | No appointments or just one |
| **Retained** | 2 appointments |
| **Frequent** | 3 appointments or more |
| **VIP** | Has been coming for more than 3 cycles |
| **Nearly lost** | Has gone 3 cycles without coming |
| **Lost** | Has gone 4 cycles without coming |
| **Recovered** | Was lost and came back |
| **Block** | You blocked them; they can't book online |

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change a client's type by hand?**
No. The app calculates it on its own from their appointments. The only thing you decide is whether to block them or not.

**Where do I see how many clients I have of each type?**
Tap each tab in **Clients**: the number next to **Client list** changes. In Metrics, the donut chart [Appointments by client type](/ayuda/metricas/clientes-citas-por-tipo-de-cliente) groups them by period.

**Can a VIP client become Nearly lost?**
Yes. If they stop coming for 3 cycles, their type changes even if they used to be VIP. See [How a client's type changes over time](/ayuda/clientes/como-se-clasifica-un-cliente).
