# View a client: general information

**In short:** from the **Clients** list, tap **Actions** › **View** in someone's row. **View client** opens, with five tabs. The first, **General information**, sums up the client: their twelve indicators, what you need to know about them, their last appointment and their notes. At the top right, **Actions** lets you create a note, create an appointment or block them.

![The View client record on General information: indicators, Important information, Last appointment and Client notes](/assets/es/clientes/ver-un-cliente/pantalla.png)

## How to open the record

1. Tap **Clients** in the side menu.
2. In the client's row, tap **Actions**.
3. Tap **View**.

The record only opens from the list. To go back, tap the **←** arrow next to **View client**.

## The five tabs

| Tab | What it has |
|---|---|
| **General information** | What this article covers: indicators, **Important information**, **Last appointment** and **Client notes**. |
| **Profile** | Their details: location, date of birth, family and friends. See [A client's profile](/ayuda/clientes/perfil-de-un-cliente). |
| **Appointments** | One card for each appointment. See [A client's appointments](/ayuda/clientes/citas-de-un-cliente). |
| **Payment methods** | Their transactions. See [A client's payments](/ayuda/clientes/pagos-de-un-cliente). |
| **Products** | What they've bought. See [The products a client has bought](/ayuda/clientes/productos-de-un-cliente). |

## The Actions button

At the top right. It offers three options:

- **Create note**: a note, a medication or an allergy. See [Client notes](/ayuda/clientes/notas-del-cliente).
- **Create appointment**: opens the same **Create appointment** panel as the calendar, with this client. See [Create an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/crear-una-cita).
- **Block client**: stops them from booking online. See [Block a client](/ayuda/clientes/bloquear-a-un-cliente).

![The record's Actions menu with Create note, Create appointment and Block client](/assets/es/clientes/ver-un-cliente/acciones.png)

## The indicators

Twelve cards with the client's numbers: **Total appointments**, **View index**, **FOV**, **LTV**, **Average sale**, **Number of transactions**, **Completed**, **Canceled**, **Didn't attend**, **Rebooks**, **Booking time** and **AVF**.

![The twelve indicator cards in a client's record](/assets/es/clientes/ver-un-cliente/indicadores.png)

What each one means is in [A client's indicators](/ayuda/clientes/indicadores-de-un-cliente).

## Important information

Two counters: **No-show**, the number of times the client missed without notice, and **Canceled**, the appointments that were canceled. For example, “No-show 1 · Canceled 3.”

![The Important information block with No-show and Canceled](/assets/es/clientes/ver-un-cliente/informacion-importante.png)

## Last appointment

The card for their most recent appointment: the service (“Haircut”), the **Date**, the **Barber** and its status (**Pending**, **Completed**…). The **View** button opens that appointment. The **See more** button below takes you to the **Appointments** tab with all of them.

![The Last appointment card with the service, the date, the barber, the status and the View and See more buttons](/assets/es/clientes/ver-un-cliente/ultima-cita.png)

## Client notes

Three tabs: **Notes**, **Medication** and **Allergies**. If there's nothing, it says “You'll find the client's notes here.” The **Create note** button adds one.

![The Client notes block with the Notes, Medication and Allergies tabs and the Create note button](/assets/es/clientes/ver-un-cliente/notas.png)

> [!TIP]
> Before serving someone who's new to you, check **Allergies**. It takes a second and saves you a bad moment with a product.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do I see how much a client has spent in total?**
On the **LTV** card in this tab.

**Can I book an appointment for them from here?**
Yes. Tap **Actions** › **Create appointment**.

**Where do I change their phone number or email?**
In the **Profile** tab. See [A client's profile](/ayuda/clientes/perfil-de-un-cliente).

**Does a barber see the same thing?**
They see the record. What they can touch under **Actions** depends on their role's permissions.
