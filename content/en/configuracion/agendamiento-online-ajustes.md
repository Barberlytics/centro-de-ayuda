# Online booking: every setting on the Online booking screen

**In short:** the **Online booking** card in **Settings** opens the “Online booking” screen. There you decide how your clients get to book (your website, a link, a kiosk at the shop) and the booking rules: how far ahead an appointment can be booked, up to what date, the extra charge when the schedule is full, the waitlist, how clients pay and how late they can cancel. Everything is saved with **Save**, at the bottom of the page.

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Online booking**. The card says: “Adjust how clients can book and manage their appointments online.”

![The Online booking screen with the block for adding bookings to your website](/assets/es/configuracion/agendamiento-online-ajustes/pantalla.png)

The screen opens with: “Adjust how clients can book and manage their appointments online. These are general settings, but they can be set individually for each location.”

## The blocks on the screen, in order

| Block | What it's for | Example | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Add bookings to your website** | Three snippets to put booking on your page: **Side panel**, **Open from link** and **Embedded booking**. | Your website already exists and you want a floating “Book now” button: you copy the one under **Side panel**. | [Floating “Book now” button](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/panel-lateral) |
| **Online booking link for your website** | The **Direct booking link** and the “UTM tracking (optional)” (**Source**, **Medium**, **Campaign**). | You paste the link in your Instagram bio with Source “instagram”. | [The direct link and UTMs](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/enlace-directo-y-utm) |
| **Front Desk link** | The link for a kiosk where the client checks in on arrival. | A tablet at the entrance with the **Front Desk kiosk link** open. | [Front Desk: the reception kiosk](/ayuda/front-desk/que-es) |
| **Online booking availability** | The **Minimum notice to book**, how many weeks ahead a client can book and the **Default time slot interval**. | 30 minutes of notice, “No more than 4 weeks in the future”, 30-minute slots. | [Minimum notice and time slots](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/anticipacion-y-franjas) |
| **Price increase** | An extra charge per service when occupancy goes above a set level. | At 90% occupancy the price goes up 5%. | [Price increase by occupancy](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/aumento-de-precios-por-ocupacion) |
| **Waitlist** | How long the client has to accept an appointment that opened up and to get there. | 15 minutes to accept and 15 to arrive. | [Waitlist times](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/tiempos-de-la-lista-de-espera) |
| **Accepted payment methods** | How the client can pay for the appointment they book online: cash at the shop or with Stripe. | Both boxes checked. | [Payment methods for online booking](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/metodos-de-pago-de-la-reserva) |
| **Online cancellation and rescheduling** | How late the client can cancel or move their appointment, the fee for canceling late and how many times they can cancel online. | “Up to 3 hours in advance”, 10% fee, maximum 5. | [Cancel or reschedule online](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/cancelar-o-reprogramar-en-linea) |
| **Preview of scheduling and cancellation policies** | The policy text the client sees when booking, with tags that fill themselves in. | “<<time_for_cancel>>” becomes “3 hours”. | [Booking policies](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/politicas-de-reserva) |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The rule settings (from **Online booking availability** down to the policies) are saved together with the **Save** button at the bottom. If you change something and leave without saving, the change is lost.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where is the screen where my clients see and cancel their appointments?**
It's a different card: **Client portal**, right below **Online booking**. See [Client portal](/ayuda/configuracion/portal-del-cliente).

**Are these settings the same in all my locations?**
The screen says they are general and can be set for each location. Where you change them per location hasn't been confirmed yet. See [General settings and per-location settings](/ayuda/sucursales/ajustes-generales-y-por-sucursal).

**Who can change these settings?**
The owner. A commission barber doesn't see this card. For the other roles it depends on the **Account settings** row in **Settings › Permissions**. See [Permissions: where they're changed and how they're saved](/ayuda/configuracion/permisos-donde-se-cambian).
