# Notification center: how it works

**In short:** in **Settings › Notification center** you decide which automatic alerts Barberlytics sends and through which channel. There is one tab per role and, inside each, one switch for every alert and every channel: **Email** and **Push**. It's a general setting for all locations.

## Where it is

**Settings › Notifications › Notification center**. The card says: “Review notifications sent to clients and barbers about appointments.” The screen explains: “View and manage all the automatic messages sent to your team and clients. This setting is general and applies to all locations.”

![The Notification center with the tabs by role and the alerts with their Email and Push switches](/assets/es/configuracion/centro-de-notificaciones-como-funciona/pantalla.png)

## How it's organized

- **One tab per role:** **Location administrator**, **Commission barber**, **Rent barber**, **Receptionist - front desk** and **Owner**. See [Which alerts each role gets](/ayuda/configuracion/notificaciones-por-rol).
- **The alerts, grouped by topic:**

| Group | What it alerts you about | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Clients** | Imports, bad reviews, status changes (Churn, Pre-Churn, Recovered, VIP) and blocks. | [Client alerts](/ayuda/configuracion/notificaciones-de-clientes) |
| **Products** | Low stock or sold out, for products and combos, and imports. | [Product and combo alerts](/ayuda/configuracion/notificaciones-de-productos) |
| **Calendar** | Schedule booked above a percentage, blocks and vacations. | [Calendar alerts](/ayuda/configuracion/notificaciones-del-calendario) |
| **Metrics** | The health indicator going down or up, and each barber's retention. | [Metrics alerts](/ayuda/configuracion/notificaciones-de-metricas) |
| **Reports** | “Daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / annual summary report delivery” and “Price increase suggestion when you have strong demand and retention.” | — |

- **One switch per alert and per channel.** **Email** sends the alert to the person's email; **Push** sends it to the app. You can turn on one, both or neither.

## Change an alert

1. Pick the role's tab.
2. Turn **Email** and **Push** on or off for the alert you want.
3. Tap **Save**, at the bottom of the page.

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows when you tap **Save** hasn't been confirmed yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this the same as the bell at the top?**
No. The bell shows the alerts you've already received, in the **All** and **Unread** tabs. See [See your notifications](/ayuda/navegacion/notificaciones). The **Notification center** decides which ones get sent.

**Can I get an alert by email but not by push?**
Yes. Each alert has one switch for **Email** and another for **Push**.

**Can I set it differently for each location?**
No. The screen says this setting is general and applies to all locations.
