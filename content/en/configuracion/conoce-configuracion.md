# Get to know Settings: the four groups of settings

**In short:** **Settings** gathers all of your barbershop's settings in four groups: **Account settings**, **Team**, **Sales** and **Notifications**. Each setting is a card with its title and a sentence that says what it's for. At the bottom of the page is **Delete account**.

## Where it is

**Settings** is the last option in the menu. It looks the same no matter which location you have selected at the top, because these are company settings.

![The Settings index with the four groups of cards](/assets/es/configuracion/conoce-configuracion/indice.png)

## Account settings

| Card | What the card says | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Business details** | “Manage settings such as your company name and time zone.” | Time zone, time format, start of the week, opening hours, holidays and the links to your website and social media. See [Business details: time zone, time format, week and holidays](/ayuda/configuracion/detalles-del-negocio-hora-y-calendario). |
| **Billing details and invoices** | “See your Barbelytics fee and invoice activity and manage your billing information.” | Your plan, your billing details, your card and your invoices. See [Billing details and invoices](/ayuda/configuracion/detalles-de-facturacion-y-facturas). |
| **Online booking** | “Adjust how clients can book and manage their appointments online.” | How your clients book (your website, the direct link, the Front Desk kiosk) and the rules: notice, price increase, waitlist, payments, cancellation and policies. See [Online booking: every setting](/ayuda/configuracion/agendamiento-online-ajustes). |
| **Client portal** | “Put on your website the screen where your clients see, reschedule and cancel their appointments.” | The screen where your clients see, move and cancel their appointments on their own. See [Client portal](/ayuda/configuracion/portal-del-cliente). |
| **Business closure periods** | “Set the period when your business will be closed.” | The dates when the barbershop is closed. See [Business closure periods](/ayuda/configuracion/periodos-de-cierre). |
| **Payment devices** | “Connect your system to the devices that let you process payments.” | The card readers you use to charge by card. See [Payment devices](/ayuda/configuracion/dispositivos-de-pago). |
| **Locations** | “Manage the business's locations: create them, edit them and see their details.” | The list of your locations, with the **Create Location** button. See [Company and locations: how they differ](/ayuda/sucursales/empresa-y-sucursales). |
| **Company** | “View and manage your company's details.” | The list of your companies. See [Company: your company's details](/ayuda/configuracion/empresa). |

## Team

| Card | What the card says | What it's for |
|---|---|---|

| Card | What the card says | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Notification center** | “Review notifications sent to clients and barbers about appointments.” | Which alerts are sent, by email or push, with one tab per role. See [Notification center: how it works](/ayuda/configuracion/centro-de-notificaciones-como-funciona). |

## Delete account

At the bottom of the page is **Delete account**: “Permanently deletes your account and all the data associated with your business. This action is irreversible and cannot be undone.”

> [!WARNING]
> **Delete account** can't be undone. See [Delete your account](/ayuda/configuracion/eliminar-tu-cuenta) before you tap it.

## Frequently asked questions

**I'm a commission barber and I only see two settings. Is that normal?**
Yes. With the commission barber profile, **Settings** only shows **Time format** and **Delete account**. The time format screen warns: “these changes will only show on your calendar, not on the barbershop's calendar or your clients'.” The other settings belong to the owner.

**Does changing a setting affect all my locations?**
The **Settings** screens look the same in whichever location you pick at the top. Several say they can be adjusted per location, but where you do that hasn't been confirmed yet. See [General settings and per-location settings](/ayuda/sucursales/ajustes-generales-y-por-sucursal).

**Where do I change the time zone?**
In **Settings › Business details**, under “Time and calendar settings.”
