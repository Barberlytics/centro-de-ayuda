# Billing details and invoices

**In short:** in **Settings › Billing details and invoices** you see what you pay Barberlytics: your plan and its amount, the details that appear on your invoice, the card you pay with and the list of invoices to download.

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Billing details and invoices**. The card says: “See your Barbelytics fee and invoice activity and manage your billing information.”

![The billing screen with the billing details, the payment method and the list of invoices](/assets/es/configuracion/detalles-de-facturacion-y-facturas/pantalla.png)

## What's on the screen

**Billing details.** “Add or edit your default billing details to show on the Barbelytics invoice.” It shows the name, the plan (for example “Annual”), the amount and the address, for example Mi Barbería, Calle 10 # 20-30. The **Edit billing details** button changes them.

**Default payment method.** “Add or edit your default payment method for Barbelytics fees and purchases.” It shows your card with the last four digits, for example “**** **** **** 4242”.

**List of invoices and fees.** How many documents you have and when the next one comes out: “Your next invoice will be available on …”. Each row has **Type**, **Total**, **Date** and, under **Action**, the **Download** button.

## Manage your cards

1. Tap **Manage payment methods**.
2. “Manage payment methods” opens: “Control how you pay for your membership. The highlighted payment method is the default; the others are secondary.”
   ![The Manage payment methods panel with the default card and the Add payment method button](/assets/es/configuracion/detalles-de-facturacion-y-facturas/metodos-de-pago.png)
3. To add another card, tap **Add payment method**.

## Download an invoice

1. Filter the list by **Type**, **Year** or **Month** if you have many.
2. On the row you want, tap **Download**.

> [!NOTE]
> What **Edit billing details** and **Add payment method** ask for after you tap them hasn't been confirmed yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I change the address on my invoice?**
Tap **Edit billing details**, in the **Billing details** block.

**When does my next invoice arrive?**
The date is in the “List of invoices and fees,” in the sentence “Your next invoice will be available on …”.

**Is this screen the same as My profile?**
No. **My profile** shows your account details, your plan, your card and your invoices. Here you also get the billing details, card management and the list with filters. See [My profile](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/mi-perfil).
