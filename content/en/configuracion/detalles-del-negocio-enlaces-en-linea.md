# Business details: website and social media

**In short:** in **Settings › Business details**, the “Online links” block stores your barbershop's website, Instagram, Facebook and Google Maps links. Those links appear in the emails the app sends to your clients.

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Business details**. The card says: “Manage settings such as your company name and time zone.” The “Online links” block is below “Time and calendar settings.”

## The fields

The screen explains it like this: “Add your company's website and social media links. These links will be visible in emails and other platform interactions with your clients.”

![The Online links block with the Website, Instagram page, Facebook page and Google Maps fields](/assets/es/configuracion/detalles-del-negocio-enlaces-en-linea/enlaces.png)

| Field | Required | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Website** | No | Your page's address, for example `https://mibarberia.com`. |
| **Instagram page** | No | The link to your Instagram profile. |
| **Facebook page** | No | The link to your Facebook page. |
| **Google Maps** | No | The link to your barbershop on Google Maps, so clients know how to get there. |

## Steps

1. Paste each full link into its field, starting with `https://`.
2. Tap **Save**, at the bottom of the page.

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows when you tap **Save** and exactly which emails each link appears in haven't been confirmed yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this where I put the link for my clients to book?**
No. This block is for your website and your social media. The booking link is in **Settings › Online booking**. See [The direct booking link and UTM parameters](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/enlace-directo-y-utm).

**I don't have a website. Do I leave the field empty?**
Yes. No link is required. Fill in only the ones you have.

**Where do I get the Google Maps link?**
Search for your barbershop on Google Maps, tap **Share** and copy the link. Paste it into **Google Maps**.
