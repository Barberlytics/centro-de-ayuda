# Delete your account

**In short:** **Delete account** is at the bottom of **Settings**. It erases your account and all of your barbershop's data, and it can't be undone.

## Where it is

**Settings**, at the bottom of the page, below the four groups of settings. The card says: “Permanently deletes your account and all the data associated with your business. This action is irreversible and cannot be undone.”

![The Delete account card at the bottom of Settings](/assets/es/configuracion/eliminar-tu-cuenta/tarjeta.png)

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting the account is irreversible. You lose the appointments, clients, team, sales and everything else in your barbershop. There is no way to get them back afterward.

## Before you tap it

- Download whatever you want to keep: your invoices from [Billing details and invoices](/ayuda/configuracion/detalles-de-facturacion-y-facturas) and your payroll from **Payroll**.
- If you just want to stop using the app for a while, don't delete the account: sign out. See [Sign out](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/cerrar-sesion).

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows when you tap **Delete account** (whether it asks for confirmation, a code or a reason) hasn't been confirmed yet. It wasn't tapped so nothing would be erased.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does deleting my account cancel what I pay Barberlytics?**
The card says it deletes the account and all the business data. What happens to the plan and any pending charges isn't documented yet. Check [Billing details and invoices](/ayuda/configuracion/detalles-de-facturacion-y-facturas) first.

**I'm a barber and I want to leave the barbershop. Should I delete my account?**
With the barber profile, **Settings** also shows **Delete account**. Before you do it, talk to the owner: they can deactivate your profile without erasing your history.

**Can I delete just one location?**
Yes, without touching the account. In **Settings › Locations**, each location has **Deactivate** and **Delete**.
