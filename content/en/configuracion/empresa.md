# Company: your company's details

**In short:** in **Settings › Company** you'll find your account's “List of companies,” with each one's name, ID, phone and status, and the **View** and **Edit** actions. The company is the whole; your shops are the locations.

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Company**. The card says: “View and manage your company's details.” The screen is called “Companies” and explains: “In this section you can stay in full control of your companies.”

![The list of companies with its columns and the Action column](/assets/es/configuracion/empresa/lista.png)

## What you see

The “List of companies” says how many you have, for example “1 Company,” and shows one row for each:

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Company** | The name, for example Mi Barbería. |
| **ID** | The number it's registered under. |
| **Phone** | The company's contact phone. |
| **Status** | Whether it's active. |
| **Action** | **View** opens the record; **Edit** opens its details so you can change them. |

## Company and locations

Your company groups the locations, the team and the service and product catalogs. It's created once and each location activates its own. Locations are managed separately, in **Settings › Locations**, with the **Create Location** button.

![The company's list of locations, with the Create Location button](/assets/es/sucursales/empresa-y-sucursales/lista.png)

To understand the difference, see [Company and locations: how they differ](/ayuda/sucursales/empresa-y-sucursales).

## Frequently asked questions

**Are the company and the location the same thing?**
No. The company is the whole; each location is a shop of that company, with its own currency, time zone and language.

**Can I have more than one company?**
The list is designed for several (“List of companies”). How many your plan allows and how another one is created isn't documented yet.

**Where do I change the currency or the time zone?**
In each location: **Settings › Locations › Edit**. See [Edit a location](/ayuda/sucursales/editar-una-sucursal). The general time zone is in **Settings › Business details**.
