# Which alerts each role gets

**In short:** the Notification center has one tab per role. Whatever you turn on in a tab is received by the people with that role, by **Email**, by **Push** or both. The setting is general: it applies to all locations.

## Where it is

**Settings › Notifications › Notification center**. The card says: “Review notifications sent to clients and barbers about appointments.” The screen warns: “This setting is general and applies to all locations.”

## The tabs

At the top there are five tabs, one per role:

![The five tabs of the Notification center: Location administrator, Commission barber, Rent barber, Receptionist and Owner](/assets/es/configuracion/notificaciones-por-rol/pestanas.png)

| Tab | Who gets what you turn on there |
|---|---|
| **Location administrator** | Whoever manages a location. |
| **Commission barber** | The barbers you pay commission. |
| **Rent barber** | The barbers who pay rent for their chair. |
| **Receptionist - front desk** | Whoever staffs the front desk. |
| **Owner** | The barbershop's owner. |

What each role is can be found in [The five roles](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/los-cinco-roles).

## Steps

1. Tap the role's tab.
   ![The Commission barber tab with its alerts and switches](/assets/es/configuracion/notificaciones-por-rol/barbero-por-comision.png)
2. Turn **Email** and **Push** on or off for each alert. The alerts are grouped into **Clients**, **Products**, **Calendar**, **Metrics** and **Reports**. The list for each group is in [Notification center: how it works](/ayuda/configuracion/centro-de-notificaciones-como-funciona).
   ![The Receptionist tab with its alerts and switches](/assets/es/configuracion/notificaciones-por-rol/recepcion.png)
3. Tap **Save**, at the bottom of the page.

> [!NOTE]
> Whether every role has exactly the same list of alerts or whether any change between tabs wasn't compared alert by alert. Neither was what the app shows when you tap **Save**.

## Frequently asked questions

**I want only the owner to get the client block alert.**
Turn it on in the **Owner** tab and leave it off in the others.

**Can I set it differently for each location?**
No. The screen says this setting is general and applies to all locations.

**A barber says they aren't getting the alerts.**
Check that the alert has **Push** or **Email** turned on in their role's tab. Alerts already received show up under the bell. See [See your notifications](/ayuda/navegacion/notificaciones).
