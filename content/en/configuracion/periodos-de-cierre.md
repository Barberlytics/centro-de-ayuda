# Business closure periods

**In short:** in **Settings › Business closure periods** you register the days the barbershop is closed, with **Add closed period**. While the closure lasts, the app warns: “Online bookings can't be made while your business is closed.”

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Business closure periods**. The card says: “Set the period when your business will be closed.” The screen is called “Company closure periods” and explains: “Set the period when your barbershop will be closed.”

![The list of closure periods with the Add new button](/assets/es/configuracion/periodos-de-cierre/pantalla.png)

Below is the list: “Here you'll find the list of all your barbershop's closure periods.”

## Add a closure period

1. Tap **Add new** or **Add closed period**.
2. The “Add closed period” panel opens.
   ![The Add closed period panel with the dates, locations and description](/assets/es/configuracion/periodos-de-cierre/formulario.png)
3. Fill in the fields:

| Field | Required | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Start date** | Yes | The first closed day. |
| **End date** | Yes | The last closed day. |
| **Select locations** | Yes | The locations that close on those dates. |
| **Short description (optional)** | No | Why you're closing, for example “Year-end vacation.” |

4. Tap **Save**.

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows when you tap **Save**, how the closure looks on the calendar and how an existing period is edited or deleted haven't been confirmed yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a closure block appointments that were already booked?**
The screen only says online bookings can't be made while the business is closed. What happens to appointments that already exist on those dates hasn't been confirmed yet. Check them on the calendar before closing.

**Only one barber is going on vacation.**
That's not a closure. Use that person's vacation. See [A barber's vacation](/ayuda/calendario/vacaciones-de-un-barbero).

**I'm only closing for one afternoon.**
A closure period works by days. For a few hours, block the calendar. See [Block the calendar](/ayuda/calendario/bloquear-el-calendario).
