# Client portal: the screen where your client sees, reschedules and cancels their appointments

**In short:** the **Client portal** card in **Settings** gives you what you need for your clients to manage their appointments on their own: they sign in with their phone number and a code, see their appointments and can reschedule or cancel the upcoming ones. You put it on your website with a snippet (**Inside a page** or **Open from a link**) or you share the **Portal link**.

## Where it is

**Settings › Account settings › Client portal**. The card says: “Put on your website the screen where your clients see, reschedule and cancel their appointments.”

![The Client portal screen with what your client can do and the first way to put it on your website](/assets/es/configuracion/portal-del-cliente/pantalla.png)

The screen explains it like this: “The portal is the screen where your client signs in with their phone, gets a code and from there sees their upcoming appointments, reschedules them or cancels them.”

## The blocks on the screen

| Block | What it's for | Example | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What your client can do** | What the client sees and does in the portal. | Laura moves her Thursday haircut to Friday without calling you. | [What it is and what your client can do](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/portal-del-cliente-que-es) |
| **Inside a page** | The portal as a section of your website. | A “My appointments” page on your site. | [Put it on your website](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/portal-del-cliente-en-tu-sitio) |
| **Open from a link** | The portal opens in a panel when the client taps a link of yours. | The “My appointments” link in your site's menu. | [Put it on your website](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/portal-del-cliente-en-tu-sitio) |
| **Direct link** | A link to the portal hosted by Barberlytics, without touching your website. | You send it by WhatsApp after each appointment. | [The portal's direct link](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/portal-del-cliente-enlace-directo) |

> [!NOTE]
> This screen has no **Save** button: you only copy snippets and links. The rules for how late an appointment can be canceled or rescheduled are in **Online booking**. The screen says so: “Rescheduling and canceling respect the minimum times you've set in Online booking.”

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a website to use the portal?**
No. You can share the **Portal link**. Before you do, read the warning in [The portal's direct link](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/portal-del-cliente-enlace-directo).

**Can I stop people from canceling at the last minute?**
Yes, from **Online booking › Online cancellation and rescheduling**. See [Cancel or reschedule online](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/cancelar-o-reprogramar-en-linea).
