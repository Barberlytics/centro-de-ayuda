# Credit card fee

**In short:** in **Settings › Credit card fee** you turn on a surcharge for when the client pays by credit card. You give it a name and decide whether it's a percentage of the total or a fixed amount.

## Where it is

**Settings › Sales › Credit card fee**. The card says: “Turn on and name the credit card usage fee and set it as a percentage or a fixed amount.”

The screen explains: “Here you can turn on the credit card usage fee, give it a custom name and set the amount. The fee can be set as a percentage of the total or as a fixed amount of money, whichever you prefer.”

![The credit card fee screen with the switch, the name and the rate](/assets/es/configuracion/tarifa-por-uso-de-tarjeta/pantalla.png)

## The fields

| Field | Required | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Enable this option** | — | The switch that turns the surcharge on or off. |
| **Name** | Yes | What the surcharge is called, as the client will see it, for example “Credit card surcharge.” |
| **Rate** | Yes | How much is charged: a percentage of the total or a fixed amount. |

## Steps

1. Turn on **Enable this option**.
2. Type the **Name**.
3. Type the **Rate** and choose whether it's a percentage or a fixed amount.
4. Tap **Save**.

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows when you tap **Save**, how you choose between a percentage and a fixed amount on the screen and how the surcharge looks at checkout and on the receipt haven't been confirmed yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it also charged on debit cards?**
The setting is called “Credit card fee.” Whether it applies to debit hasn't been confirmed yet.

**Is it legal to charge a card surcharge?**
It depends on your country. Check before you turn it on; Barberlytics only gives you the option.

**Is this a tax?**
No. Taxes go in **Settings › Taxes**. See [Taxes: how to add them and how they apply](/ayuda/configuracion/impuestos).
