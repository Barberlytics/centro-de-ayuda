# A day at the front desk

**In short:** the receptionist lives in the **Calendar**: you book, welcome the client, send them to the chair and charge. You also manage the **Clients** list. You don't see **Team**, **Expenses** or **Services**. This is a suggested order of work with what the role allows by default.

> [!NOTE]
> This guide comes from the permissions table of the **Receptionist - front desk** role and from the screens seen with the owner's account. We haven't yet signed in to the app with a receptionist account, so your menu may have small differences. Tell us if something doesn't match.

## How the app describes your role

“This role has permissions to register appointments and do check in and check out. It is usually assigned to the barbershop's secretaries.”

## When you open

1. **Open the Calendar.** One column per barber. With **Team\*** set to **Working**, you only see whoever has a schedule today.
2. **Go over the day in Agenda.** The view selector has **Agenda**, **Day** and **Month**. **Agenda** is the list of the day's appointments, one below the other ([See the day, the agenda or the month](/ayuda/calendario/ver-el-dia-la-agenda-o-el-mes)).
3. **Check the notifications.** The bell, at the top, with **All** and **Unread**. That's where notices like “Agenda: The client Carlos booked an appointment” arrive.

## When a client calls or writes

- **Book.** **Actions › Create appointment**: you choose **Client**, **Service**, **Barber** and **Date & Time** and tap **Create** ([Create an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/crear-una-cita)).
- **New client.** Inside **Client** there's the **Create client** button ([Create an appointment for a new client](/ayuda/calendario/crear-una-cita-para-un-cliente-nuevo)).
- **Find an appointment.** The magnifying glass at the top opens **Search** ([Search for an appointment with the magnifying glass](/ayuda/navegacion/buscar-una-cita)).
- **No opening today.** The calendar's settings button opens the **Waitlist** ([The waitlist](/ayuda/calendario/lista-de-espera)).

## When the client arrives

1. Tap the appointment card. Under **Actions** you'll find **Barber ready**, **Check in**, **Seated**, **Charge**, **No-show** and **Cancel**.
2. Tap **Check in** when they arrive ([Mark that the client has arrived](/ayuda/calendario/marcar-que-el-cliente-llego)).
3. Tap **Seated** when they move to the chair ([Move the client to the chair](/ayuda/calendario/pasar-al-cliente-a-la-silla)).
4. If they arrived without an appointment, create the appointment with the **Walk-in client** switch turned on ([A client who walks in without an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cliente-sin-cita-previa)).

## When they're done

1. Tap **Charge** under **Actions**, or **Pay appointment** in the summary. Checkout offers **Cash**, **Credit/Debit**, **Payment link** and **Split payment** ([Charge an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cobrar-una-cita)).
2. If they didn't show up, tap **No-show** ([The client didn't show up](/ayuda/calendario/el-cliente-no-se-presento)).

## At closing

Open **Actions › Payment list**. The **To collect** tab says “Here you'll find all your appointments pending payment”; **Paid**, “Here you'll find all your paid appointments.” Make sure the first one is empty.

## What your role allows by default

| You can | You can't |
|---|---|
| Book appointments and block the calendar (View, Create, Edit, Delete) | See **Team**, **Expenses**, **Services** or **Payroll** |
| View, create and edit **Clients** | Delete clients |
| View **Products** and their **Categories** | Create or edit products |
| View **Metrics** (Business key performance indicators) | Change **Permissions** |

If your owner adjusted your permissions, your screen may be different. The full table is in [What reception can do and see](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/permisos-recepcion).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I charge?**
By default you have full **Book appointments** and **Pricing** turned on. Which charge buttons you see with a receptionist account hasn't been checked on screen yet.

**I don't see Team in my menu.**
That's normal: the receptionist role doesn't include it. See [I can't see a menu section](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/no-veo-una-seccion-del-menu).

**Can I use the app from the front desk phone?**
Yes. The bar at the bottom has five buttons and **More** opens the full menu ([The menu on your phone](/ayuda/navegacion/el-menu-en-el-celular)).
