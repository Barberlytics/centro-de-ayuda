# A day as a location administrator

**In short:** the location administrator has almost everything the owner has within their location: schedule, clients, team, services, products, expenses, payroll and permissions. What you don't have by default is the **Barber payments section** and **Order barbers' calendars**. This is a suggested order of work with what your role allows.

> [!NOTE]
> This guide comes from the default permissions table of the **Location administrator** role and from the screens seen with the owner's account. We haven't yet signed in to the app with an administrator account, so what you see in your menu may have small differences. Tell us if something doesn't match.

## How the app describes your role

“This role has everything needed to manage a location. It is usually assigned to the location's administrator.”

## When you open

1. **Confirm the location.** At the top, the location name decides what you see. If you manage just one, leave it fixed.
2. **Look at the Calendar.** One column per barber, with the hours of the day. The **Team\*** filter set to **Working** shows only whoever has a schedule today.
3. **Check who's working.** In **Team › Working hours** you see each barber by day. If someone shows as “Not working” and they are coming in, tap their box and choose **Edit this day** ([The team's working hours](/ayuda/equipo/horas-de-trabajo-del-equipo)).
4. **Check the notifications.** The bell, at the top, has the **All** and **Unread** tabs.

## During the day

- **Book and serve.** In the **Calendar**, **Actions** has **Create appointment**, **Create quick sale**, **Walk-in sale** and **Payment list**. Your role has full **Book appointments** and **Block calendar**.
- **Charge.** Open the appointment and tap **Charge** under **Actions**, or **Pay appointment** in the summary ([Charge an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cobrar-una-cita)).
- **Clients.** You can view, create, edit and delete clients, and you have **Client information** and **Import clients** turned on.
- **A new barber at your location.** If the person already exists in the company, activate them in **Team › Actions › Activate team in this location** ([Activate the team in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-equipo-en-una-sucursal)). If they don't exist, create them with **Create team member**.
- **A service or product that doesn't show up.** Activate it in **Services › Actions › Activate services** or in **Products › Actions › Activate barbershop products**.

## At closing

1. **Make sure nothing is left to collect.** In **Calendar › Actions › Payment list**, the **To collect** tab says “Here you'll find all your appointments pending payment.”
2. **Record the day's expenses.** In **Expenses › Actions**, **Create variable expense** for one-off costs and **Create fixed expense** for what repeats.

## Every pay cycle

- Open **Payroll**, choose the location and the **Date range**. Your role has **Payroll** with View, Create and Edit.
- If a number doesn't add up, follow [Payroll doesn't match what I expect](/ayuda/nomina/la-nomina-no-coincide-con-lo-que-espero).

## What you don't have by default

| Feature | Who has it |
|---|---|
| **Barber payments section** | Only the owner |
| **Order barbers' calendars** | Only the owner |
| **Client download** | The owner and the rent barber |

If you need one, the owner can give it to you in your record, **Permissions** tab ([Change a person's permissions](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/cambiar-los-permisos-de-una-persona)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change my team's permissions?**
By default your role has **Permissions** with View and Edit. How they're changed is in [Change the default permissions of a role](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/cambiar-los-permisos-de-un-rol).

**Do I see the other locations?**
What you see depends on the locations where you're activated and on the selector at the top. See [What determines what you see](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/de-que-depende-lo-que-ves).

**Do I have to do all of this every day?**
No. It's an order so that nothing slips through. Take what's useful to you.
