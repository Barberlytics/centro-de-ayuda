# A day as a rent barber

**In short:** as a rent barber you pay the barbershop a **Rent base** for your chair, and the barbershop deducts a percentage for the clients it brought you. In the app you manage your schedule, your clients and your expenses. By default you don't see **Team**, **Services** or **Payroll**.

> [!NOTE]
> We haven't yet signed in to the app with a rent barber account. This guide comes from the default permissions table of the **Rent barber** role and from a rent barber's record seen with the owner's account. Tell us if your menu doesn't match.

## How the app describes your role

“This role is for team members who are rented a workspace.”

## When you open

1. **Open the Calendar.** Your appointments for the day are there. The calendar's **Actions** are **Create appointment**, **Create quick sale**, **Walk-in sale** and **Payment list**.
2. **Check the notifications.** The bell, at the top, with **All** and **Unread**.

## During the day

- **Book.** **Actions › Create appointment**: **Client**, **Service**, **Barber**, **Date & Time** and **Create** ([Create an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/crear-una-cita)).
- **Clients.** Your role has full **Clients** (View, Create, Edit, Delete), **Import clients** and **Client download**.
- **Block your time.** Your role has full **Block calendar**. From your name in the **Month** view, **Create time block** ([Block the calendar](/ayuda/calendario/bloquear-el-calendario)).
- **Vacations.** When a rent barber creates a vacation period, the Notification center can alert the owner (“When a rent barber creates a vacation period”).
- **Expenses.** You have full **Expenses**: **Actions › Create fixed expense** or **Create variable expense**.

## At closing

Open **Actions › Payment list** and check that no appointment of yours is left under **To collect**.

## How what you pay is calculated

Your record has the **Payments** tab with the **Payment logic**:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Rent base** | What you pay for the chair each cycle, for example $50,000.00 |
| **Walk-in appointment deduction** | A percentage the barbershop deducts for each client who arrived without an appointment |
| **New client deduction** | A percentage for each new client |
| **No-preference appointment deduction** | A percentage for each client who didn't ask for a barber |

These are the clients the barbershop brought you. In **Payroll**, the **Rent barber** tab adds up the **Fixed rent** column, and your **Net pay** can come out negative when the rent is more than what you generated ([My net pay is negative](/ayuda/nomina/mi-pago-neto-sale-negativo)).

## What your role allows by default

Only three blocks: **Bookings and clients**, **POS** (Prices) and, under **Business**, **Account settings** (View only) and **Expenses**. **Team**, **Metrics**, **Inventory**, **Services** and **Payroll** don't appear. The table is in [What the rent barber can do and see](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/permisos-barbero-de-renta).

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference from the commission barber?**
The commission barber is paid a percentage per service. You pay a base and get deductions for the barbershop's clients. See [Commission or rent barber](/ayuda/equipo/comision-o-renta).

**Where is my bank account?**
In your record, **Payments** tab, under **Bank information** (**Bank name** and **Account number**). The owner sees and edits it.

**Do I see Metrics?**
By default the rent role doesn't include **Business key performance indicators**. If your owner turned it on in your record, you do.
