# A day as a commission barber

**In short:** as a commission barber you see your own column in the **Calendar**, the **Clients** list, your numbers on **Home**, your location's **Team** and your **Expenses**. You're paid a percentage of each service, and that percentage changes depending on the type of client. This is the suggested order of work.

> [!NOTE]
> The menu and screens in this guide were seen with the **Barber comission** profile of the test account. What you can do inside each screen comes from the default permissions table; if your owner adjusted your permissions, it may change.

## Your menu

**Home · Calendar · Clients · Team · Expenses · Settings.** **Services & Products**, **Payroll** and **Transactions** don't appear. On your phone, the bar at the bottom has **Home**, **Calendar**, **Expenses**, **Clients** and **More**.

When you switch to this profile from your account icon, the app reloads and lands on the **Calendar**.

## When you open

1. **Look at your column.** In the **Calendar** only your column appears, with your appointments for the day. The **Actions** are the same: **Create appointment**, **Create quick sale**, **Walk-in sale** and **Payment list**.
2. **Look at your numbers.** **Home** shows three tabs, **Business**, **Clients** and **Barbers**, with your numbers, not the whole team's.
3. **Check the notifications.** The bell, at the top, with **All** and **Unread**.

## During the day

- **Book.** **Actions › Create appointment**, or tap an empty slot in your column: the panel opens with your name and the time already filled in ([Create an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/crear-una-cita)).
- **A client who arrives without an appointment.** Turn on **Walk-in client** when you create it. The screen explains it: “The client arrived without an appointment. The barber's walk-in commission applies and it shows in the booking reports.”
- **Clients.** Your role has **View** and **Create** in Clients. Phone numbers appear masked (`+57******2020`).
- **Block your time.** Your role has **Block calendar** with **View** only: by default you don't create blocks. If you need one, ask the owner.
- **Expenses.** You have full **Expenses**: **Actions › Create fixed expense** or **Create variable expense**.

## At closing

Open **Actions › Payment list** and check that none of your appointments is left under **To collect**.

## How you're paid

Your record has a **Compensation** tab with five percentages: **Basic commission**, **Returning client commission**, **Walk-in client commission**, **No-preference client commission** and **New client commission**. The one that applies depends on each appointment's client. In **Payroll**, the owner sees your **Net pay** per cycle ([Payroll for commission barbers](/ayuda/nomina/nomina-barberos-por-comision)).

## Your Settings

Only two settings: **Time format** (“…these changes will only be reflected in your calendar, not in the barbershop's calendar or your clients'”) and **Delete account**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why don't I see Payroll?**
The commission barber role doesn't include it. The owner sees your pay in **Payroll**.

**I'm an owner and I also cut hair. Do I have to sign out to see my schedule as a barber?**
No. From your account icon you switch to the **Barber comission** profile without signing in again ([I have two profiles](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/tienes-dos-perfiles)).

**Do I see the other barbers?**
In **Team**, yes: your location's list, with **Barbers**, **Levels** and **Working hours**. In the **Calendar**, only your column.
