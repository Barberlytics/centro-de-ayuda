# A day as an owner: what to check and in what order

**In short:** start the day in **Metrics**, continue with the **Calendar** while you serve clients and close with **Transactions**. From time to time, check **Payroll** and **Expenses**. This order is a suggestion: each section works on its own.

> [!NOTE]
> This guide is a suggested order of work. It only uses screens and buttons that already exist in Barberlytics. Each step links to the article that explains it.

## When you open

1. **Look at Metrics.** Open **Home** and leave the period on **Today**. The **Business** tab gives you the average ticket, sales and appointments for the day.
2. **Check the alerts.** In the **AI Insights** tab you'll find the **Client Risk Alerts** (Lost, At risk and Overdue) and the **Smart Recommendations**.
3. **Check the notifications.** Tap the bell at the top. It has the **All** and **Unread** tabs.
4. **Open the Calendar.** You see one column per barber, with the hours of the day. If a barber is missing, check their schedule in **Team › Working hours**.

## During the day

- In **Calendar**, the **Actions** button lets you create an appointment, a quick sale or a walk-in sale, and open the **Payment list**.
- In the **Payment list**, the **To collect** tab shows the appointments pending payment.
- If a client arrives without an appointment, see A client who arrives without an appointment.

## At closing

1. **Make sure nothing is left to collect.** Open **Transactions** and look at the **Billed**, **Collected** and **To collect** cards.
2. **Look for what's pending.** In **Transactions**, the **Bill status** filter has the **To collect** option.

## Every week or every two weeks

- **Clients:** open the **Nearly lost** and **Lost** tabs to see who you're losing.
- **Expenses:** check the **Fixed** and the **Variable**.
- **Payroll:** when the pay cycle ends, open **Payroll** and choose the date range. You can download it with **Download**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to do all of this every day?**
No. It's an order so that nothing slips through. Take what's useful to you and skip the rest.

**Where do I see how much I owe each barber?**
In **Payroll**. There's a tab for each type of pay: **Commission barber**, **Rent barber** and **Salary barber**.

**Can I see all my locations together?**
Yes. In the location selector at the top, choose **All locations**.
