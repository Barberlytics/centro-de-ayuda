# First steps: get your barbershop ready to book

**In short:** before you book your first appointment, get three things ready: your location, your services and your team. Then check the settings and, if you want, open online booking. This is the order we suggest.

> [!NOTE]
> This article tells you **where** each thing is and in what order it makes sense to do it. The full forms (creating an appointment, charging, creating a team member) aren't documented here yet: when you get to them, the screen guides you field by field.

## The big idea

Services, products and the team are **created once, for your whole company**, and then **activated at each location** where they're used. So if something doesn't show up at your location, it almost always needs to be activated there.

## Steps

1. **Check your location.** Go to **Settings › Locations**. There you see the list of locations with their team, country and city. If one is missing, tap **Create Location**. To check its details, open it with **Edit**: it has **Currency**, **Language** and **Time zone**.
2. **Set the time and the calendar.** Go to **Settings › Business details**. Under “Time and calendar settings” you'll find the **Time zone**, the **Time format**, the **Week start**, the **Start time** and the **End time**. Tap **Save**.
3. **Define how you charge.** In **Settings › Pricing** you choose the **Currency** and whether “Prices don't include taxes” or “Prices include taxes.” If you charge taxes, add them in **Settings › Taxes** with **Add tax**.
4. **Create your services.** Go to **Services**. Create the categories first and then each service. Tap **Actions** and choose **Create category** or **Create Service**. A service asks for **Service name**, **Base price** and **Category**.
5. **Create your products (if you sell).** Go to **Products**. Tap **Actions** and choose **Create Category** or **Create product**.
6. **Create your team.** Go to **Team**, tap **Actions** and choose **Create team member**. You choose each person's role. If you don't know whether your barber is a commission or a rent barber, see Commission or rent barber.
7. **Activate everything at your location.** Choose your location at the top, in the selector. Then:
   - In **Team**, tap **Actions › Activate team in this location**.
   - In **Services**, tap **Actions › Activate services**.
   - In **Products**, tap **Actions › Activate barbershop products**.
8. **Check the working hours.** In **Team**, open the **Working hours** tab. You see each barber by day and you can edit each box.
9. **Check the team's payment settings.** In **Settings › Payroll** you choose the **Pay cycle** and the **Cycle start day**. In **Settings › Commissions** you decide what gets deducted before the commission is calculated.
10. **Book your first appointment.** Go to **Calendar**, tap **Actions** and choose **Create appointment**.
11. **Charge.** Appointments pending payment are in **Calendar › Actions › Payment list**, in the **To collect** tab.

> [!TIP]
> If you're going to take bookings from your clients online, open **Settings › Online booking** next. Each service has a **Private / Public** switch that decides whether it's visible in online booking.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to create everything again for each location?**
No. The team, services and products are created once. At each location you only activate them.

**I don't see my barber on the calendar. What do I do?**
Check that they're activated at your location: **Team › Actions › Activate team in this location**.

**Can I change these settings later?**
Yes. They all live in **Settings**, in **Services**, in **Products** and in **Team**, and you can go back to them whenever you want.

**What if I'm only a barber and not an owner?**
This order is for the owner. The other profiles see fewer sections: what you see depends on your role.
