# What Barberlytics is and what you can do with it

**In short:** Barberlytics is the app you use to run your barbershop from a single screen: the schedule, the clients, the team, what you charge and what you pay each barber. You sign in with your phone and a code, with no password. Each section of the menu takes care of one part of the day.

## The sections, one by one

These are the sections of an owner's menu, with the phrase each one uses to introduce itself.

![Barberlytics' side menu with all its sections](/assets/es/navegacion/las-secciones-de-barberlytics/menu.png)

| Section | What you do there | How it introduces itself |
|---|---|---|
| **Metrics** (Home) | See how the barbershop is doing: sales, appointments, clients, barbers, marketing and recommendations | “In this section you can have full control of your team” |
| **Calendar** | Book, move and charge appointments; quick sales; the payment list | One column per barber, with the hours of the day |
| **Clients** | The client list, their type (New, VIP, Lost…), their record, their notes and their appointments | “In this section you can manage your clients.” |
| **Services** | Categories, services and combos, with their prices | “In this section you can have full control of your services.” |
| **Products** | What you sell, with its price and its categories | “In this section you can have full control of your products.” |
| **Team** | Barbers, levels, working hours and managers | “In this section you can have full control of your team.” |
| **Expenses** | The barbershop's fixed and variable expenses | “Here you can keep control of all your expenses, whether they're recurring or one-off.” |
| **Payroll** | How much each barber is owed in the pay cycle | “In this section you can have traceability and control of your barbershop.” |
| **Transactions** | Everything charged, pending and refunded | Five cards and a table with each account |
| **Settings** | Every barbershop setting | Four groups with one card per setting |

The details of each one are in [The Barberlytics sections](/ayuda/navegacion/las-secciones-de-barberlytics).

## What you can do, in three sentences

1. **Fill the schedule.** You create appointments from the **Calendar** and your clients can book on their own online: **Settings › Online booking** says “Adjust how clients can book and manage their appointments online.”
   ![The calendar with one column per barber](/assets/es/calendario/conoce-el-calendario/pantalla.png)
2. **Charge and know how much came in.** Each appointment is charged from the calendar and ends up in **Transactions**. In **Metrics › Business** you see the average ticket, sales and income for the day, the week or the month.
   ![The Business tab in Metrics](/assets/es/metricas/como-leer-metricas/negocio.png)
3. **Pay your team well.** Each barber is either **commission** or **rent**. **Payroll** calculates what's owed to each one based on the cycle you define in **Settings › Payroll**: “Define the pay cycle and set the start and end dates.”

## How it's organized behind the scenes

- You have **one company** and one or more **locations**. Each location has its own currency, time zone and language.
- The team, services and products are created **once** and **activated** at each location where they're used.
- Each person signs in with a **role**: owner, location administrator, receptionist, commission barber or rent barber. The role decides which sections they see.

This is covered in [How your barbershop is organized](/ayuda/sucursales/como-se-organiza-tu-barberia) and [The five roles](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/los-cinco-roles).

## Settings: the ones that exist

![The Settings index with its four groups](/assets/es/configuracion/conoce-configuracion/indice.png)

The four groups are **Account settings** (Business details, Billing details and invoices, Online booking, Business closure periods, Payment devices, Locations, Company), **Team** (Permissions, Payroll, Commissions), **Sales** (Taxes, Credit card usage fee, Tips, Prices, Payment integration) and **Notifications** (Notification center). They're described in [Get to know Settings](/ayuda/configuracion/conoce-configuracion).

## Frequently asked questions

**Does it work from my phone?**
Yes. On a narrow screen the menu moves to a bar at the bottom with five buttons. See [Use Barberlytics from your phone](/ayuda/empezar/usar-barberlytics-desde-el-celular).

**Where do I start?**
With [First steps](/ayuda/empezar/primeros-pasos-del-dueno): location, services, team and settings, in that order.

**Do my clients have to install anything?**
No. They book from a link or from your website, with whatever you set up in **Settings › Online booking**.
