# Use Barberlytics from your phone

**In short:** on a narrow screen, like a phone's, the menu on the left moves to a bar at the bottom with five buttons. The last one, **More**, opens the full menu.

## The bottom bar

The buttons depend on your profile.

| Profile | Buttons in the bar |
|---|---|
| Owner (**Business**) | **Home**, **Calendar**, **Team**, **Clients** and **More** |
| Commission barber (**Barber comission**) | **Home**, **Calendar**, **Expenses**, **Clients** and **More** |

**Home** takes you to Metrics.

## How to get to everything else

1. Tap **More**.
2. The full menu for your profile opens.
3. Tap the section you're looking for.

## What changes depending on your profile

The menu is built from your profile's permissions. If your profile doesn't have a section, you don't see it in the bar or in the full menu. That's why a barber's bar isn't the same as an owner's.

## Frequently asked questions

**I can't find Settings in the bottom bar. Where is it?**
Tap **More**. The full menu opens there.

**Why does my barber see a different bar from mine?**
Because each profile only sees what its permissions allow. As the owner, you see more sections than a commission barber.

**I have two profiles, owner and barber. Does the bar change?**
Yes. When you switch profiles from your account icon, the app reloads and the bar becomes the one for the profile you chose.
