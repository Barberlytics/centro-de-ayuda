# Delete a team member

**In short:** on the person's row, tap **Actions** › **Delete Commission barber** (or **Delete Rent barber**). It is the permanent option. If they just stopped working with you, use **Deactivate**.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting can't be undone. Before you delete, think about whether deactivating is enough.

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Under **Barbers**, choose the **Commission barber** or **Rent barber** view.
3. On the person's row, tap **Actions** › **Delete Commission barber** (or **Delete Rent barber**).
   ![The Actions menu of a row with the Delete option](/assets/es/equipo/la-lista-de-barberos/acciones-fila.png)

> [!NOTE]
> What happens after you tap **Delete** hasn't been checked yet: whether it asks you to confirm, what happens to their appointments, history and payroll, and whether you can delete someone with future appointments. It will be completed with a test barber.

## Delete or deactivate

| | **Deactivate** | **Delete** |
|---|---|---|
| What it's for | Someone who stops working with you | Removing the person |
| Can be undone | Still to confirm | No |
| Where it is | In the same **Actions** menu on the row | In the same **Actions** menu on the row |

See [Deactivate a team member](/ayuda/equipo/inactivar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo).

## Who can delete

With the factory permissions, the owner and the location administrator have **Delete** in **Team**. In the **Managers** tab the **Delete** option wasn't seen on the rows: an owner can be **Deactivated**, not deleted.

## Frequently asked questions

**I don't see Delete.**
Check your permissions in **Team**. See [I can't edit or delete](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/no-puedo-editar-o-borrar). In **Managers** the option doesn't appear.

**Do I lose what they sold?**
It hasn't been checked yet. Until it's confirmed, deactivate instead of deleting if you need to keep their history.

**Can I delete an owner?**
In the **Managers** list only **Deactivate Owner** was seen, not **Delete Owner**.
