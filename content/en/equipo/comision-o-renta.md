# Commission barber or rent barber: which to choose

**In short:** you pay a **commission** barber a percentage of each service. A **rent** barber rents the chair from you: they pay a base amount and you deduct a percentage for the clients the barbershop brings them. You choose when you create them, in **Create team member**, and it determines which money tab their record has and which **Payroll** tab they appear in.

## How the app describes them

When you tap **Actions** › **Create team member** you see one card per role:

- **Commission barber**: “This role is for barbers who are paid commissions.”
- **Rent barber**: “This role is for team members who are rented a workspace.”

![The role cards in Create team member, with Commission barber selected](/assets/es/equipo/crear-barbero-por-comision/roles.png)

## What's different between the two

| | Commission barber | Rent barber |
|---|---|---|
| **How they get paid** | A percentage per service, different depending on the type of client: **Basic commission**, **Returning client commission**, **Walk-in client commission**, **No-preference client commission** and **New client commission**. Plus compensation for product sales. | They pay a **Rent base**. The barbershop deducts a percentage for each **walk-in appointment**, for **new clients** and for **no-preference appointments**: the clients the barbershop brought them. |
| **Money tab in their record** | **Compensation** | **Payments**, with the **Bank information** (bank and account number) |
| **In the barber list** | **Commission barber** view | **Rent barber** view, with the **Bank account** column |
| **In Payroll** | **Commission barber** tab | **Rent barber** tab, with the **Fixed rent** column |
| **Factory permissions** | Books appointments, views and creates clients, views the team and records expenses | Only Bookings and clients, POS, and under Business: Account settings and Expenses |

![The Payments tab of a rent barber, with the Payment logic and the Bank information](/assets/es/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-de-renta-pagos/ver.png)

## How to decide

- If you bring in the clients, provide the products and set the price, and the barber takes a share of each cut: **commission**.
- If the barber brings their own clientele, collects their own money and pays you for the spot: **rent**. The deductions let you charge them for the clients who weren't theirs.
- If you pay a fixed salary, the commission barber's record lets you choose **Salary** as the **Compensation type**, and **Payroll** has a **Salary barber** tab. See [How each type of barber gets paid](/ayuda/equipo/como-se-le-paga-a-un-barbero).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You choose the role when you create the person. If you get it wrong, there's no button to switch from commission to rent: you'd have to create them again with the other role.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I have both types of barbers at the same location?**
Yes. The **Barbers** list separates them into two views and **Payroll** into two tabs.

**Does the rent barber see the same things as the commission barber in the app?**
No. With the factory permissions the rent barber sees less: they don't see **Team**. See [Permissions table: what each role can do](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol).

**Where do I change the percentages later?**
In the person's record: **Edit** › **Compensation** tab (commission) or **Payments** tab (rent).
