# Get to know the Team section

**In short:** in **Team** you manage the people who work at your location: barbers, levels, schedules and managers. Everything that gets created or sorted comes from the **Actions** button. The team is one team for your whole barbershop and is activated at each location where it works.

## How to get there

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. The screen says: “In this section you can have full control of your team.” At the top are the four tabs and, on the right, the **Actions** button.
   ![The Team section with the Barbers, Levels, Working hours and Managers tabs and the Actions button](/assets/es/equipo/conoce-la-seccion-equipo/pantalla.png)

## The four tabs

| Tab | What's in it | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **Barbers** | The **Barber list**, with two views: **Commission barber** and **Rent barber**. From each row you open the record, edit, deactivate or delete. | [The barber list](/ayuda/equipo/la-lista-de-barberos) |
| **Levels** | The **Level list**: the name of each level (for example, “Senior”) and how many barbers have it. | [Barber levels](/ayuda/equipo/niveles-de-barberos) |
| **Working hours** | A grid of barber by day with each person's shift (“6am - 11pm”) or “Not working.” You move through it by week and each shift can be edited. | [The team's working hours](/ayuda/equipo/horas-de-trabajo-del-equipo) |
| **Managers** | The **Member list**: owners and administrators with their role. | [Managers](/ayuda/equipo/gerentes) |

## The Actions button

![The Team Actions menu with its five options](/assets/es/equipo/conoce-la-seccion-equipo/menu-acciones.png)

| Option | What it's for | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **Create team member** | Add someone new: you choose one of the five roles and fill in their record. | [Create a commission barber](/ayuda/equipo/crear-barbero-por-comision) |
| **Create level** | Add a level, like “Senior” or “Master.” | [Create and edit a level](/ayuda/equipo/crear-y-editar-un-nivel) |
| **Sort barbers** | Choose the order the calendar columns appear in. | [Sort the barbers](/ayuda/equipo/ordenar-barberos) |
| **Order for walk-in and no-preference booking** | Decide which barbers take clients who come in without an appointment or without a preference, and in what order. | [Order for walk-in clients](/ayuda/equipo/orden-para-clientes-sin-cita) |
| **Activate team in this location** | Turn on the people who work at the location you're in. | [Activate the team in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-equipo-en-una-sucursal) |

## You only see people who are active at your location

Your team belongs to the whole barbershop, but **Team** shows only the people who are active at the location you chose at the top. If you open a new location, the **Barbers** tab says “0 Barbers” and “Here you'll see your team's barbers,” with the **Create commission barber** button.

To bring in someone who already exists in your barbershop:

1. Tap **Actions** › **Activate team in this location**.
   ![The Activate team in this location screen with a switch for each person](/assets/es/sucursales/activar-equipo-en-una-sucursal/pantalla.png)
2. Choose **Barbers** or **Managers** and, within Barbers, **Commission** or **Rent**.
3. Turn on the switch for each person and tap **Save**.

The same person can be active at several locations. It's explained in [The team belongs to the company and is activated in each location](/ayuda/sucursales/equipo-compartido-entre-sucursales).

## Who sees what

- The **owner** and the **location administrator** see all four tabs and all the actions. Only the owner has **Sort barbers**.
- With the **commission barber** profile the section has three tabs: **Barbers**, **Levels** and **Working hours**. **Managers** doesn't appear.
- The **receptionist** and the **rent barber** don't see **Team** with the factory permissions. See [Permissions table: what each role can do](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol).

## Frequently asked questions

**I hired a new barber. Where do I start?**
Tap **Actions** › **Create team member**, choose **Commission barber** or **Rent barber** and tap **Create**. If you don't know which one yet, see [Commission barber or rent barber: which to choose](/ayuda/equipo/comision-o-renta).

**I don't see a barber I know works with me.**
Most of the time they're not activated at this location. Go to **Actions** › **Activate team in this location** and turn on their switch.

**Can a barber work at two locations?**
Yes. It's one person, activated at both.
