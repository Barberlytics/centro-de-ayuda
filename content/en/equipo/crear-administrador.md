# Create a location administrator

**In short:** in **Team** › **Actions** › **Create team member**, select **Location administrator**, tap **Create**, fill in **Profile** and review **Permissions**. It's the role for the person who runs a location without being an owner.

## Before you start

The app describes the role like this: “This role has everything needed to manage a location. It's usually assigned to the location's administrator.” With the factory permissions they can do almost everything the owner can, except the **Barber payments section** and **Order barbers' calendars**. See [Location administrator permissions](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/permisos-administrador-de-la-locacion).

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Create team member**.
3. Select the **Location administrator** card and tap **Create**.
   ![The role cards in Create team member](/assets/es/equipo/crear-barbero-por-comision/roles.png)
4. Fill in the **Profile** tab.
   ![The Profile form for a location administrator](/assets/es/equipo/crear-administrador/perfil.png)

   | Field | Required | What it is |
   |---|---|---|
   | **First name\*** | Yes | Their first name, for example Laura. |
   | **Last name\*** | Yes | Their last name. |
   | **Phone\*** | Yes | Their phone number. |
   | **Email\*** | Yes | Their email. |
   | **This person is a barber** | No | Switch. Turn it on if, besides managing, they serve clients. |

5. Tap **Continue**. The **Permissions** tab becomes available.
6. In **Permissions** review the matrix. It comes with the role's factory settings and you can adjust it for this person.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens when you finish the form, how the person gets their access and what changes when you turn on **This person is a barber** hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed when a test administrator is created.

## After you create them

They appear in the **Managers** tab of **Team**. If they work at more than one location, activate them at each one with **Actions** › **Activate team in this location**, **Managers** tab.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between an administrator and an owner?**
The owner has full access. The administrator has what's needed to run a location, and their permissions can be adjusted. See [The five roles](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/los-cinco-roles).

**My administrator also cuts hair.**
Turn on **This person is a barber** in their **Profile**. What exactly that switch adds hasn't been checked yet.

**Can I change their permissions later?**
Yes, from **Managers** › **Actions** › **Edit**, in the **Permissions** tab.
