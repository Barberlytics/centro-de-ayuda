# Create a rent barber

**In short:** in **Team** › **Actions** › **Create team member**, select **Rent barber** and tap **Create**. The form is the same as the commission barber's, except for the money tab: instead of **Compensation** it has **Payments**, with the rent base, the deductions and the bank account.

## Before you start

- The app describes the role like this: “This role is for team members who are rented a workspace.” If you pay them a percentage per service, see [Create a commission barber](/ayuda/equipo/crear-barbero-por-comision).
- Have their phone number, email, level, how much rent they pay and their bank account handy.

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Create team member**.
3. Select the **Rent barber** card and tap **Create**.
   ![The role cards in Create team member](/assets/es/equipo/crear-barbero-por-comision/roles.png)
4. Fill in the **Profile** tab of the **Create Rent barber** form.
   ![The Profile form when creating a rent barber](/assets/es/equipo/crear-barbero-de-renta/perfil.png)

   | Field | Required | What it is |
   |---|---|---|
   | Photo | No | The barber's photo. |
   | **First name\*** | Yes | Their first name, for example Carlos. |
   | **Last name\*** | Yes | Their last name. |
   | **Phone\*** | Yes | Their phone number. |
   | **Email\*** | Yes | Their email. |
   | **Description** | No | A text about the barber. |
   | **Visibility for booking** | No | Switch. See [Visibility for booking](/ayuda/equipo/visibilidad-para-agendamiento). |
   | **Barber level\*** | Yes | Their level, for example “Senior.” |
   | **Skills** | No | What they know how to do. |
   | **Gallery - 0/5** | No | Up to five photos of their work. |

5. Tap **Continue**. The next tab becomes available.
6. Continue with **Services**, **Payments**, **Calendar**, **Permissions** and **Options**. In **Payments** go the **Rent base**, the three deductions and the **Bank information**. It's explained in [A rent barber's record: payments](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-de-renta-pagos).

If you want to leave without creating anyone, tap **Cancel**.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens when you tap **Continue** on each tab, how the photo and the gallery are uploaded, and how the barber gets their access hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed when a test rent barber is created.

## After you create them

The barber appears in **Team** › **Barbers**, **Rent barber** view, with their **Bank account** and **Active** status. In **Payroll** they show up in the **Rent barber** tab. If they work at another location, activate them there with **Actions** › **Activate team in this location**.

## Frequently asked questions

**What are the deductions?**
A percentage the barbershop deducts for walk-in appointments, new clients and no-preference appointments: the clients the barbershop brought them. See [How each type of barber gets paid](/ayuda/equipo/como-se-le-paga-a-un-barbero).

**Does the rent barber see the whole team?**
No. With the factory permissions they only have Bookings and clients, POS, and under Business: Account settings and Expenses. See [Rent barber permissions](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/permisos-barbero-de-renta).

**Can I change them to commission later?**
There's no button to change the role. You'd have to create them again as a commission barber.
