# Create a commission barber

**In short:** in **Team** › **Actions** › **Create team member**, leave **Commission barber** selected and tap **Create**. You fill in **Profile** and tap **Continue**; each tab that follows (**Services**, **Compensation**, **Calendar**, **Permissions**, **Options**) becomes available when you complete the previous one.

## Before you start

- The app describes the role like this: “This role is for barbers who are paid commissions.” If the barber rents the chair from you, see [Create a rent barber](/ayuda/equipo/crear-barbero-de-renta).
- Have their phone number, email and the level you're going to give them handy. If the level doesn't exist, create it first with **Actions** › **Create level**.

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Create team member**.
3. A panel opens with one card per role. **Commission barber** is already selected. Tap **Create**.
   ![The role cards in Create team member, with Commission barber selected](/assets/es/equipo/crear-barbero-por-comision/roles.png)
4. Fill in the **Profile** tab of the **Create Commission barber** form.
   ![The Profile form when creating a commission barber](/assets/es/equipo/crear-barbero-por-comision/perfil.png)

   | Field | Required | What it is |
   |---|---|---|
   | Photo | No | The barber's photo. |
   | **First name\*** | Yes | Their first name, for example Carlos. |
   | **Last name\*** | Yes | Their last name. |
   | **Phone\*** | Yes | Their phone number. |
   | **Email\*** | Yes | Their email. |
   | **Description** | No | A text about the barber. |
   | **Visibility for booking** | No | Switch. See [Visibility for booking](/ayuda/equipo/visibilidad-para-agendamiento). |
   | **Barber level\*** | Yes | Their level, for example “Senior.” See [Barber levels](/ayuda/equipo/niveles-de-barberos). |
   | **Skills** | No | What they know how to do. |
   | **Gallery - 0/5** | No | Up to five photos of their work. |

5. Tap **Continue**. The next tab becomes available.
6. Continue with **Services**, **Compensation**, **Calendar**, **Permissions** and **Options**. What each one holds is covered in the record articles:
   - [Own services and prices](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-servicios)
   - [Compensation](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-compensacion): the five commission percentages.
   - [Their weekly schedule](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-calendario)
   - [Permissions](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-permisos)
   - [Options](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-opciones)

If you want to leave without creating anyone, tap **Cancel**.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens when you tap **Continue** on each tab, how the photo and the gallery are uploaded, and how the barber gets their access hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed when a test barber is created.

## After you create them

The barber appears in **Team** › **Barbers**, **Commission barber** view, with **Active** status. If they work at another location, activate them there with **Actions** › **Activate team in this location**. To show up in the calendar they need a schedule: check their **Calendar** tab and [The team's working hours](/ayuda/equipo/horas-de-trabajo-del-equipo).

## Frequently asked questions

**What percentage should I set?**
You decide in the **Compensation** tab, with one percentage per type of client. See [How each type of barber gets paid](/ayuda/equipo/como-se-le-paga-a-un-barbero).

**Do I have to assign services one by one?**
In the **Services** tab you see each service with its price and its time. Empty means it uses what's in the catalog. See [Own services and prices](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-servicios).

**I created them and they don't show up in the calendar.**
Check that they're activated at that location, that they have a schedule that day and that **Visibility for booking** is on. See [A barber doesn't show up on the calendar](/ayuda/calendario/un-barbero-no-aparece-en-el-calendario).
