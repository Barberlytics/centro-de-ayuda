# Create an owner

**In short:** in **Team** › **Actions** › **Create team member**, select **Owner** and tap **Create**. An owner has access to everything: the app says “Owner accounts have full access to the system.” Use it only for a partner or someone you trust completely.

## Before you start

- The app describes the role like this: “This role has all the permissions normally granted to the barbershop's owner.”
- If the person is only going to run one location, create a [location administrator](/ayuda/equipo/crear-administrador) instead.
- If the owner also serves clients, they can later have a second barber profile. In **Managers** it appears as **Owner - Commission barber**.

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Create team member**.
3. Select the **Owner** card and tap **Create**.
   ![The role cards in Create team member](/assets/es/equipo/crear-barbero-por-comision/roles.png)
4. Fill in the **Profile** tab of the **Create Owner** form.
   ![The Profile form when creating an owner, with the Profile and Permissions tabs](/assets/es/equipo/crear-dueno/perfil.png)

   | Field | Required | What it is |
   |---|---|---|
   | Photo | No | The person's photo. |
   | **First name\*** | Yes | Their first name, for example Laura. |
   | **Last name\*** | Yes | Their last name. |
   | **Phone\*** | Yes | Their phone number, with the country. |
   | **Email\*** | Yes | Their email. |
   | **This person is a barber** | No | Switch. Turn it on if the owner also serves clients. |

5. Tap **Continue**. The **Permissions** tab becomes available.

If you want to leave without creating anyone, tap **Cancel**.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens after **Continue**, what **Permissions** shows for an owner (the app says owners have full access), what changes when you turn on **This person is a barber** and how the person gets their access hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed when a test owner is created.

## After you create them

The new owner appears in the **Managers** tab of **Team**, with the **Owner** role. If you have several locations, activate them at each one with **Actions** › **Activate team in this location**, **Managers** tab. See [Managers](/ayuda/equipo/gerentes).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I limit what an owner does?**
Not with the factory permissions: the app says owner accounts have full access. If you need to limit someone, give them the location administrator role.

**I want my partner to cut hair too. Do I create them twice?**
No. Turn on **This person is a barber** in their **Profile**. The same person can have two profiles, owner and commission barber, and switch between them without signing in again. See [Switch roles](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/cambiar-de-rol).

**How many owners can I have?**
The app doesn't show a limit. In the test account there are several owners in the **Managers** list.
