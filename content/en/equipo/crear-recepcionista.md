# Create a receptionist

**In short:** in **Team** › **Actions** › **Create team member**, select **Receptionist**, tap **Create** and fill in **Profile**: first name, last name, phone and email. Then review **Permissions**. It's the role for whoever books appointments and welcomes clients at the front desk.

## Before you start

The app describes the role like this: “This role has permission to record appointments and check clients in and out. It's usually assigned to the barbershop's secretaries.” With the factory permissions they handle appointments and clients, see products and metrics, but don't see **Team** or **Expenses**. See [Receptionist permissions](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/permisos-recepcion).

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Create team member**.
3. Select the **Receptionist** card and tap **Create**.
   ![The role cards in Create team member](/assets/es/equipo/crear-barbero-por-comision/roles.png)
4. Fill in the **Profile** tab.
   ![The Profile form for a receptionist](/assets/es/equipo/crear-recepcionista/perfil.png)

   | Field | Required | What it is |
   |---|---|---|
   | **First name\*** | Yes | Their first name, for example Laura. |
   | **Last name\*** | Yes | Their last name. |
   | **Phone\*** | Yes | Their phone number. |
   | **Email\*** | Yes | Their email. |

5. Tap **Continue**. The **Permissions** tab becomes available.
6. In **Permissions** review the matrix. It comes with the role's factory settings and you can adjust it for this person.

If you'd rather leave without creating anyone, tap **Cancel**.

> What happens when you finish the form and how the person gets their access hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed when a test receptionist is created.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the receptionist take payments?**
With the factory permissions they have full **Book appointments** and the **POS** block. If you want to give them only what they need, see [Give reception only what it needs](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/darle-a-recepcion-solo-lo-necesario).

**Where do I see them after I create them?**
In **Team** › **Managers**, in the **Member list**. Exactly which role that list shows for a receptionist hasn't been checked yet.

**Can they see payroll or expenses?**
Not with the factory permissions. If they need to, adjust it in their **Permissions** tab.
