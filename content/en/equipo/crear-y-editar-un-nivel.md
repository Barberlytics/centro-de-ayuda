# Create and edit a level

**In short:** in **Team** › **Actions** › **Create level**, type the **Level name\*** and tap **Create**. To rename it or delete it, go to the **Levels** tab and use **Edit** or **Delete** on its row.

## Create a level

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Create level**.
3. The **Create level** panel opens.
   ![The Create level panel with the Level name field and the Create button](/assets/es/equipo/crear-y-editar-un-nivel/crear.png)

   | Field | Required | What it is |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Level name\*** | Yes | The name of the level, for example “Senior.” |

4. Tap **Create**.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens after you tap **Create** hasn't been checked yet: whether the panel closes and the level shows up right away in the **Level list** and in the **Barber level\*** field. It will be completed with a test level.

## Edit or delete a level

1. Tap the **Levels** tab.
2. On the level's row, tap **Edit** to change the name or **Delete** to remove it.

> [!NOTE]
> The **Edit** form and what happens when you **Delete** a level that has barbers hasn't been checked yet.

## Assign the level to a barber

The level is chosen in each barber's record, under **Profile** › **Barber level\***, when you create or edit them. See [Edit a team member](/ayuda/equipo/editar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo).

## Frequently asked questions

**I created the level and it doesn't show up when I create a barber.**
Close the barber form and open it again so it loads the level list again. If it still doesn't show up, check in the **Levels** tab that it was created.

**What happens to the barbers if I delete their level?**
It hasn't been checked yet. Before you delete, move them to another level from their record.

**Can I have two levels with the same name?**
The app doesn't show a rule about it. Avoid it: the **Level** filter couldn't tell them apart.
