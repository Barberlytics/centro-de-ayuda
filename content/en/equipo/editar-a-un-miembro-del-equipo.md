# Edit a team member

**In short:** on the person's row tap **Actions** › **Edit Commission barber** (or **Edit Rent barber**, **Edit Owner**...). Their record opens with the same tabs as when you created them, each with its own **Save** button.

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Find the person under **Barbers** (**Commission barber** or **Rent barber** view) or under **Managers**.
3. On their row, tap **Actions** › **Edit Commission barber** (or the **Edit** option for their role).
   ![The Profile tab of a barber in edit mode](/assets/es/equipo/editar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo/perfil.png)
4. Go to the tab you want to change and edit the fields.
5. Tap **Save** on that tab.

You can also open the record with **View** and use the record's **Actions** button to switch to editing.

## What you can change in each tab

| Tab | What you change | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **Profile** | Photo, **First name\***, **Last name\***, **Phone\***, **Email\***, **Description**, **Visibility for booking**, **Barber level\***, **Skills** and **Gallery - 0/5**. | [Profile](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-perfil) |
| **Services** | Price, time and commission per service for this person. | [Own services and prices](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-servicios) |
| **Compensation** (commission) | **Compensation type\*** and the five percentages. | [Compensation](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-compensacion) |
| **Payments** (rent) | **Rent base**, deductions and **Bank information**. | [Payments](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-de-renta-pagos) |
| **Calendar** | Checkbox per day, **Start time** and **Closing time**. | [Their weekly schedule](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-calendario) |
| **Permissions** | That person's matrix. | [Permissions](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-permisos) |
| **Options** | Tips, multiple appointments, new clients, cancellations, interval and reminders. | [Options](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-opciones) |

For an owner, an administrator or a receptionist, **Edit** opens **Profile** and **Permissions**.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens when you tap **Save** on each tab, and whether the change applies right away to a person who's already in the app, hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed with a test barber.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change a barber from commission to rent?**
There's no field for that when you edit. The role is chosen when you create the person.

**I changed their schedule and the calendar is still the same.**
Check that you tapped **Save** in the **Calendar** tab. If it's only for this week, edit the day in **Team** › **Working hours**.

**Who can edit?**
With the factory permissions, the owner and the location administrator have **Edit** in **Team**. The commission barber only has **View**.
