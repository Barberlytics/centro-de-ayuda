# A rent barber's record: payments

**In short:** a rent barber doesn't have **Compensation** but **Payments**. That's where the **Payment logic** is (the **Rent base** and three deductions as percentages) and the **Bank information** (bank and account number).

## How to get there

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Under **Barbers**, tap the **Rent barber** view.
3. On the person's row, tap **Actions** › **View Rent barber**.
4. Tap the **Payments** tab.
   ![The Payments tab of a rent barber, with the Payment logic and the Bank information](/assets/es/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-de-renta-pagos/ver.png)

## Payment logic

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Rent base** | What the barber pays for their spot each period, for example $400,000.00. In **Payroll** it appears as **Fixed rent**. |
| **Walk-in appointment deduction** | Percentage the barbershop deducts for each client who came in without an appointment, in %. |
| **New client deduction** | Percentage it deducts for each client who comes in for the first time, in %. |
| **No-preference appointment deduction** | Percentage it deducts for each client who didn't ask for a barber, in %. |

The idea: the rent barber brings their own clientele and keeps what they earn. When the barbershop was the one who brought in the client (they came in without an appointment, are new or didn't ask for anyone), the barbershop keeps a percentage of that service.

## Bank information

**Bank name** and **Account number**. It's the account shown in the **Bank account** column of the rent barber list.

> [!WARNING]
> Bank details are sensitive. Only people with access to **Team** can see them. Don't copy them outside the app.

## Where you see the result

In **Payroll**, **Rent barber** tab, with the **Fixed rent** column and the **Net pay**. If the rent is more than what they generated, the net pay comes out negative: that's what the barber owes the barbershop. See [How payroll works](/ayuda/nomina/como-funciona-la-nomina).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I set different deductions for each rent barber?**
Yes. The percentages belong to each person, in their **Payments** tab.

**How do I change the base or the deductions?**
In the list, **Actions** › **Edit Rent barber**, **Payments** tab. See [Edit a team member](/ayuda/equipo/editar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo).

**Is anything deducted from their product commission?**
In **Payroll** the **Rent barber** tab also has **Product sales** and **Product commission**. How that commission is set up for a rent barber isn't documented yet.
