# A barber's record: profile

**In short:** the record gathers everything about a barber in six tabs: **Profile**, **Services**, **Compensation** (or **Payments** if they're a rent barber), **Calendar**, **Permissions** and **Options**. You open it from the list with **View**. The **Profile** tab holds their photo, name, type and level, their **Personal information** and their **Details**.

## How to open the record

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Under **Barbers**, choose the **Commission barber** or **Rent barber** view.
3. On the person's row, tap **Actions** › **View Commission barber** (or **View Rent barber**).
   ![The Profile tab of a commission barber's record](/assets/es/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-perfil/ver.png)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The record only loads if you enter from the list. If you type its address in the browser, you'll see an empty record.

At the top right is the record's **Actions** button, to edit or deactivate the person without going back to the list.

## What's in the Profile tab

It's the same for the commission barber and the rent barber.

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Header | The photo, the name and, below, their type and level: “Commission barber \| Senior” (“-” if they have no level). |
| **Personal information** | **Phone number**, **Email** and **Code**. |
| **Details** | **Visibility for booking** (**Enabled** or **Disabled**) and **Skills** (“--” if they have none). |

- **Visibility for booking** decides whether the barber can be booked. See [Visibility for booking](/ayuda/equipo/visibilidad-para-agendamiento).
- The **Code** is sensitive information about the person. Don't share it. See [A barber's code](/ayuda/equipo/codigo-de-un-barbero).
- The **Description**, the **Skills** and the photo gallery are filled in when you create or edit, in the **Profile** tab. See [A barber's skills and photos](/ayuda/equipo/habilidades-y-fotos).

## To change something

From the record, tap **Actions** › **Edit**, or go back to the list and choose **Edit Commission barber**. See [Edit a team member](/ayuda/equipo/editar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo).

## Frequently asked questions

**I open the record and it's empty.**
You came in through the direct address. Go back to **Team** and open it from the list with **Actions** › **View**.

**Is the record the same for commission and rent?**
Almost. Only the money tab changes: **Compensation** for commission and **Payments** for rent.

**Can I see what they earn from here?**
In **Compensation** or **Payments** you see how they get paid. How much they earned in a period is in **Payroll**. See [How payroll works](/ayuda/nomina/como-funciona-la-nomina).
