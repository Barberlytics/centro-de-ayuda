# Managers: who they are and what they can do

**In short:** the **Managers** tab in **Team** shows the **Member list** of people who are not just barbers: owners and administrators, with their role. A person with two profiles, like **Owner - Commission barber**, has the actions for each one.

## How to get there

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap the **Managers** tab. You'll see the **Member list**, how many there are (“6 Members”) and a search box.
   ![The Member list in the Managers tab](/assets/es/equipo/gerentes/lista.png)

## What's in the list

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The person's name, for example Laura. |
| **Location** | Their location. |
| **Role** | For example **Owner** or **Owner - Commission barber**. |
| **Email** | Their email. |
| **Phone** | Their phone number. |
| **Status** | **Active**. |
| **Action** | The **Actions** button for that row. |

## The actions on each row

Tap **Actions** on the row. For an owner you'll see **View Owner**, **Edit Owner** and **Deactivate Owner**. If that person is also a commission barber, you'll also see **View Commission barber**, **Edit Commission barber** and **Deactivate Commission barber**: each profile has its own actions.

![The Actions menu of a Managers row with the actions for each profile](/assets/es/equipo/gerentes/acciones-fila.png)

The **Delete** option wasn't seen in this tab.

## An owner who is also a barber

A person can be an owner and also serve clients as a commission barber. In the list they appear with the role **Owner - Commission barber** and sign in to the app with two profiles, which they switch between without signing in again. See [Switch roles](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/cambiar-de-rol).

## Who sees this tab

- With the factory permissions, the owner and the location administrator have **Managers**.
- With the commission barber profile, **Team** has only three tabs and **Managers** doesn't appear.

## Activate managers at a location

Just like barbers, managers are activated per location: **Actions** › **Activate team in this location**, **Managers** tab. See [Activate the team in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-equipo-en-una-sucursal).

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I add a partner or an administrator?**
With **Actions** › **Create team member** and the **Owner** or **Location administrator** role. See [Create an owner](/ayuda/equipo/crear-dueno) and [Create a location administrator](/ayuda/equipo/crear-administrador).

**Does the receptionist show up here?**
In the test account only the **Owner** and **Owner - Commission barber** roles were seen. Whether the receptionist shows up in this list hasn't been checked yet.

**Can I be an owner and a barber at the same time?**
Yes. In the list you appear as **Owner - Commission barber**, with the actions for both profiles.
