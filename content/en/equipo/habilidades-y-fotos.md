# A barber's skills and photos

**In short:** the profile photo, the **Description**, the **Skills** and the **Gallery - 0/5** (up to five photos) are filled in the **Profile** tab when you create or edit a barber. In their record, the photo shows at the top next to the name and the **Skills** in the **Details** block.

## Where they're uploaded

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Create the barber with **Actions** › **Create team member**, or edit them from their row with **Actions** › **Edit Commission barber** (or **Rent barber**).
3. In the **Profile** tab:
   ![A barber's Profile form with the photo, the Description, the Skills and the Gallery](/assets/es/equipo/crear-barbero-por-comision/perfil.png)

   | Field | Required | What it is |
   |---|---|---|
   | Photo | No | The barber's profile photo. |
   | **Description** | No | A short text about them, for example “Classic cut and beard specialist.” |
   | **Skills** | No | What they know how to do. |
   | **Gallery - 0/5** | No | Up to five photos of their work. The counter says how many you've added. |

4. Tap **Continue** (when creating) or **Save** (when editing).

> [!NOTE]
> How a photo is uploaded, whether skills are typed freely or chosen from a list, and where clients see these photos hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed with a test barber.

## Where they show up

In the barber's record, **Profile** tab: the photo at the top next to the name and, in **Details**, the **Skills** (“--” if they have none). The **Description** and the gallery photos weren't seen in the record; where they're shown hasn't been checked yet. See [A barber's record: profile](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-perfil).

## Frequently asked questions

**How many photos can I upload?**
Five in the gallery, plus the profile photo. The field is called **Gallery - 0/5**.

**Do clients see them?**
It hasn't been checked yet. In the meantime, upload only photos you don't mind showing.

**Can I change the photo later?**
Yes, from **Actions** › **Edit** on the barber's row, **Profile** tab.
