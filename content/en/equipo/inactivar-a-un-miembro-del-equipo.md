# Deactivate a team member

**In short:** on the person's row tap **Actions** › **Deactivate Commission barber** (or **Deactivate Rent barber**, **Deactivate Owner**...). Deactivating is the option for someone who stops working with you: it takes them off the team without deleting them.

## Before you deactivate

Think about which of the options you need:

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| Them to stop working at **one** location but stay at another | **Actions** › **Activate team in this location** and turn off their switch. See [Activate the team in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-equipo-en-una-sucursal). |
| Them not to get appointments for a few days | A **Vacation** block. See [A barber's vacation](/ayuda/calendario/vacaciones-de-un-barbero). |
| Them to stop working with you | **Deactivate**, which is this article. |
| To remove them completely | **Delete**. See [Delete a team member](/ayuda/equipo/borrar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo). |

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Find the person under **Barbers** (**Commission barber** or **Rent barber** view) or under **Managers**.
3. On their row, tap **Actions** › **Deactivate Commission barber** (or the **Deactivate** option for their role).
   ![The Actions menu of a row with the Deactivate option](/assets/es/equipo/la-lista-de-barberos/acciones-fila.png)

## Frequently asked questions

**Does deactivating delete their history?**
It hasn't been checked yet. What is known is that **Deactivate** and **Delete** are two different options in the same menu; **Delete** is the permanent one.

**A barber is going on vacation. Do I deactivate them?**
No. Block their days with a block in the **Vacation** category and you keep everything else.

**If a person has two profiles, do I deactivate them separately?**
In **Managers**, someone who is **Owner - Commission barber** has **Deactivate Owner** and **Deactivate Commission barber** separately.
