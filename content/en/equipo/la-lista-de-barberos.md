# The barber list: commission and rent

**In short:** the **Barbers** tab shows the **Barber list** in two views: **Commission barber** and **Rent barber**. Each button says how many there are at the location. You can filter by **Level**, search for someone and, on each row, open **Actions** to view, edit, deactivate or delete.

## How to get there

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Stay on the **Barbers** tab.
3. Choose the **Commission barber** or **Rent barber** view. Each button shows in parentheses how many barbers of that type there are at the location.
   ![The Barber list in the Commission barber view](/assets/es/equipo/la-lista-de-barberos/comision.png)

## The two views

- **Commission barber:** “This role is for barbers who are paid commissions.”
- **Rent barber:** “This role is for team members who are rented a workspace.”

![The Barber list in the Rent barber view, with the Bank account column](/assets/es/equipo/la-lista-de-barberos/renta.png)

Which one suits each person is explained in [Commission barber or rent barber: which to choose](/ayuda/equipo/comision-o-renta).

## What's in each column

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The barber's name, for example Carlos. |
| **Location** | The location you're in. |
| **Level** | Their level, for example “Senior,” or “-” if they have none. |
| **Email** | Their email. |
| **Phone** | Their phone number. |
| **Bank account** | Only in the **Rent barber** view: the account saved in their **Payments** tab. |
| **Status** | **Active**. |
| **Action** | The **Actions** button for that row. |

## Filter and search

- The **Level** filter shows only the barbers of one level, for example the “Senior” ones.
- The search box finds a person by name.

## The actions on each row

Tap **Actions** on the person's row. In the commission view you'll see **View Commission barber**, **Edit Commission barber**, **Deactivate Commission barber** and **Delete Commission barber**. In the rent view, the same four with “Rent barber.”

![The Actions menu of a row with View, Edit, Deactivate and Delete](/assets/es/equipo/la-lista-de-barberos/acciones-fila.png)

| Action | What it does | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **View** | Opens the person's record with all its tabs. | [A barber's record: profile](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-perfil) |
| **Edit** | Opens the same record so you can change details. | [Edit a team member](/ayuda/equipo/editar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo) |
| **Deactivate** | Takes the person off the team without deleting them. | [Deactivate a team member](/ayuda/equipo/inactivar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo) |
| **Delete** | Removes the person. | [Delete a team member](/ayuda/equipo/borrar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo) |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The record only loads if you enter from this list with **View**. If you type its address in the browser, it comes up empty.

## Frequently asked questions

**I don't see a barber I know works here.**
Check two things: that you're in the right view (**Commission barber** or **Rent barber**) and that they're activated at this location with **Actions** › **Activate team in this location**.

**Why do rent barbers have a bank account column and commission barbers don't?**
Because a rent barber's record stores their **Bank information**, in the **Payments** tab. The commission barber doesn't have that tab.

**How do I see only the barbers of one level?**
With the **Level** filter, above the table.
