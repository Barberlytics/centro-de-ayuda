# Barber levels: what they're for

**In short:** a level is a label that groups your barbers, for example “Senior” or “Master.” Every barber has a level (it's required when you create them) and you can use it to filter the list. Levels are managed in the **Levels** tab of **Team**.

## Where they show up

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap the **Levels** tab. You'll see the **Level list** with how many there are, a filter and a search box.
   ![The Level list with the Name, Barbers and Actions columns](/assets/es/equipo/niveles-de-barberos/lista.png)

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The name of the level, for example “Senior.” A “-” groups barbers with no level. |
| **Barbers** | How many barbers have that level. |
| **Actions** | **Edit** and **Delete**. |

## Where a barber's level appears

- When you create or edit a barber, in the **Barber level\*** field of the **Profile** tab.
- In the record, under the name: “Commission barber | Senior.”
- In the **Barbers** list, in the **Level** column and in the **Level** filter.
- In **Actions** › **Sort barbers**, which has a **Level** tab.

## What it's for

- To quickly find those of one level with the list's **Level** filter.
- So clients and the team know who's who: the level shows in the barber's record.

How to create a new one or rename it is covered in [Create and edit a level](/ayuda/equipo/crear-y-editar-un-nivel).

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the level change the price or the commission?**
Not by itself. The price of each service and the commission are set per barber, in their record. The level is just the label that groups them.

**Can a barber have no level?**
When you create them, **Barber level\*** is required. In the test account some barbers show “-” in the **Level** column, so it can be empty for older people.

**How many levels can I have?**
The app doesn't show a limit. Create the ones you actually use; too many levels make filtering harder.
