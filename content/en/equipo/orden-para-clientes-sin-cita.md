# Order for walk-in clients

**In short:** in **Team** › **Actions** › **Order for walk-in and no-preference booking**, **Walk-in booking** tab, you turn on the barbers who can take people who come in without booking and drag them into the order they're up in. Then tap **Save**.

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Order for walk-in and no-preference booking**.
3. Stay on the **Walk-in booking** tab. The screen says: “Barbers you turn off on this screen won't be able to take clients who come in without an appointment.”
   ![The Walk-in booking tab with a switch and a number for each barber](/assets/es/equipo/orden-para-clientes-sin-cita/pantalla.png)
4. Turn on the switch for each barber who does take walk-ins. The ones turned on have a number (1, 2, 3...); the ones turned off, “–”.
5. Drag the barbers to change the order.
6. Tap **Save**.

## How it's used

When a client comes in without an appointment, the order says whose turn it is. A barber turned off here can't take walk-in clients, even if they're free.

How that client is recorded in the calendar is covered in [A client who walks in without an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cliente-sin-cita-previa). The barber is paid with their **Walk-in client commission**; see [Compensation](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-compensacion).

## Who can do it

With the factory permissions, **Get in line** (that's what this function is called in the matrix) belongs to the owner and the location administrator.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it the same order as the calendar columns?**
No. The columns are sorted in **Actions** › **Sort barbers**. See [Sort the barbers](/ayuda/equipo/ordenar-barberos).

**What about clients who don't ask for a barber?**
That's the other tab on this same screen, **No preference**. See [Order for no-preference clients](/ayuda/equipo/orden-para-clientes-sin-preferencia).

**A new barber doesn't appear in the list.**
Only barbers who are active at the location you're in show up. Activate them with **Actions** › **Activate team in this location**.
