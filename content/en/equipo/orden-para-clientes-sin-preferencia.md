# Order for no-preference clients

**In short:** in **Team** › **Actions** › **Order for walk-in and no-preference booking**, **No preference** tab, you turn on the barbers who can take a client who didn't ask for anyone and drag them into the order they're up in. Then tap **Save**.

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Order for walk-in and no-preference booking**.
3. Tap the **No preference** tab.
   ![The No preference tab with a switch and a number for each barber](/assets/es/equipo/orden-para-clientes-sin-preferencia/pantalla.png)
4. Turn on the switch for each barber who can take no-preference clients. The ones turned on have a number (1, 2, 3...); the ones turned off, “–”.
5. Drag the barbers to change the order.
6. Tap **Save**.

## How it's used

A no-preference client is one who doesn't ask for anyone: whoever is available takes them. This order says whose turn it is. A barber turned off here doesn't get no-preference clients.

How that client looks on the appointment is covered in [Clients with no preferred barber: who serves them](/ayuda/calendario/clientes-sin-preferencia-de-barbero). A commission barber is paid with their **No-preference client commission**; a rent barber gets the **No-preference appointment deduction** applied.

## Who can do it

With the factory permissions, **Get in line** belongs to the owner and the location administrator.

## Frequently asked questions

**How is it different from “walk-in”?**
A walk-in is someone who comes in without booking. No preference is someone who books or comes in but doesn't ask for a barber. Each case has its own tab and its own order. See [Order for walk-in clients](/ayuda/equipo/orden-para-clientes-sin-cita).

**I want the new barbers to get more no-preference clients.**
Put them first in the list by dragging them up and tap **Save**.

**Can a rent barber be here?**
Yes. Keep in mind that a percentage is deducted from them for each no-preference appointment. See [Payments](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-de-renta-pagos).
