# Sort the barbers

**In short:** in **Team** › **Actions** › **Sort barbers** you drag the barbers until they're in the order you want and tap **Save**. That's the order of the calendar columns.

## Steps

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Sort barbers**.
3. **Sort barbers** opens with two tabs, **Barber** and **Level**. In **Barber** there's a numbered list with each barber's photo and name.
   ![The Sort barbers screen with the numbered list of barbers in the Barber tab](/assets/es/equipo/ordenar-barberos/pantalla.png)
4. Drag each barber by the icon on the left to the position you want. The number updates.
5. Tap **Save**. If you change your mind, tap **Cancel**.

> [!NOTE]
> What gets sorted in the **Level** tab and what happens when you tap **Save** hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed with the test account.

## Where you'll notice it

In the **Calendar**, each barber has a column. They appear in the order you set here.

![The barbers' columns in the calendar](/assets/es/calendario/conoce-el-calendario/columnas.png)

## Who can do it

With the factory permissions, **Order barbers' calendars** belongs only to the owner. See [Permissions table: what each role can do](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol).

## Frequently asked questions

**Does this order decide who takes walk-in clients?**
No. That's set in **Actions** › **Order for walk-in and no-preference booking**. See [Order for walk-in clients](/ayuda/equipo/orden-para-clientes-sin-cita).

**Is the order per location?**
The list shows the barbers who are active at the location you're in. Sort each location's own.

**I want a barber not to show up in the calendar.**
Sorting doesn't hide them. For that, check the calendar's **Team** filter or their **Visibility for booking**. See [Filter the calendar by team](/ayuda/calendario/filtrar-el-calendario-por-equipo).
