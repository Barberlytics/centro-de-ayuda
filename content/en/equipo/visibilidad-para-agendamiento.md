# Visibility for booking: show or hide a barber

**In short:** **Visibility for booking** is a switch in each barber's record. In their record it appears as **Enabled** or **Disabled**. By its name, it decides whether the barber can be booked. You change it when you create or edit the person.

## Where you see it

1. Tap **Team** in the menu.
2. Under **Barbers**, tap **Actions** › **View Commission barber** (or **View Rent barber**) on the person's row.
3. In the **Profile** tab, **Details** block, you'll find **Visibility for booking** with its value: **Enabled** or **Disabled**.
   ![The Profile tab of the record with Visibility for booking under Details](/assets/es/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-perfil/ver.png)

## How to change it

1. In the list, tap **Actions** › **Edit Commission barber** (or **Rent barber**).
2. In **Profile**, turn the **Visibility for booking** switch on or off.
3. Tap **Save**.

You also choose it when you create the barber, in the same **Profile** tab.

> [!NOTE]
> What exactly changes when you disable it hasn't been checked yet: whether the barber disappears from the calendar, from the list when you create an appointment, or only from online booking. Until that's confirmed, if a barber can't be booked, check this switch along with everything else.

## If a barber can't be booked

Check in this order:

1. That they're activated at this location (**Actions** › **Activate team in this location**).
2. That they have a schedule that day (record › **Calendar** and **Working hours**).
3. That **Visibility for booking** is **Enabled**.
4. That the calendar's **Team** filter shows them.

It's detailed in [A barber doesn't show up on the calendar](/ayuda/calendario/un-barbero-no-aparece-en-el-calendario).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it the same as deactivating?**
No. Deactivating takes the person off the team. Visibility is a setting in the profile of someone who stays on the team. See [Deactivate a team member](/ayuda/equipo/inactivar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo).

**I want a barber to only serve their regular clients.**
Look at **Can take new clients** in the **Options** tab, which is more precise than hiding them completely. See [Options](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-opciones).

**Does it apply to all locations?**
It's a detail of the person's profile, not of the location. Whether it affects all of them at once hasn't been checked yet.
