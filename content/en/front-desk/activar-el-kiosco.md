# Activate the kiosk on the tablet

**In short:** open the kiosk link on the tablet. The **Reception** screen asks someone on the team to sign in once; after that, it's ready for your clients.

## Steps

1. Copy the link from **Settings › Online booking › Front Desk link** and open it in the tablet's browser.
2. You'll see **Reception**: “Staff sign-in is required to activate the kiosk.”
   ![The kiosk's Reception screen, which asks for a team member's phone number or to sign in with Barberlytics](/assets/es/front-desk/activar-el-kiosco/recepcion.png)
3. Sign in one of two ways:
   - **With your phone number:** in **Staff phone**, pick the country (+57, +1 or +58), type your phone number and tap **Sign in with code**. A 6-digit code arrives: type it in “Enter the verification code” and tap **Activate kiosk**.
   - **With your account:** tap **Sign in with Barberlytics**. If you already have Barberlytics open in that browser, the kiosk activates right away.
4. **Welcome** appears with the phone number field. The kiosk is ready.
   ![The active kiosk with Welcome, the Phone number field and the Check in button](/assets/es/front-desk/que-es/bienvenido.png)
5. Tap **Full screen** (bottom left) so the client doesn't see the browser bar.

> [!TIP]
> Keep the tablet plugged into its charger with the screen lock turned off, so the kiosk is always on display.

## Errors you may see

| What the screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The code doesn't arrive | The message was delayed or the number was typed wrong | Tap “Didn't get it? Resend code” or **Back** to fix the number |

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to activate it every day?**
No. It stays active until someone taps **Lock**.

**Which phone number do I use to activate it?**
One belonging to someone on the team with a Barberlytics account. The client never sees this screen.
