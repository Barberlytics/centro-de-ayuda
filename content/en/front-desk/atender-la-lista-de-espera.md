# Serve the kiosk waitlist

**In short:** clients who sign up at the kiosk show up in **Calendar › Waitlist**. When you're ready to serve one, tap **Check in**: their appointment is created on the spot, in the column of the barber they chose, and it's marked as already arrived.

## Steps

1. In the **Calendar**, tap the settings button (the one with the sliders) and choose **Waitlist**.
2. Each client shows up on a card with their name, the service, the price, “Any time (opening-closing),” the **New** label and the **Check in** button.
3. Tap **Check in**. “Updated successfully” appears, the card disappears from the list and the appointment shows up in the calendar at the current time, with the service and barber the client chose.
4. The appointment is set to **Checked in** and **NEXT STEP** says “Laura is waiting.” When the barber takes them, tap **Seat**. Then, **Pay appointment**.

> [!NOTE]
> The waitlist card doesn't show the barber the client chose. You see it when you tap **Check in**, in the column where the appointment appears.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the waitlist per location?**
Yes. If you don't see the client, make sure the kiosk's location is selected in the header.

**Can I add someone myself?**
Yes, with **Add to waitlist** (in the ⋮ menu of the panel). See [The calendar waitlist](/ayuda/calendario/lista-de-espera).
