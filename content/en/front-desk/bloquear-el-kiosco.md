# Lock the kiosk

**In short:** tap **Lock** (bottom left, next to **Full screen**). The kiosk goes back to **Reception** and nobody can use it until someone on the team signs in again.

## Steps

1. On the kiosk screen, tap **Lock**. It doesn't ask for confirmation.
   ![The active kiosk with the Lock button highlighted at the bottom left](/assets/es/front-desk/bloquear-el-kiosco/botones.png)
2. **Reception** appears: “Staff sign-in is required to activate the kiosk.”
   ![The kiosk's Reception screen, which asks for a team member's phone number or to sign in with Barberlytics](/assets/es/front-desk/activar-el-kiosco/recepcion.png)
3. To use it again, activate it like the first time: [Activate the kiosk on the tablet](/ayuda/front-desk/activar-el-kiosco).

> [!TIP]
> If you have Barberlytics open in that browser, **Sign in with Barberlytics** reactivates it right away, no code needed.
