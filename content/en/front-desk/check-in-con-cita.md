# Check-in for a client with an appointment

**In short:** the client types their phone number, sees their appointment details and taps **Check in**. The appointment ends up as if you had tapped **Check in** in the calendar: the barber knows their client is already at the barbershop.

## What the client does

1. On **Welcome**, they type their phone number in **Phone number** (picking the country if it isn't +57) and tap **Check in**.
2. **Welcome back!** appears · “Your appointment details. Check in and wait to be called.” with the **Location**, **Service**, **Barber** and **Date and time** of their appointment.
3. They tap **Check in**.
4. **Check in** appears (with a green check mark) · “We'll be with you shortly” · “We know you've arrived. Now take a seat and wait for your barber to call you.” They tap **Back to start** to leave the kiosk ready; this screen doesn't close on its own.

## What you see in the calendar

On the appointment's card, the **Check in** button changes to **Seat**. The appointment moves to **Checked in**. When you open it, **NEXT STEP** says “Laura is waiting.” and has the **Seat** button with the appointment's price. When the barber takes them, tap **Seat**. See [Move the client to the chair](/ayuda/calendario/pasar-al-cliente-a-la-silla).

If you have barber notifications turned on in **Settings**, the barber gets the alert.

> [!TIP]
> If on **Welcome back!** the client sees a detail that's wrong (a different barber or time), they should tap **Back** and let you know before checking in.
