# New client at the kiosk

**In short:** if the phone number isn't in your barbershop, the kiosk opens **Let's get to know you** to create their profile: first name, last name and email. Then it moves on to booking.

## What the client sees

1. They type their phone number and tap **Check in**.
2. **Let's get to know you** appears: “We'll use this information to create your profile and make booking even easier.”
   ![The Let's get to know you panel with the number already typed in and the First name, Last name and Email fields](/assets/es/front-desk/cliente-nuevo/conozcamonos.png)
3. The **Phone number** is already filled in. They fill in **First name**, **Last name** and **Email**. Below, it says “We'll never share your information.”
4. They tap **Continue**.

The X at the top closes the form and goes back to **Welcome**.
