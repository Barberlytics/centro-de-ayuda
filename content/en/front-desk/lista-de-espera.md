# Walk-in client: the waitlist from the kiosk

**In short:** if the client has no appointment today, the kiosk says “No appointment found” and offers **Join the waitlist**. They choose a service and barber, confirm, and show up in the **Waitlist** in your calendar.

## What the client does

1. They type their phone number and tap **Check in**. “No appointment found · Book an appointment or join the waitlist to be seen as a walk-in” appears.
   ![The No appointment found screen with Book an appointment and Join the waitlist](/assets/es/front-desk/no-encuentra-la-cita/sin-cita.png)
2. They tap **Join the waitlist**.
3. In **Choose a service**, they tap the service (each one shows its duration and price) and **Continue**.
   ![The Choose a service list with the duration and price of each service](/assets/es/front-desk/lista-de-espera/servicio.png)
4. In **Choose a barber**, they tap the barber (their level shows: Senior, Master…) and **Continue**.
   ![The Choose a barber list with each barber's level and the Continue button](/assets/es/front-desk/lista-de-espera/barbero.png)
5. In **Confirm your waitlist details**, they review their name, the service and the barber, and tap **Confirm waitlist**.
   ![Confirm your waitlist details with the client, the service, the barber and Confirm waitlist](/assets/es/front-desk/lista-de-espera/confirmar.png)
6. **Waitlist confirmed** appears · “We'll be with you shortly” · “You're on the waitlist. We'll call you when a barber is available to see you.” They tap **Back to start** to leave the kiosk ready for the next person.

> [!TIP]
> Ask the client to tap **Back to start** when they're done: the confirmation screen doesn't close on its own.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can they choose “any barber”?**
No. On the kiosk waitlist, they have to choose a barber.

**They tapped Back and ended up at the beginning. Is that normal?**
Yes. **Back** takes you to **Welcome**, not to the previous step: they have to start over.

**Where do I see them?**
In the calendar, in **Waitlist**. See [Serve the kiosk waitlist](/ayuda/front-desk/atender-la-lista-de-espera).
