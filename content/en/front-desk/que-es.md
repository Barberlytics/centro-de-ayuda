# Front Desk: the reception kiosk

**In short:** Front Desk is a screen for your clients. You open its link on a tablet or a computer at the barbershop and, when they arrive, the client types their phone number. If they have an appointment, they're marked as arrived. If they don't, they get on the waitlist or book an appointment right there.

## What it's for

- **Check-in without going through the front desk.** A client with an appointment types their phone number and the appointment changes on its own in your calendar, as if you had tapped **Check in**.
- **Walk-in clients.** Someone who arrives without an appointment can **Join the waitlist** or **Book an appointment**.
- **New clients.** If the phone number isn't in your barbershop, the screen asks for their details to create their profile.

You don't need to buy anything: it's just a link. Any tablet, phone or computer with a browser works.

## Where the link is

1. Go to **Settings › Online booking**.
2. Find the **Front Desk link** block: “Use this link on a tablet or kiosk at your location so clients can check in when they arrive.”
3. In **Front Desk kiosk link**, tap **Copy**. The link starts with `https://fd.barberlytics.com/front-desk/`.
   ![The Front Desk link block with the Front Desk kiosk link and the Copy button](/assets/es/front-desk/que-es/enlace.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Don't mix it up with the **Direct booking link** (`…/book/…`), which is right above it. That one is for the client to book from home; the kiosk one is for the screen at your location.

## What the client sees, step by step

1. **Welcome** · “Enter your phone number” · **Check in**.
   ![The active kiosk with Welcome, the Phone number field and the Check in button](/assets/es/front-desk/que-es/bienvenido.png)
2. Depending on their case:
   - They have an appointment today → it's recorded that they arrived. See [Check-in for a client with an appointment](/ayuda/front-desk/check-in-con-cita).
   - They have no appointment → “No appointment found.” See [The waitlist from the kiosk](/ayuda/front-desk/lista-de-espera).
   - They're new → “Let's get to know you.” See [New client at the kiosk](/ayuda/front-desk/cliente-nuevo).

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the kiosk turn off or expire on its own?**
No. Once activated, it stays ready until someone taps **Lock**.

**Does it let the barber know when their client arrives?**
Yes, if you have those notifications turned on in **Settings**.

**Do I have one link per location?**
The kiosk works with one location. Which location it uses and how it's chosen is still to be confirmed.
