# Book an appointment from the kiosk

**In short:** on “No appointment found,” **Book an appointment** opens the same online booking your clients use from home: **Location › Service/Barber › Date and time › Confirm**. It works for someone who prefers a set time, today or another day, instead of waiting.

## Steps

1. Tap **Book an appointment**.
2. Follow the booking steps. They're the same as in [How your client books](/ayuda/agendamiento-online/como-reserva-tu-cliente).
   ![Booking opened from the kiosk, with the client's last visit preselected and the available times](/assets/es/front-desk/reservar-desde-el-kiosco/reserva.png)
3. The X at the top closes the booking and goes back to **Welcome**.

> [!NOTE]
> In the **Location** step, all the locations of your barbershop show up, not just the kiosk's. Tell the client to choose yours.
