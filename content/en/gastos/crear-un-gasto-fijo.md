# Create a fixed expense

**In short:** in **Expenses**, tap **Actions › Create fixed expense**, type the **Name** and the **Amount**, pick the **Frequency** and tap **Create**. The description and photos are optional.

## Steps

1. Tap **Expenses** in the menu.
2. At the top right, tap **Actions** and choose **Create fixed expense**. The “Create fixed expense” screen opens.
   ![The Create fixed expense form: Name, Amount, Frequency, Description, Add photos and the Create button](/assets/es/gastos/crear-un-gasto-fijo/formulario.png)
3. Type the **Name**, for example “Shop rent.”
4. Type the **Amount**, for example 1200000. The field already shows the peso sign.
5. Pick the **Frequency**: Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, Quarterly, Every 6 months or Annual. [A fixed expense's frequency](/ayuda/gastos/frecuencia-de-un-gasto-fijo) explains each one.
6. If you want, type a **Description** (“Paid on the 5th of each month to the landlord”).
7. If you want to keep the invoice or receipt, tap the **+** under **Add photos - 0/3**. You can add up to 3 photos.
8. Tap **Create**.

To leave without saving, tap **Cancel** at the top right.

The fields with an asterisk (**Name\***, **Amount\***, **Frequency\***) are required.

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows right after you tap **Create** (the message, and which screen it goes back to) and how an uploaded photo looks in the form still need to be checked with a test expense.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I enter the amount with decimals?**
The amount is saved with two decimals: in the list it shows as $1,200,000.00.

**Which location does the expense go to?**
The one you have selected in the header when you create it.

**I picked the wrong frequency. Do I delete it?**
No need. In the list, tap **Actions › Edit** on the expense's row ([Edit or delete an expense](/ayuda/gastos/editar-o-borrar-un-gasto)).
