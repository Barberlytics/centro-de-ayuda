# Edit or delete an expense

**In short:** find the expense on its tab (**Fixed** or **Variable**), tap **Actions** on its row and choose **View**, **Edit** or **Delete**.

## View an expense

1. Tap **Expenses** in the menu and open the tab where the expense is.
2. On the expense's row, tap **Actions › View**.
3. The expense's profile opens with the **Information** block (**Name**, **Amount** and, if it's fixed, **Frequency**), the **Description** and the **Photos** (“1/3”).
   ![A fixed expense's profile: Information with name, amount and frequency, the description and the photos](/assets/es/gastos/editar-o-borrar-un-gasto/ver.png)
4. To go back to the list, tap the **←** arrow next to the title.

From the profile you also have the **Actions** button at the top right.

## Edit an expense

1. On the expense's row, tap **Actions › Edit**.
2. Change whatever you need: the name, the amount, the frequency (fixed expenses only), the description or the photos.
3. Save your changes.

## Delete an expense

1. On the expense's row, tap **Actions › Delete**.
2. Confirm.

> [!WARNING]
> A deleted expense can't be recovered. If you only want to correct a detail, use **Edit**.

> [!NOTE]
> The **Edit** form and the **Delete** confirmation couldn't be opened without changing real data. The exact wording of those steps will be filled in with a test expense.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change a fixed expense to variable?**
That option wasn't there. Delete the expense and create it again as the other type.

**I deleted an expense by mistake.**
There's no trash. Create it again with **Actions › Create fixed expense** or **Create variable expense**.

**Who can delete expenses?**
The owner, the location admin and barbers have **Delete** in Expenses by default. It can be removed by role or by person ([How permissions work](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/como-funcionan-los-permisos)).
