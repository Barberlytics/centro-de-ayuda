# A fixed expense's frequency

**In short:** when you create a fixed expense, you pick how often it's paid in **Frequency**: **Weekly**, **Every 2 weeks**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Every 6 months** or **Annual**. It's required and shows in the **Frequency** column of the list.

![The Frequency dropdown open with the six options](/assets/es/gastos/frecuencia-de-un-gasto-fijo/opciones.png)

## The six options

| Option | How often it's paid | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** | Every week | Towel laundering |
| **Every 2 weeks** | Every two weeks | A biweekly payment to the front desk |
| **Monthly** | Every month | “Shop rent,” $1,200,000 |
| **Quarterly** | Every three months | Air conditioner maintenance |
| **Every 6 months** | Every six months | Fire extinguisher check |
| **Annual** | Once a year | Shop insurance, brand renewal |

## Where you pick it

In **Expenses › Actions › Create fixed expense**, in the **Frequency\*** field. Only fixed expenses have it; the variable expense form doesn't ask for it. The full steps are in [Create a fixed expense](/ayuda/gastos/crear-un-gasto-fijo).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I set a different frequency, like every 10 days?**
No. Only the six on the list are available. Pick the closest one and put the detail in the **Description**.

**Does the frequency change the amount I enter?**
No. The **Amount** is what you pay each time. If rent is $1,200,000 a month, you type 1200000 and pick **Monthly**.

**Can I change the frequency later?**
Yes, with **Actions › Edit** on the expense's row ([Edit or delete an expense](/ayuda/gastos/editar-o-borrar-un-gasto)).
