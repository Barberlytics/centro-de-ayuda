# Fixed and variable expenses: what's the difference

**In short:** a **fixed** expense repeats every so often and has a **Frequency** (rent, electricity, your phone plan). A **variable** expense happens once and only has a name and an amount (buying towels, a repair). You create both from **Actions** in **Expenses**.

The screen puts it this way: “Here you can keep track of all your expenses, whether they're recurring or occasional.”

![The Expenses Actions menu open, with Create fixed expense and Create variable expense](/assets/es/gastos/gastos-fijos-y-variables/menu-acciones.png)

## What each type saves

| | Fixed expense | Variable expense |
|---|---|---|
| Tab where it appears | **Fixed** | **Variable** |
| Required details | **Name**, **Amount** and **Frequency** | **Name** and **Amount** |
| Optional details | **Description** and up to 3 photos | **Description** and up to 3 photos |
| Example | “Shop rent,” $1,200,000, Monthly | “New towels,” $180,000 |

The only real difference is the **Frequency**. A fixed expense says how often it repeats; a variable expense doesn't.

## Which one to pick

- If you always pay it, on the same date and for about the same amount, it's **fixed**: rent, utilities, internet, subscriptions.
- If you pay it once and don't know when it will come back, it's **variable**: a new chair, a repair, an extra product order.

> [!TIP]
> If you're not sure, ask yourself whether you'd give it a frequency. If the answer is “every month” or “every week,” it's fixed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a fixed expense get recorded on its own every month?**
The app wasn't seen creating copies of the expense by itself. The **Frequency** is saved on the expense and shown in the list; what the app does with it afterward isn't confirmed.

**Does an expense belong to the whole barbershop or to one location?**
The expenses you see are the ones for the location you have selected at the top. Switch locations to see another one's.

**Who can record expenses?**
The owner, the location admin and barbers (commission and rent). The receptionist doesn't see **Expenses**. Permissions are adjusted by role and by person; see [How permissions work](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/como-funcionan-los-permisos).
