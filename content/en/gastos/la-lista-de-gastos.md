# The expense list

**In short:** **Expenses** has two tabs, **Fixed** and **Variable**. Each one is a table with the location's expenses, a search box and an **Actions** button on every row with **View**, **Edit** and **Delete**. The big **Actions** button at the top right is for creating.

## The Fixed tab

It opens by default. The title says “Fixed expense list” and, next to it, how many there are (“2 Fixed expenses”).

![The Fixed tab: title with the count, search box and the table with Name, Frequency, Amount and Actions](/assets/es/gastos/la-lista-de-gastos/fijos.png)

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The name you gave the expense, for example “Shop rent” |
| **Frequency** | How often it repeats: Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, Quarterly, Every 6 months or Annual |
| **Amount** | The expense's amount, for example $1,200,000.00 |
| **Actions** | The row's menu: **View**, **Edit** and **Delete** |

## The Variable tab

Tap **Variable**. The title changes to “Variable expense list” with its count.

![The Variable tab: the table with Name, Amount and Actions](/assets/es/gastos/la-lista-de-gastos/variables.png)

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The expense's name, for example “New towels” |
| **Amount** | The expense's amount |
| **Actions** | The row's menu: **View**, **Edit** and **Delete** |

There's no **Frequency** column: a variable expense doesn't repeat.

## Search for an expense

1. Type part of the name in the **Search** field on the tab where the expense is.
2. Tap **Search**.

Each tab has its own search box: if the expense is fixed, look for it on **Fixed**.

## Each row's menu

Tap **Actions** on the expense's row to open **View**, **Edit** and **Delete**. [Edit or delete an expense](/ayuda/gastos/editar-o-borrar-un-gasto) walks through it step by step.

![The Actions button on a row of the fixed expense list](/assets/es/gastos/la-lista-de-gastos/acciones-fila.png)

## At the bottom: how many per page

At the bottom of the table, **Rows per page** (in English, just like that) sets how many expenses to see per page, and the arrows move between pages. Next to it you see the range you're viewing (“1-2 of 2”).

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I see the expenses of all my locations together?**
The list shows the ones for the location selected in the header. Switch locations to see another one's ([Switch locations](/ayuda/navegacion/cambiar-de-sucursal)).

**Is there a total for expenses?**
Not in the list. You see how many expenses there are (“2 Fixed expenses”) and the amount of each one, but not a sum.

**Where's the button to create one?**
In the **Actions** button at the top right, not the one on the row. **Create fixed expense** and **Create variable expense** are there.
