# Glossary: every Barberlytics word in one sentence

**In short:** here is every term you'll see in the app, in alphabetical order and in a single sentence. The definitions in quotation marks are the ones the app itself shows. When a term has its own article, the sentence links to it.

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Active** | The permissions column that says whether a feature exists for that role or person; also the status of a barber, service or location that is in use. |
| **Agenda** | The calendar view that lists the day's appointments one below the other. |
| **Arrived** | The appointment status once the client is at the barbershop (action **Check in**). |
| **AVF** | An indicator on the client's record that the app shows in **Days** ([AVF](/ayuda/glosario/que-es-el-avf)). |
| **Average ticket** | How much is charged, on average, per appointment. |
| **Billed** | The Transactions card with the total of all the period's accounts, charged or not. |
| **Block (calendar)** | A block of time in a barber's schedule (**Sick**, **Vacation**, **Holiday**, **Lunch**, **Meeting**, **Business block**, **Personal block**). |
| **Block (client)** | The Clients tab with “the clients we have blocked for some reason. These clients can't book appointments online.” |
| **Canceled** | An appointment that was canceled; it counts toward the **Cancellation rate** along with the ones that didn't show up. |
| **Churn** | In the Notification center, “'Churn' (lost)”: a client's **Lost** status ([Churn](/ayuda/glosario/que-es-el-churn)). |
| **Client download** | The permission to download the client list. |
| **Closed** | An appointment status, between **In chair** and **Completed**. |
| **Collected** | A Transactions account that has already been paid ([Account statuses](/ayuda/transacciones/estados-de-una-cuenta)). |
| **Combo** | Several services sold together with a **Combined price** and a **Combo duration**. |
| **Commission** | The percentage of the service that a commission barber keeps: basic, returning client, walk-in clients, no preference or new. |
| **Commission barber** | The role “for barbers who are paid commissions”: a percentage per service, different depending on the client type. |
| **Company** | The whole business; you have one, and it groups locations, team and catalogs ([Company and locations](/ayuda/sucursales/empresa-y-sucursales)). |
| **Completed** | The last status of a finished appointment. |
| **Fixed expense** | An expense that repeats with a **Frequency** (Weekly, Monthly…); a variable one is occasional. |
| **Fixed rent** | The Payroll column for rent barbers with what they pay for the chair. |
| **FOV** | An indicator on the client's record that the app shows in **Week** ([FOV](/ayuda/glosario/que-es-el-fov)). |
| **Frequency cycle** | How often a client usually comes in; used by the VIP, Nearly lost and Lost types ([How a client's type changes](/ayuda/clientes/como-se-clasifica-un-cliente)). |
| **Frequent** | “Clients who have 3 or more appointments at the barbershop and are already regulars at the barbershop.” |
| **Health Score** | The 0 to 100 number in AI Insights ([Health Score](/ayuda/metricas/puntaje-de-salud)). |
| **In chair** | The appointment status when the client is already with the barber (action **Seated**). |
| **Internal** | An appointment created by the team, not by the client. |
| **Level** | A barber's group (Master, Senior, “No level”…); used to filter and choose a barber. |
| **License** | One of the three figures in View location, with its expiration date. |
| **Lineup** | The **Order for walk-in and no-preference booking** screen: the order in which barbers take those clients. |
| **Location** | One site of the company, with its currency, time zone and language. |
| **Lost** | “Clients who have stopped coming to the barbershop for 4 visit frequency cycles and have already been lost.” |
| **LTV** | How much a client has spent at your barbershop in total ([LTV](/ayuda/glosario/que-es-el-ltv)). |
| **Managers** | The Team tab with “Member list”: the people who aren't barbers, with their role. |
| **Metrics** | The Home section, with Business, Clients, Barbers, Marketing and AI Insights. |
| **Nearly lost** | “Clients who have stopped coming to the barbershop for 3 frequency cycles, meaning they are at risk of being lost.” |
| **Net pay** | The last column of Payroll: what the barber is paid (or what they owe, if it comes out negative). |
| **New** | “New clients are those who haven't had any appointments yet or only one appointment.” |
| **No preference** | An appointment where the client didn't ask for a barber; it has its own commission and its own order in the lineup. |
| **Occupancy** | The **Occ.** percentage for each day in the Month view of the calendar ([Occupancy](/ayuda/glosario/que-es-la-ocupacion)). |
| **Online appointment** | An appointment the client booked online; the opposite of **Internal**. |
| **Owner** | The role that “has all the permissions normally granted to the barbershop owner”. In the profile it shows as **Business** and **Owner**. |
| **Pay cycle** | The payroll period: **WEEKLY**, **BIWEEKLY** or **MONTHLY**, with its **Cycle start day** ([Pay cycle](/ayuda/nomina/ciclo-de-pago)). |
| **Payment list** | The “Ready to pay” panel on the calendar, with **To collect** and **Paid**. |
| **Payroll** | What each barber is owed in the cycle, with their **Net pay**. |
| **Permissions** | The **View · Create · Edit · Delete · Active** table by feature, for each role and each person. |
| **Pre-Churn** | In the Notification center, “'Pre-Churn' (Possible loss)”: the **Nearly lost** status. |
| **Private / Public** | The switch that decides whether a service or product is “visible in online booking”. |
| **Profile** | The “hat” you sign in with; one person can have two, such as **Business** and **Barber comission**. |
| **Quick sale** | “New sale”: selling products without an appointment, with **Pay order**. |
| **Receptionist** | The role with “permissions to book appointments and check in and check out”; in Permissions, **Receptionist - front desk**. |
| **Recovered** | “Clients who were already lost but came back to the barbershop, and whom we need to take care of so they come back often.” |
| **Refunded** | An account whose payment was returned; it adds to the **REFUNDED** card. |
| **Rent barber** | The role “for team members who are rented a workspace”: they pay a base amount and the barbershop deducts a percentage for the clients it brings them. |
| **Rent base** | What a rent barber pays for their chair each cycle. |
| **Retained** | “These clients are those who have had 2 appointments at the barbershop.” |
| **Role** | What a person is at the barbershop: owner, location administrator, receptionist, commission barber or rent barber ([The five roles](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/los-cinco-roles)). |
| **Salary barber** | The Payroll tab for someone who has **Salary** as their compensation type. |
| **Scheduled** | The first status of an appointment: created, with the client not yet arrived ([Appointment statuses](/ayuda/calendario/estados-de-una-cita)). |
| **Settings** | The section with the barbershop's 17 settings in four groups. |
| **Split commission** | A field per service in the barber's record; empty (“--”) uses the catalog value. |
| **Team** | All the people in the company; in each location you only see the ones turned on there. |
| **Tip** | What the client adds to the payment; it has its own card in Transactions and its own column in Payroll. |
| **To collect** | An account that hasn't been paid yet; the card with the same name adds them up. |
| **Transactions** | The register: all the period's accounts with their status and payment method. |
| **View / Create / Edit / Delete** | The four permission columns: view the feature, create in it, change what exists and delete. |
| **VIP** | “Clients who have visited us for more than 3 cycles, which means they are very loyal to the barbershop.” |
| **Waitlist** | “All the appointments that come in through the waitlist in scheduling order.” |
| **Walk in** | The client with no record; that is how they show in Transactions and as the first option when choosing a client in Create appointment. |
| **Walk-in client** | The switch in Create appointment for someone who arrived without booking: “Applies the barber's walk-in commission and is reflected in the booking reports.” |
| **Walk-in sale** | Recording a service done without an appointment, with date, time, client, service and barber. |

## Frequently asked questions

**I can't find a word.**
Tell us which one. This glossary only includes terms the app shows or that already have an article; what isn't here is documented once it's checked on screen.

**Do the acronyms AVF, FOV and LTV have a formula?**
The app shows their value and their unit. The exact formula for each one is still to be confirmed with the team; each acronym has its own article with what is known.
