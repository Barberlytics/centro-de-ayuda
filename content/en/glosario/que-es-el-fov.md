# FOV: visit frequency

**In short:** **FOV** is the card on a client's record that shows their visit frequency in weeks (“2 Week”). It is the per-client version of the **Visit frequency** card in Metrics, which groups everyone into Weekly, Biweekly and more.

## Where it shows

1. Tap **Clients** and, on the client's row, **Actions › View**.
2. In **General information**, the **FOV** card is in the first row, next to **Total appointments** and **View index**.
   ![The twelve indicator cards on a client's record](/assets/es/clientes/ver-un-cliente/indicadores.png)

## What the screen shows

| Where | Unit | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **FOV** card on the record | **Week** | Laura: 2 Week |
| **Visit frequency** card in **Metrics › Clients** | Groups | **Weekly** 1 · **Biweekly** 4 |

![The Visit frequency card in Metrics with the Weekly and Biweekly groups](/assets/es/metricas/clientes-frecuencia-de-visita/tarjeta.png)

> [!NOTE]
> The exact FOV formula is still to be confirmed with the team: how many visits are needed to calculate it, whether it's an average, and whether it's the “frequency cycle” used by the VIP, Nearly lost and Lost types. Here we only describe what the screen shows and its unit.

## What it's for

- A client with an FOV of 2 weeks who hasn't come in for a month is slipping away. Look for them under **Nearly lost** or in the **Client Risk Alerts**.
- If most of your clients are biweekly, your schedule repeats every two weeks: book the next appointment before the client leaves.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are FOV and AVF the same?**
They come in different units: FOV in weeks and AVF in days ([AVF](/ayuda/glosario/que-es-el-avf)). Whether they're the same data is still to be confirmed.

**Why does Visit frequency say 5 clients if I served 40?**
It only counts the ones that already have a frequency assigned. A client who has come just once doesn't have a known rhythm yet ([How often your clients visit](/ayuda/metricas/clientes-frecuencia-de-visita)).
