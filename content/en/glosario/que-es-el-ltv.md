# LTV: how much a client has spent

**In short:** **LTV** is how much money a client has left at your barbershop since their first appointment. It shows as a column in the **Clients** list and as a card on their record, in the location's currency.

## Where it shows

- **In the list.** **Clients** has the **LTV** column between **Type** and **Phone**. The table sorts when you tap the column title: that's how you see who has spent the most.
- **On the record.** **Actions › View**, **General information** tab, **LTV** card.
  ![The twelve indicator cards, with LTV among them](/assets/es/clientes/ver-un-cliente/indicadores.png)

## What the screen shows

| Where | Example |
|---|---|
| **LTV** column in the list | $1,250,000.00 |
| **LTV** card on the record | $1,250,000.00 |
| **Average sale** card (next to it) | $52,000.00 |

**LTV** is the total; **Average sale** is how much they spend each time they're charged. With **Number of transactions** you see how many times that has been.

> [!NOTE]
> Still to confirm with the team: whether LTV includes products and tips as well as services, whether it subtracts refunds, and whether it's calculated per location or for the whole barbershop. Here we only describe what the screen shows.

## What it's for

- **High LTV and low Average sale**: they come often and spend little each time. Offer them an extra service or a product.
- **Low LTV and high Average sale**: they spend well but come rarely. Book their next appointment before they leave.
- Sort the list by **LTV** to know who to take care of first.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it the same as Average income per client in Metrics?**
No. That card is an average across all clients in a period ([Average revenue per client](/ayuda/metricas/clientes-ingreso-promedio-por-cliente)). LTV is one client's total.

**Where do I see the payments that make up the LTV?**
On the record, **Payment methods** tab: the table of transactions with **Date**, **Type**, **Amount** and **View details** ([A client's payments](/ayuda/clientes/pagos-de-un-cliente)).
