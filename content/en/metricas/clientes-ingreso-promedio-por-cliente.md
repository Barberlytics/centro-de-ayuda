# Average revenue per client

**In short:** how much each client brings in on average during the period. A client who comes three times a month and buys product raises this number; one who comes once and only gets a haircut lowers it.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › Clients**, in the top row, next to **Retention rate**. It responds to the location, **Whole team** (or one barber) and period filters. With the barber profile it's the average for your clients.

## What it shows

- **AVERAGE REVENUE / CLIENT**: an amount of money, for example **$95,000.00**.

It's an average per client, not per payment. If a client came twice in the month and paid $40,000 each time, they add $80,000 to this average.

> [!NOTE]
> Exactly how it's calculated (what counts as revenue and which clients are included): still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- It's the number that goes up when you sell more per visit or when clients come more often.
- If it's much higher than the **Average ticket** in **Business**, your clients come back within the period. If they're almost the same, most come only once.
- Compare it by barber to see who sells product and combos, and who only does the haircut.

## Frequently asked questions

**How is it different from the Average ticket?**
The [Average ticket](/ayuda/metricas/ticket-promedio-cancelacion-y-propinas) is per payment. This one is per client: it adds up everything that client paid in the period.

**Why does it change so much between Today, Week and Month?**
Because with **Month** the same client can come several times and it all adds up. With **Today** it's almost always a single visit per client.
