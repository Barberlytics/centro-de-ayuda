# Online bookings and walk-in rate

**In short:** **Online bookings** tells you what share of appointments the client booked on their own, over the internet. **Walk-in rate** tells you what share of clients showed up without an appointment.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › Clients**, in the top row. They respond to the location, **Whole team** (or one barber) and period filters. With the barber profile you only see your own appointments.

## What it shows

### Online bookings

- **ONLINE BOOKINGS**: a percentage, for example **20.0%**.
- **vs. in person**: the label below. It compares appointments booked online with the ones booked at the barbershop.

For example, **20.0%** means one in five appointments was booked by the client through your link or your page.

![Online bookings card with the percentage and the vs. in person label](/assets/es/metricas/clientes-reservas-online-y-rate-walk-in/reservas-online.png)

### Walk-in rate

- **WALK-IN RATE**: a percentage, for example **15.0%**.
- **Walk-in**: the label below. These are the clients who came to the barbershop without booking.

![Walk-in rate card with the percentage and the Walk-in label](/assets/es/metricas/clientes-reservas-online-y-rate-walk-in/rate-walk-in.png)

> [!NOTE]
> What total each percentage is calculated over: still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- If **Online bookings** is low, your clients are still messaging you on WhatsApp to ask for an appointment. Put your online booking link on your Instagram and in your welcome message.
- The more online booking goes up, the less time your team spends on the phone.
- A high **Walk-in rate** tells you that you depend on people walking through the door. It's good for filling gaps, but it doesn't let you plan the day.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do I see how many appointments were online, not the percentage?**
In **Business**, in the [Booking method](/ayuda/metricas/metodo-de-agendamiento-y-citas) donut: **Online appointment** has the number.

**Is this card the same as the one in Marketing?**
Yes. **Online bookings** also appears on the **Marketing** tab with the same percentage. There it comes with the channels those appointments came through.

**Does a walk-in count as an appointment?**
Yes. When you serve a client without an appointment, it's recorded as **Walk-in** and counts toward the **Walk-in rate**.
