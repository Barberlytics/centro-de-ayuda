# How to read Metrics: tabs, filters and periods

**In short:** **Metrics** is the screen Barberlytics opens with (the first icon in the menu, **Home**). It answers “how is my barbershop doing” with cards of numbers and charts, spread across five tabs: **Business**, **Clients**, **Barbers**, **Marketing** and **AI Insights**. At the top you choose the location, the barber and the period, and all the cards change at once.

![The Metrics screen on the Business tab, with the filters at the top and the number cards](/assets/es/metricas/como-leer-metricas/negocio.png)

## The screen, from top to bottom

1. **The title** “Metrics” and the line “In this section you can have full control of your team”.
2. **The five tabs**. The active one is shown in blue and underlined.
   ![The Business, Clients, Barbers, Marketing and AI Insights tabs](/assets/es/metricas/como-leer-metricas/pestanas.png)
3. **The filter bar**: the location, **Whole team** (or one barber), the **Today** / **Week** / **Month** buttons and the period with two arrows. How to use it is in [Filter by location, by barber and by period](/ayuda/metricas/filtrar-por-sucursal-equipo-y-periodo).
4. **The cards**. Each one has a title in capital letters, a big number or a chart, and sometimes a small line that clarifies what is counted (“Per transaction”, “Canceled + No-show”).

## What each tab answers

### Business: how much you sold and how

| Card | The question it answers | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **Average ticket**, **Cancellation rate**, **Tip %** | How much does each payment bring in? How many appointments are lost? How much do they leave as a tip? | [Average ticket, cancellation rate and tip %](/ayuda/metricas/ticket-promedio-cancelacion-y-propinas) |
| **Sales**, **Revenue** | How much came in and from where: services, products, taxes, tips, fees? | [Sales and revenue](/ayuda/metricas/ventas-e-ingresos) |
| **Booking method**, **Appointments** | How do appointments come in (internal, online, walk-in) and what status do they end in? | [Booking method and appointments](/ayuda/metricas/metodo-de-agendamiento-y-citas) |
| **Appointment request hours**, **Traffic by time of day** | At what times do people look for you? | [Appointment request hours](/ayuda/metricas/horas-de-solicitud-de-citas) |
| **Payment distribution**, **Transactions**, **Refunds**, **Discounts** | What do they pay you with? How much did you refund or discount? | [Payment distribution, refunds and discounts](/ayuda/metricas/distribucion-de-pagos-reembolsos-y-descuentos) |
| **Sales by service**, **Physical sales**, **Product sales** | Which services and products sell the most? | [Sales by service, physical sales and product sales](/ayuda/metricas/ventas-por-servicio-y-de-productos) |

### Clients: who comes in and whether they come back

![The Clients tab of Metrics](/assets/es/metricas/como-leer-metricas/clientes.png)

**Retention rate**, **Average revenue / client**, **Online bookings**, **Walk-in rate**, **Barber preference**, **Appointments by client type**, **New vs returning** and **Visit frequency**. Start with [Retention rate and returning clients](/ayuda/metricas/clientes-tasa-de-retencion).

### Barbers: how much each one brings in

![The Barbers tab of Metrics](/assets/es/metricas/como-leer-metricas/barberos.png)

**Team net total**, **Barber loyalty** and the list of barbers sorted by what each one generated. It's explained in [Team net total and barber loyalty](/ayuda/metricas/total-neto-del-equipo).

### Marketing: where online bookings come from

![The Marketing tab of Metrics](/assets/es/metricas/como-leer-metricas/marketing.png)

**Online bookings**, **Best channel**, **Digital channels**, **Campaign performance** and **Conversion by channel**. It's explained in [Marketing: digital channels, campaigns and conversion](/ayuda/metricas/marketing-canales-y-campanas).

### AI Insights: what's happening and what to do

![The AI Insights tab, with the Health Score, the alerts, the Capacity Map and the recommendations](/assets/es/metricas/como-leer-metricas/ai-insights.png)

Four blocks: **Health Score** (from 0 to 100), **Client Risk Alerts**, **Capacity Map** and **Smart Recommendations**. On this tab the only filter is the location: there's no barber or period, because the recommendations are calculated “over the last 3 months”. Start with [Your barbershop's Health Score](/ayuda/metricas/puntaje-de-salud).

## Details that help you read it right

- **The titles in capital letters** are the card's name. In this help center we quote them as they are.
- **A donut** (circle) splits a total into parts; the number in the center is the total. Next to it, the legend gives each part with its number or percentage.
- **A stacked bar** does the same horizontally (for example, Morning / Afternoon / Evening).
- **The lists** (“Sales by service · 14”) go from highest to lowest and the number in the title is the total.
- A **“—”** means there's no data yet for that card (for example, **Best channel** with no online bookings).
- Next to some AI Insights titles there's an **ⓘ** icon; its help text isn't documented yet.

## If you sign in with the barber profile

With the barber profile you only see three tabs: **Business**, **Clients** and **Barbers**. The barber filter doesn't appear and the numbers are that person's, not the whole team's. To switch profiles, see [Switch roles](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/cambiar-de-rol).

## Frequently asked questions

**Are the numbers for the whole barbershop or for one location?**
For the location you chose in the filter. If you have several, the same filter offers **All locations**.

**Why doesn't my barber see Marketing or AI Insights?**
Because with the barber profile Metrics only has **Business**, **Clients** and **Barbers**.

**Do the numbers in Metrics match Transactions and Payroll?**
Metrics summarizes the period you chose; **Transactions** lists each payment and **Payroll** calculates what is paid out. If a number doesn't add up, first check that all three have the same date range and the same location.

**Can I download Metrics?**
There's no download button on this screen. **Payroll** can be downloaded (CSV, Excel or PDF).
