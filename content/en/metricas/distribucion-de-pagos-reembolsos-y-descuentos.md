# Payment distribution, refunds and discounts

**In short:** four cards about how you got paid. **Payment distribution** tells you what your clients paid with. **Transactions** counts the payments. **Refunds** and **Discounts** tell you how much you refunded and how much you discounted.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › Business**, below **Appointment request hours**. They respond to the location, **Whole team** (or one barber) and period filters. With the barber profile you only see your own payments.

## What it shows

### Payment distribution

- **PAYMENT DISTRIBUTION**: the total figure, for example **$8,500,000.00**, with the **Revenue** label.
- A donut with one slice for each payment method and its amount, for example **Cash $6,000,000.00** and the rest on card or transfer.

If only one method appears, every payment in that period was recorded with it.

![Payment distribution donut with the total Revenue and the amount for each payment method](/assets/es/metricas/distribucion-de-pagos-reembolsos-y-descuentos/distribucion-de-pagos.png)

### Transactions

- **TRANSACTIONS**: the number of payments in the period, for example **18**.

A transaction is one payment. If a client paid for a haircut and a product together, that's just one.

![Transactions card with the number of payments](/assets/es/metricas/distribucion-de-pagos-reembolsos-y-descuentos/transacciones.png)

### Refunds

- **REFUNDS**: the money you gave back, for example **$40,000.00**, and its percentage, for example **0.47 %**.

With **$0.00 · 0.00 %** there were no refunds in the period.

![Refunds card with the amount and the percentage](/assets/es/metricas/distribucion-de-pagos-reembolsos-y-descuentos/reembolsos.png)

### Discounts

- **DISCOUNTS**: the money you discounted, for example **$120,000.00**, and its percentage, for example **1.41 %**.

With **$0.00 · 0.00 %** you didn't apply any discounts in the period.

![Discounts card with the amount and the percentage](/assets/es/metricas/distribucion-de-pagos-reembolsos-y-descuentos/descuentos.png)

> [!NOTE]
> What base the **Refunds** and **Discounts** percentages are calculated on: still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- If almost everything is cash, compare it with what's in the register at closing. It's the fastest way to spot a payment that wasn't recorded.
- If discounts go up, check who is applying them and why. A discount with no active promotion is money leaving without control.
- A refund always has a story behind it: a service the client didn't like, a double charge. Look for it in **Transactions**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Transactions the same as appointments?**
No. **Transactions** counts payments. An appointment can have a payment, or a walk-in client can generate a payment. The number of appointments is in [Booking method and appointments](/ayuda/metricas/metodo-de-agendamiento-y-citas).

**Why do I only see Cash in the donut?**
Because every payment in that period was recorded as cash. If you charged by card and it doesn't show up, check how the payment was recorded when you charged the appointment.

**Where do I see the detail of each refund?**
In the **Transactions** section of the menu, not in Metrics. Here you only see the total.
