# Filter by location, by barber and by period

**In short:** the **Filters** bar is below the **Metrics** tabs. It has four controls: the location, **Whole team**, the **Today** / **Week** / **Month** buttons and the period with arrows. Change one and all the cards on the tab are recalculated.

![The filter bar: location, Whole team, Today / Week / Month and the month with arrows](/assets/es/metricas/filtrar-por-sucursal-equipo-y-periodo/filtros.png)

## 1. Choose the location

1. Tap the first button, the one with the name of your location.
2. Choose another location, or **All locations** at the end of the list to see the numbers together.
   ![The location dropdown open, with the locations and the All locations option](/assets/es/metricas/filtrar-por-sucursal-equipo-y-periodo/sucursal.png)

## 2. See the whole team or a single barber

1. Tap **Whole team**.
2. Choose a barber. All the cards switch to showing only their numbers: their sales, their appointments, their clients.
   ![The Whole team dropdown open, with the list of barbers](/assets/es/metricas/filtrar-por-sucursal-equipo-y-periodo/equipo.png)
3. To go back, choose **Whole team** again.

The list shows the barbers who are active at the chosen location (see [The team belongs to the company and is activated in each location](/ayuda/sucursales/equipo-compartido-entre-sucursales)).

## 3. Choose the period

Tap **Today**, **Week** or **Month**. The chosen button turns blue and the text next to it changes to the current period (for example, “September 2026” with **Month**).

![The three buttons Today, Week and Month](/assets/es/metricas/filtrar-por-sucursal-equipo-y-periodo/periodo.png)

## 4. Move to another period

With the **‹** and **›** arrows you go to the previous or the next period: yesterday, last week, last month.

![The period with its arrows](/assets/es/metricas/filtrar-por-sucursal-equipo-y-periodo/mes.png)

> [!TIP]
> To compare two months, look at the cards with **Month**, note down the ones you care about, tap **‹** and look at them again.

## Where they don't apply

- On **AI Insights** there is only the location filter: no barber or period, because that tab always looks at the last 3 months.
- With the barber profile, **Whole team** doesn't appear: the numbers are always that person's.

> [!NOTE]
> Exactly which days **Week** covers (whether it starts on Monday or on the day you set in **Settings › Business details › Week start**) isn't confirmed yet. There's no option to type your own date range.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I see Metrics for all my locations at once?**
Choose **All locations** in the location filter in Metrics.

**How do I see just one barber?**
Change **Whole team** to the barber you want to see.

**How do I see last month?**
Choose **Month** and tap the **‹** arrow.

**I changed the filter and the numbers didn't change.**
That barber or that location may have no activity in the period. Try **Month** or another period.
