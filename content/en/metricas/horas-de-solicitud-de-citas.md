# Appointment request hours: morning, afternoon and evening

**In short:** this card tells you what time of day you get the most appointments. At the top, split into morning, afternoon and evening; below, hour by hour.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › Business**, below **Booking method** and **Appointments**. It responds to the location, **Whole team** (or one barber) and period filters. With the barber profile you only see your own appointments.

## What it shows

### The three time slots

- **APPOINTMENT REQUEST HOURS**: a three-color bar with the number of appointments and the percentage for each slot. Each slot has its icon (sunrise, sun and moon):
  - **Morning**: for example **15 Appointments · 38%**.
  - **Afternoon**: for example **5 Appointments · 12%**.
  - **Evening**: for example **20 Appointments · 50%**.

![Appointment request hours bar with the Morning, Afternoon and Evening slots](/assets/es/metricas/horas-de-solicitud-de-citas/horas.png)

### Traffic by time of day

- **Traffic by time of day**: an hour-by-hour chart, from **03:00 am** to **11:00 pm**.
- As you move over each hour you see the number of appointments, for example **09:00 am | 1 Appointments** or **06:00 pm | 4 Appointments**.

![Traffic by time of day chart with the number of appointments per hour](/assets/es/metricas/horas-de-solicitud-de-citas/trafico.png)

> [!NOTE]
> What time each slot (Morning, Afternoon and Evening) starts and ends, and whether the time is the appointment's or the moment it was requested: still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- The hours with the most appointments are your peak hours: that's when you need the whole team in their chairs.
- The hours with few appointments are the ones you can fill with a promotion, for example a special price between 2 and 4 in the afternoon.
- If a barber's chart is empty in a slot where the barbershop does have appointments, check their schedule.
- To see the same thing by day of the week, look at the [Capacity Map](/ayuda/metricas/mapa-de-capacidad) in **AI Insights**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the time the appointment's or the time the client requested it?**
The card is called **Appointment request hours** and the chart **Traffic by time of day**. Which of the two times is used is still to be confirmed.

**Why does the chart start at 3 in the morning?**
The chart always runs from **03:00 am** to **11:00 pm**, whether or not you have appointments at those hours. Hours with no appointments show as zero.

**Can I see this for a single barber?**
Yes. Change **Whole team** to the barber and the card shows only their appointments.
