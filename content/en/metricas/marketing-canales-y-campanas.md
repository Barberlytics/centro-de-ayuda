# Marketing: digital channels, campaigns and conversion

**In short:** the **Marketing** tab tells you where online appointments come from: what share of the total they are, which channel they came through (direct link, website), which campaign brought them and how much each channel converts.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › Marketing**. It responds to the location, **Whole team** (or one barber) and period filters. With the barber profile this tab doesn't appear.

## What it shows

### Online bookings

- **ONLINE BOOKINGS**: a percentage, for example **20.0%**, with the **vs. in person** label. It's the same number as in **Clients**.

![Online bookings card with the percentage and the vs. in person label](/assets/es/metricas/marketing-canales-y-campanas/reservas-online.png)

### Best channel

- **BEST CHANNEL**: the channel through which the most online appointments came, for example **website**. If there isn't enough data it shows **—**.

![Best channel card](/assets/es/metricas/marketing-canales-y-campanas/mejor-canal.png)

### Digital channels

- **DIGITAL CHANNELS**: at the top, the total of online appointments, for example **8 ONLINE APPOINTMENTS**.
- Below, each channel with its number of appointments:
  - **direct_link**: the client came in through your direct link (the one you share on WhatsApp or Instagram).
  - **website**: the client came in from your website.

For example: **direct_link 5 · website 3**.

![Digital channels card with the total of online appointments and the direct_link and website rows](/assets/es/metricas/marketing-canales-y-campanas/canales-digitales.png)

### Campaign performance

- **CAMPAIGN PERFORMANCE**: at the top, the total of appointments, for example **8 APPOINTMENTS**.
- Below, each campaign with its appointments. Appointments that didn't come through any campaign appear as **No campaign**.

![Campaign performance card with the total of appointments and the No campaign row](/assets/es/metricas/marketing-canales-y-campanas/campanas.png)

### Conversion by channel

- **CONVERSION BY CHANNEL**: a percentage per channel, for example **direct_link 12.5%** and **website 8.0%**.

![Conversion by channel card with the direct_link and website percentage](/assets/es/metricas/marketing-canales-y-campanas/conversion.png)

> [!NOTE]
> How each channel's conversion is calculated (what counts as a visit and what counts as an appointment), how an appointment is assigned to a campaign and when **Best channel** stops showing “—”: still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- If almost everything comes through **direct_link**, your WhatsApp and Instagram are working. If **website** is at zero, check that the booking button on your page leads to your link.
- **No campaign** with all the appointments tells you that you're not measuring your promotions. If you run a campaign, look at it here to see whether it brought in appointments.
- A conversion of **0.0%** with appointments on the channel is odd: how it's calculated is still to be confirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is direct_link?**
Your barbershop's direct online booking link, the one you share on social media and in messages. **website** is your web page.

**Why does Best channel show “—”?**
Because there isn't enough data in that period to pick one. Switch to **Month** or move with the arrows.

**How do I create a campaign so it shows up here?**
Where campaigns are created and how an appointment is linked to them is still to be confirmed.
