# Booking method and appointments: internal, online and walk-in

**In short:** **Booking method** tells you where appointments came from: your team booked them, the client booked them online, or the client showed up without an appointment. **Appointments** tells you how they ended: completed, pending payment, canceled or the client didn't come.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › Business**, below **Sales** and **Revenue**. They respond to the location, **Whole team** (or one barber) and period filters. With the barber profile you only see your own appointments.

## What it shows

### Booking method

- **BOOKING METHOD**: in the center of the donut, the total, for example **40 Appointments**.
- Three parts:
  - **Internal**: appointments booked by someone on your team from the calendar.
  - **Online appointment**: appointments booked by the client through your link or your page.
  - **Walk-in**: clients who came to the barbershop without booking.

For example: **Internal 27 · Online appointment 6 · Walk-in 7**.

![Booking method donut with Internal, Online appointment and Walk-in](/assets/es/metricas/metodo-de-agendamiento-y-citas/metodo-de-agendamiento.png)

### Appointments

- **APPOINTMENTS**: in the center, the number of clients, for example **9 Clients**.
- Four parts:
  - **Completed**: appointments that were served and paid.
  - **Pending payment**: served appointments that haven't been paid yet.
  - **Canceled**: appointments that were canceled.
  - **No-show**: appointments where the client didn't come.

For example: **Completed 9 · Pending payment 0 · Canceled 0 · No-show 0**.

![Appointments donut with Completed, Pending payment, Canceled and No-show](/assets/es/metricas/metodo-de-agendamiento-y-citas/citas.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Why the center of **Appointments** counts clients and not appointments, and how a client with several appointments in the period is counted: still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- If almost everything is **Internal**, your clients aren't using online booking. Share your link on Instagram and WhatsApp.
- If **Walk-in** is high, you have clients who show up to try their luck. That's fine, but they're the ones most likely to leave if there's a line.
- If **Pending payment** isn't at zero when you close the day, there are served appointments nobody charged. Check them in the calendar.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between Internal and Online appointment?**
**Internal** is when someone on your team books the appointment. **Online appointment** is when the client books it on their own, through your link or your page.

**Does a client who came without an appointment count as an appointment?**
Yes. They appear in **Walk-in** within **Booking method** and, once paid, in **Completed**.

**Where do I see the percentage of online appointments versus in-person ones?**
On the **Clients** tab, in [Online bookings and walk-in rate](/ayuda/metricas/clientes-reservas-online-y-rate-walk-in).
