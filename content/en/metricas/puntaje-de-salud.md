# Your barbershop's Health Score

**In short:** the **Health Score** is a grade from 0 to 100 that sums up how your barbershop is doing. Below it you see the four figures that go with it: how many appointments are completed, how many clients come back, how many don't show up and how much each payment brings in.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › AI Insights**, the first card. With the barber profile this tab doesn't appear.

## What it shows

- **Health Score**: the big number out of 100, for example **72 /100**.
- Below, four figures:
  - **Completion rate**: for example **65.0%**. The share of appointments that were served all the way through.
  - **Retention rate**: for example **70.0%**. The share of clients who come back.
  - **Didn't attend**: for example **5.0%**. The share of appointments where the client didn't come.
  - **Avg. ticket**: for example **$52000.00**. What each payment brings in on average.

![Health Score card with the grade out of 100 and the four figures below](/assets/es/metricas/puntaje-de-salud/tarjeta.png)

> [!NOTE]
> How the score is calculated, how much each figure weighs, what period it covers (the **Smart Recommendations** talk about the last 3 months) and what grade counts as good: still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- It's the number you check on a Monday to know whether last week was good or bad, without going through all the cards.
- If it drops, look at which of the four figures fell: **Didn't attend** takes you to cancellations, **Retention rate** to the at-risk clients, **Avg. ticket** to what you sell per payment.
- Don't compare it with another barbershop's. Compare it with yourself, month to month.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why doesn't the average ticket here match the one in Business?**
The one in **Business** follows the filters at the top (**Today**, **Week** or **Month**). The one in **AI Insights** may cover another period. Which one is still to be confirmed.

**What's a good score?**
The screen doesn't say. Use it as a reference to see whether you're improving or getting worse month to month.

**Can the score be raised quickly?**
What weighs most day to day is that appointments get completed and clients don't miss them. Confirming appointments the day before moves **Didn't attend**, and you can see the difference.
