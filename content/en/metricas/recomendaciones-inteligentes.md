# Smart Recommendations: where they come from and what to do with them

**In short:** they're tips the app draws from your own data. Each one tells you what's happening, why it matters and what to do. The screen puts it this way: “AI-generated recommendations based on your business's data patterns over the last 3 months.”

## Where it is

In **Metrics › AI Insights**, at the bottom of the tab. With the barber profile this tab doesn't appear.

## What it shows

- **Smart Recommendations**: the recommendations grouped in three blocks, with the number in each: **BUSINESS**, **CLIENTS** and **GROWTH**. For example, **BUSINESS (1)**, **CLIENTS (2)**, **GROWTH (3)**.
- Each recommendation has:
  - A category: **CAPACITY**, **RETENTION** or **MARKETING**.
  - A priority: **!**, **!!** or **!!!**. The more marks, the more urgent.
  - A headline with the figure, for example “4 clients at risk of being lost”.
  - An explanation, for example “These clients haven't visited in 1.5x their usual frequency. They could switch to another barbershop.”
  - A suggested action, for example “Send a reminder campaign or special offer”.

Other examples you may see:

| Headline | Suggested action |
|---|---|
| “Imbalance: busiest barber has 47 appointments, least busy has 2” | “Adjust the order of the no-preference queue or redistribute walk-ins” |
| “11 registered clients have never booked” | “Send a welcome offer for their first visit” |
| “Sat has 92% fewer bookings than Mon” | “Launch a 'Sat Special' campaign” |

It may also alert you to the difference between weekdays and weekends, for example “Weekdays have 1630% more demand per day than weekends”.

> [!NOTE]
> What rule assigns the priority (!, !! or !!!), how often the recommendations are updated and whether they respond to the filters at the top: still to confirm with the team. Days of the week may appear abbreviated (“Sat”, “Mon”).

## What it's for

- Start with the **!!!** ones. They're the ones costing you the most money.
- Each recommendation points to another card: **RETENTION** ones to [Client Risk Alerts](/ayuda/metricas/alertas-de-riesgo-de-clientes); **CAPACITY** ones to the [Capacity Map](/ayuda/metricas/mapa-de-capacidad) and to [Barber preference](/ayuda/metricas/clientes-preferencia-de-barbero); **MARKETING** ones to the **Marketing** tab.
- The suggested action is a starting point, not an order. You know your barbershop: if a “Saturday Special” doesn't suit your clientele, try something else that day.
- They're based on the last 3 months. If you've just opened or switched systems, wait until you have data before acting on them.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do the recommendations change with the filters at the top?**
Still to be confirmed. The screen says they use data from the last 3 months.

**What do the “!”, “!!” and “!!!” marks mean?**
They're the priority: the more marks, the more urgent. The exact rule is still to be confirmed.

**Can I mark a recommendation as done or dismiss it?**
The card only shows them. When the data behind it changes (for example, the at-risk clients come back), the recommendation stops appearing.
