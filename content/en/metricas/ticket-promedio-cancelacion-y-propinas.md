# Average ticket, cancellation rate and tip %

**In short:** these are the three cards at the top of **Business**. They tell you how much each payment brings in on average, what share of appointments are canceled or the client doesn't come, and what share of what you collected is tips.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › Business**, in the first row. All three respond to the filters at the top: the location, **Whole team** (or one barber) and the period (**Today**, **Week** or **Month**). With the barber profile you only see your own numbers.

## What it shows

### Average ticket

- **AVERAGE TICKET**: the big number, for example **$45,000.00**.
- **Per transaction**: the label below. It's the average per payment, not per client or per appointment.

If you charged 100 times in the month and the average ticket is $45,000, each payment brought in $45,000 on average. If a client took a haircut, a beard trim and a wax in a single payment, it counts as one transaction.

![Average ticket card with the number and the Per transaction label](/assets/es/metricas/ticket-promedio-cancelacion-y-propinas/ticket-promedio.png)

### Cancellation rate

- **CANCELLATION RATE**: a percentage, for example **4.5%**.
- **Canceled + No-show**: the label below. It adds up canceled appointments and appointments where the client didn't show up.

**0.0%** means that in that period no appointment was canceled and nobody missed theirs.

![Cancellation rate card with the percentage and the Canceled + No-show label](/assets/es/metricas/ticket-promedio-cancelacion-y-propinas/tasa-de-cancelacion.png)

### Tip %

- **TIP %**: a percentage, for example **8.0%**. It's the share of what you collected that was tips.

If it shows **0.0%**, no tips were recorded on payments in that period.

![Tip % card with the percentage](/assets/es/metricas/ticket-promedio-cancelacion-y-propinas/propinas.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Exactly how each of the three is calculated: still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- If the average ticket drops, check whether fewer combos or fewer products are being sold per appointment. If it rises, something you changed is working.
- If the cancellation rate goes up, look at which day and what time appointments fall through and consider confirming by message the day before.
- Compare the month with the previous one using the period arrows before drawing conclusions: one slow week isn't a trend.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the average ticket per client or per payment?**
Per payment. The card says so: **Per transaction**. The per-client average is in **Clients**, in [Average revenue per client](/ayuda/metricas/clientes-ingreso-promedio-por-cliente).

**Does the cancellation rate include the ones who didn't come?**
Yes. The label says **Canceled + No-show**: it counts canceled appointments and appointments where the client didn't show up.

**Why is Tip % at 0.0% if my clients do leave tips?**
The card only counts tips that are recorded on the payment inside Barberlytics. Tips handed over in person and not written down don't show up.
