# Team net total and barber loyalty

**In short:** the **Barbers** tab tells you how much the team produced in the period, how much each barber produced (sorted from highest to lowest) and what share of your clients ask for a specific barber.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › Barbers**. It responds to the location, **Whole team** (or one barber) and period filters. With the barber profile you only see your own bar and your own numbers.

## What it shows

### Team net total

- **TEAM NET TOTAL**: the big number, for example **$8,500,000.00**. It's what the whole team produced in the period.

![Team net total card with the number](/assets/es/metricas/total-neto-del-equipo/total-neto.png)

### Barber loyalty

- **BARBER LOYALTY**: a percentage, for example **85.0%**.
- **Clients with preference**: the label below. It's the share of clients who asked for a specific barber instead of anyone available.

**100.0%** means all the clients in the period asked for their barber.

![Barber loyalty card with the percentage and the Clients with preference label](/assets/es/metricas/total-neto-del-equipo/lealtad.png)

### The list by barber

- Below, another **TEAM NET TOTAL** card with the same total figure and one bar per barber with their amount, sorted from highest to lowest. For example: **Carlos $3,200,000.00 · Laura $2,900,000.00 · Andrés $2,400,000.00**.

![Team net total list with one bar per barber and their amount](/assets/es/metricas/total-neto-del-equipo/por-barbero.png)

> [!NOTE]
> What is subtracted to make the total “net” (discounts, refunds, taxes, tips), and whether it matches the payroll base: still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- It's the fastest view to know who holds the barbershop up and who needs more bookings.
- If a barber is far below the rest, before talking to them check their schedule and their **No preference** appointments: it may be a lack of clients, not a lack of work.
- Don't use it to work out commissions by hand. To pay people, use the **Payroll** section of the menu.
- Look at loyalty together with the list: a barber with a lot of money and clients who ask for them is the one you'd lose the most by losing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the net total what I pay the barber?**
No. It's what they produced. What they're owed according to their commission or rent is in **Payroll**.

**Why does Team net total appear twice?**
The card at the top only shows the number. The one below splits it by barber, with a bar for each person.

**What's the difference between Barber loyalty and Barber preference?**
**Barber loyalty** is the percentage of clients with a preference. [Barber preference](/ayuda/metricas/clientes-preferencia-de-barbero), in **Clients**, counts appointments with and without preference.
