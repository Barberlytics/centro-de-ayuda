# Sales and revenue: services, products, taxes, tips and fees

**In short:** **Sales** tells you how much you sold in the period and what share was services or products. **Revenue** breaks down what that money is made of: services, products, taxes, tips, fees and what was charged to people who didn't show up.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › Business**, below the three cards at the top. They respond to the location, **Whole team** (or one barber) and period (**Today**, **Week** or **Month**) filters. With the barber profile you only see your own.

## What it shows

### Sales

- **SALES**: the big number, for example **$8,500,000.00**, with the **Total** label.
- A donut with two parts: **Services** and **Products**, each with its percentage. For example, **Services 92%** and **Products 8%**.

If Products shows **0%**, you didn't sell any products in that period, or they were charged as part of a service.

![Sales card with the total and the Services and Products donut](/assets/es/metricas/ventas-e-ingresos/ventas.png)

### Revenue

- **REVENUE**: the same total figure, for example **$8,500,000.00**.
- Below, one bar for each component with its amount:
  - **Services**: what was charged for haircuts, beard trims and other services.
  - **Products**: what was charged for products.
  - **Taxes**: the share of taxes included in the payments.
  - **Tips**: the tips recorded on payments.
  - **Fees**: additional charges collected.
  - **No-show**: what was charged for appointments where the client didn't come.

For example: **Services $8,000,000.00 · Products $500,000.00 · Taxes $0.00 · Tips $0.00 · Fees $0.00 · No-show $0.00**.

![Revenue card with the Services, Products, Taxes, Tips, Fees and No-show bars](/assets/es/metricas/ventas-e-ingresos/ingresos.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Exactly how each bar is calculated, and whether the Sales total always matches the Revenue one: still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- Look at the total first and compare it with the previous month using the period arrows.
- If Products weighs very little, that's a sale you're not making: wax, beard oil, shampoo.
- If the **No-show** bar has an amount, it's money you charged for appointments that fell through. If it's zero and you have a lot of no-shows, check whether you're charging for those appointments.

## Frequently asked questions

**Should Sales and Revenue show the same number?**
On screen, both cards show the same total. **Sales** splits it between services and products; **Revenue** breaks it down by component.

**Where do I see which service I sold the most?**
In [Sales by service](/ayuda/metricas/ventas-por-servicio-y-de-productos), further down on the same tab.

**How do I see what each barber sold?**
Change **Whole team** to the barber, or go to the **Barbers** tab and look at [Team net total](/ayuda/metricas/total-neto-del-equipo).
