# Sales by service, physical sales and product sales

**In short:** at the bottom of **Business** there are three lists. **Sales by service** tells you how many times you sold each service. **Physical sales** splits what was sold between gift cards and products. **Product sales** tells you how many units of each product went out.

## Where it is

In **Metrics › Business**, at the bottom of the tab. They respond to the location, **Whole team** (or one barber) and period filters. With the barber profile you only see what you sold yourself.

## What it shows

### Sales by service

- **SALES BY SERVICE**: the total number of services sold, for example **20**.
- Below, a list with each service and how many times it was sold, from highest to lowest. For example: **Haircut 9 · Beard 6 · Haircut + beard 5**.

![Sales by service card with the list of services and their quantity](/assets/es/metricas/ventas-por-servicio-y-de-productos/ventas-por-servicio.png)

### Physical sales

- **PHYSICAL SALES**: two rows with units and percentage:
  - **Gift card**: for example **0 Units · 0%**.
  - **Products**: for example **3 Units · 100%**.

The percentage is the share each type represents within physical sales.

![Physical sales card with Gift card and Products, units and percentage](/assets/es/metricas/ventas-por-servicio-y-de-productos/ventas-fisicas.png)

### Product sales

- **PRODUCT SALES**: the total units sold, for example **3**.
- Below, each product with its units. For example: **Matte wax 2 · Beard oil 1**.

![Product sales card with the list of products and their units](/assets/es/metricas/ventas-por-servicio-y-de-productos/ventas-de-productos.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Whether a combo counts as one service or as each of the services it's made of: still to confirm with the team.

## What it's for

- The service at the top of the list is the one that holds the barbershop up. Look after its price and its time.
- The services at the bottom almost never sell. Think about whether they're worth keeping on the menu or whether the team isn't offering them.
- If **Product sales** stays at zero month after month, your barbers aren't offering product at the end of the haircut.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the list of services show quantity or money?**
Quantity: how many times each service was sold. The money is in [Sales and revenue](/ayuda/metricas/ventas-e-ingresos).

**Why do Physical sales and Product sales show different numbers?**
**Physical sales** splits the units between gift cards and products. **Product sales** only counts products, and breaks them down one by one.

**Can I see which services each barber sells?**
Yes. Change **Whole team** to the barber and the three lists show only their sales.
