# Search for an appointment with the magnifying glass

**In short:** the magnifying glass at the top, next to the bell, opens the **Search** screen. It has a single field for searching appointments.

## Steps

1. Tap the magnifying glass at the top right.
   ![The header with the magnifying glass highlighted](/assets/es/navegacion/buscar-una-cita/lupa.png)
2. The **Search** screen opens, with a search field.
   ![The Search screen with its field](/assets/es/navegacion/buscar-una-cita/pantalla.png)
3. Type what you're looking for.

> [!NOTE]
> This article was written from the empty screen. We haven't confirmed what the field finds (whether it searches by client name, phone number, service or date), how the results are shown or what happens when you tap one. It will be completed with test appointments.

## Other ways to find an appointment

While the search is being documented, these routes are confirmed to work:

| You know | Go to |
|---|---|
| The day | **Calendar**, move with **‹ Previous day** and **Next day ›** or with the date picker, and tap the card ([Open an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/abrir-una-cita)) |
| The client | **Clients**, find them, **Actions › View** and open the **Appointments** tab: one card per appointment with **View** ([A client's appointments](/ayuda/clientes/citas-de-un-cliente)) |
| That it was already paid | **Transactions**, with the magnifying glass on that screen ([Search for a transaction](/ayuda/transacciones/buscar-una-transaccion)) |
| That payment is pending | **Calendar › Actions › Payment list**, **To collect** tab |

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the magnifying glass search for clients?**
The screen is called **Search** and its address refers to appointments. For clients, use the search box in the **Clients** section ([Find a client](/ayuda/clientes/buscar-un-cliente)).

**Does it search all locations?**
Still to confirm. The safest thing is to pick, at the top, the location where you think the appointment is.
