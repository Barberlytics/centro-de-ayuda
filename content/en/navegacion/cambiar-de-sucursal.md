# Switch locations

**In short:** at the top, next to the magnifying glass, is the name of your location. Tap it to choose another one or to see **All locations**.

## Steps

1. Tap the location name at the top right.
   ![The location selector open](/assets/es/navegacion/cambiar-de-sucursal/selector.png)
2. Choose the location you want to see, or **All locations** at the end of the list.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I create a new location?**
In **Settings › Locations**, with the **Create Location** button.

**How do I see how many people each location has?**
In **Settings › Locations**: the list shows the team, the country, the city and the status of each one.
