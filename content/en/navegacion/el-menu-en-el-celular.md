# The menu on your phone: Home, Calendar, Team, Clients and More

**In short:** on a narrow screen the side menu turns into a bar at the bottom with five buttons. The first four are shortcuts; the fifth, **More**, opens the full menu for your profile.

## The bottom bar

![The phone's bottom bar with Home, Calendar, Team, Clients and More](/assets/es/navegacion/el-menu-en-el-celular/barra.png)

| Profile | Buttons |
|---|---|
| Owner (**Business**) | **Home** · **Calendar** · **Team** · **Clients** · **More** |
| Commission barber (**Barber comission**) | **Home** · **Calendar** · **Expenses** · **Clients** · **More** |

**Home** opens Metrics. The bar changes with the profile because the menu is built from that profile's permissions.

## More: the full menu

1. Tap **More**.
   ![The full menu opened from More](/assets/es/navegacion/el-menu-en-el-celular/mas.png)
2. All the sections for your profile open, the same ones as in the side menu: for the owner, Home, Calendar, Clients, Services & Products, Team, Expenses, Payroll, Transactions and Settings.
3. Tap the section you're looking for.

## The calendar on your phone

![The calendar on a phone screen](/assets/es/navegacion/el-menu-en-el-celular/calendario.png)

The bottom bar stays in place while you work in the **Calendar**. The calendar's toolbar (the **Team\*** filter, the date, the view and **Actions**) is at the top, just like on the big screen.

## The header

The location selector, the magnifying glass, the bell, the language and your account are still at the top. From your account you switch profiles, turn on **Dark mode** and sign out.

## Frequently asked questions

**I can't find Settings.**
Tap **More**. Settings is at the end of the full menu.

**My barber sees Expenses in the bar and I see Team.**
That's normal. Each profile gets shortcuts to what it uses most; the commission barber doesn't have the **Team** button, but can still get to Team from **More**.

**Is it an app you install?**
The help center documents the app in the browser (`dash.barberlytics.com`), which adapts to the width of the screen. If there is an app to install, it isn't documented here.
