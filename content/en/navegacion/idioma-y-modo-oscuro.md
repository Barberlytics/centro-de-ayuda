# Change the language and turn on dark mode

**In short:** you change the language with the globe icon at the top right, and dark mode with the switch in your account menu.

## Change the language

1. Tap the globe icon at the top right.
2. Choose **ES** for Spanish or **EN** for English.

## Turn on dark mode

1. Tap your account icon at the top right.
2. Turn on the **Dark mode** switch. To go back to light mode, turn it off.
   ![The Dark mode switch in the account menu](/assets/es/navegacion/idioma-y-modo-oscuro/modo-oscuro.png)

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change the language before signing in?**
Yes. The sign-in screen also has a language selector at the top right.
