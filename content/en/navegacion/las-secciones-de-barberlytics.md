# The sections of Barberlytics and what each one is for

**In short:** the menu on the left has nine places. Here's what you'll find in each one, from top to bottom.

![The menu on the left of Barberlytics](/assets/es/navegacion/las-secciones-de-barberlytics/menu.png)

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Metrics** | How your barbershop is doing: sales, clients, barbers, marketing and recommendations. It has five tabs: **Business**, **Clients**, **Barbers**, **Marketing** and **AI Insights**. |
| **Calendar** | The day's appointments by barber. From **Actions** you create appointments, quick sales and walk-in sales, and see the payment list. |
| **Clients** | All your clients, grouped by type: New, Retained, Frequent, VIP, Nearly lost, Lost, Recovered and Blocked. |
| **Services** | Your services, their categories and your combos, with their prices. |
| **Products** | What you sell at the barbershop, with its category and price. |
| **Team** | Your barbers, their levels, their working hours and the managers. |
| **Expenses** | What the barbershop spends, fixed or variable. |
| **Payroll** | What each barber is paid, depending on whether you pay by commission, rent or salary. |
| **Transactions** | Everything charged and everything still pending, with its status and payment method. |
| **Settings** | The barbershop's settings, in four groups: account, team, sales and notifications. |

**Services** and **Products** sit together in one group of the menu, as two options of the same dropdown.

## The controls at the top

At the top of every screen there are, from left to right: the menu button, the location selector, the magnifying glass
for searching appointments, the notification bell, the language selector and your account icon.

## Frequently asked questions

**I don't see some of these sections.**
What each person sees depends on their role. The owner can change it in **Settings › Permissions**.
