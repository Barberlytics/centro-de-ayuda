# See your notifications

**In short:** tap the bell at the top right. The **Notifications** panel opens with two tabs: **All** and **Unread**.

## Steps

1. Tap the bell at the top right.
   ![The notifications panel](/assets/es/navegacion/notificaciones/panel.png)
2. Read your alerts. Each one starts with the module in bold, for example **Agenda:**, followed by the message, and
   the date at the bottom.
3. The ones you haven't read have a red dot on the right. Tap **Unread** to see only those.
4. Close the panel with the **X** at the top right.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I choose which alerts I get?**
Yes. The owner sets that in **Settings › Notification center**, alert by alert and by channel: email or push, for each role.
