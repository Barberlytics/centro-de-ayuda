# The pay cycle: weekly, biweekly or monthly

**In short:** in **Settings › Payroll** you pick the **Pay cycle** (weekly, biweekly or monthly) and the **Cycle start day**. With that, **Payroll** opens already on the period you need to pay.

## Steps

1. Tap **Settings** in the menu.
2. In the **Team** group, tap **Payroll** (spelled “Nomina,” without the accent, in the app).
3. Under **Pay cycle\***, pick **weekly**, **biweekly** (the app shows it as “BIWEEKLY”) or **monthly**.
4. Under **Cycle start day**, pick the day each period begins.
5. Save.

## How it looks in Payroll

The **Date range** in **Payroll** comes set to the cycle's period: for example, with a biweekly cycle you see “01 SEP - 15 SEP · 15 Days.” With the **‹ ›** arrows you move to the previous or next period ([Filter payroll](/ayuda/nomina/filtrar-la-nomina-por-fechas-y-sucursal)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the cycle for the whole company or for each location?**
**Settings › Payroll** is among the company-wide settings. In **Payroll** you pick the location, but the cycle is the same for all of them ([General and per-location settings](/ayuda/sucursales/ajustes-generales-y-por-sucursal)).

**I pay some people weekly and others every two weeks.**
There's only one cycle. Leave it on weekly and, for the biweekly people, widen the **Date range** when you settle up.

**Does changing the cycle delete earlier payroll?**
In **Payroll** you can always go back to a past period with the **Date range**. What happens to the numbers when you change the cycle in the middle of a period isn't confirmed.
