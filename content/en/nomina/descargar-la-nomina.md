# Download payroll as CSV, Excel or PDF

**In short:** in **Payroll**, at the top right, tap **Download** and choose **CSV**, **Excel** or **PDF**. Before that, set the tab, location and range you want to take with you.

## Steps

1. Tap **Payroll** in the menu.
2. Pick the tab you need: **Commission barber**, **Rent barber** or **Salary barber**.
3. In **Filters**, check the **Location** and the **Date range** ([Filter payroll](/ayuda/nomina/filtrar-la-nomina-por-fechas-y-sucursal)).
4. Tap **Download**.
   ![The Download menu open with CSV, Excel and PDF](/assets/es/nomina/descargar-la-nomina/menu.png)
5. Choose the format.

| Format | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **CSV** | To import it into another program or send it to your accountant |
| **Excel** | To review it, add it up or filter it on your computer |
| **PDF** | To print it or send it as is over WhatsApp |

> [!NOTE]
> What the file contains (whether it has only the open tab or all three, whether it respects the range, the name it's saved under) couldn't be checked without downloading. It will be filled in after a test.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the download include all locations?**
Leave the location you need in **Filters** before downloading. For several, download them one by one.

**Can I download one barber's payroll only?**
That option wasn't there. Download the tab and filter by their name in Excel.

**Where does the file end up?**
In your browser's or your phone's downloads folder.
