# Filter payroll by dates and location

**In short:** below the **Payroll** tabs is the **Filters** bar, with **Location** and **Date range**. Next to the range you see the period (“September” or “01 SEP - 15 SEP”), the **‹ ›** arrows to change it and how many days it covers (“15 Days”). The table recalculates with every change.

![Payroll's Filters bar: Location, Date range, the period with arrows and the days](/assets/es/nomina/filtrar-la-nomina-por-fechas-y-sucursal/filtros.png)

## 1. Pick the location

1. Tap **Location**.
2. Choose the location whose payroll you want to see.

Each barber shows what they did at that location. If someone works at two, check both.

## 2. Change the date range

1. Tap the **Date range** field (it shows something like “01/SEP/26 - 30/SEP/26”).
2. Pick the start and end of the period in the picker.
   ![The date picker open over the Date range field](/assets/es/nomina/filtrar-la-nomina-por-fechas-y-sucursal/rango.png)

When you open **Payroll**, the range comes set according to your **Pay cycle** in **Settings › Payroll** ([The pay cycle](/ayuda/nomina/ciclo-de-pago)).

## 3. Move to the previous or next period

Tap **‹** to go to the previous period and **›** for the next one. The text in the middle changes (“September,” “01 SEP - 15 SEP”) and so does the day counter.

> [!TIP]
> To settle last pay period, don't type dates: tap **‹** once and check that the text in the middle shows the period you expect.

> [!NOTE]
> The picker that opens when you tap **Date range** couldn't be seen in detail. How the start and end are picked (two calendars, or one calendar with two taps) will be confirmed with the screenshot.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I see the payroll for all locations at once?**
The **Filters** bar lets you pick one location. If you need the total, download each one's ([Download payroll](/ayuda/nomina/descargar-la-nomina)).

**I changed the dates and a barber disappeared.**
They didn't charge anything in that period or weren't active at the location. Try a wider range.

**Is the filter saved when I leave?**
It wasn't seen being saved. When you come back to **Payroll**, check the range before reading the numbers.
