# My net pay is negative

**In short:** it happens on the **Rent barber** tab. The **Net pay** shows a minus sign when the **Fixed rent** is higher than what the barber generated in the period. It's not an error: it's what the barber owes the barbershop.

## What it means

A rent barber pays a **Fixed rent** for their chair. If their sales, commissions and tips in the period don't reach that amount, the difference is in your favor and payroll shows it as a negative.

Example: Carlos has a **Fixed rent** of $50,000 and only generated $30,000 this pay period. His **Net pay** comes out at -$20,000: that's what he owes you.

## What to check before you collect from them

1. The **Date range**. If the period is only partly over, there are still days to add up.
2. The **Location**. If Carlos works at two, his sales at the other aren't in this row.
3. **Transactions** with **Bill status › To collect**. Appointments still to collect don't count in payroll ([A bill ended up uncollected](/ayuda/transacciones/una-cuenta-quedo-por-cobrar)).
4. The **Rent base** in their record, **Payments** tab, in case it ended up with the wrong value.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can a commission barber come out negative?**
On the **Commission barber** tab there's no rent to subtract; their row doesn't carry that sign.

**Does the app charge the barber on its own?**
No. Payroll only shows the figure. How you collect it is up to you.

**Where do I change the rent if it's too high?**
In **Team**, their row, **Actions › Edit Rent barber**, **Payments** tab, **Rent base** field.
