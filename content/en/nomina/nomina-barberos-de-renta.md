# Payroll for rent barbers: fixed rent and net pay

**In short:** the **Rent barber** tab has the same sales and commission columns as the commission tab, but instead of **Taxes** it has **Fixed rent**: what the barber pays for their chair. The **Net pay** can be negative, which means the barber owes the barbershop.

![The rent barbers table with the Fixed rent column and the Net pay](/assets/es/nomina/nomina-barberos-de-renta/tabla.png)

## The columns

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Member** | The barber. The first row, **All**, is the rent team's total |
| **Services** | How many services they charged for in the period |
| **Service sales** | What those services added up to |
| **Product sales** | What they sold in products |
| **Service commission** | What they're owed for the services |
| **Product commission** | What they're owed for the products |
| **Total commissions** | **Service commission** plus **Product commission** |
| **Tips** | The tips they received |
| **Fixed rent** | What they pay for the chair in the period. It comes from **Rent base** in their record |
| **Net pay** | The result. If it's negative, the barber owes that amount to the barbershop |

## What the Fixed rent is

It's the **Rent base** in the barber's record, on the **Payments** tab (**Payment logic**). The three percentage deductions are there too: **Walk-in appointment deduction**, **New client deduction** and **No-preference appointment deduction**. [A rent barber's record: payments](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-de-renta-pagos) covers it; how the pieces fit together is in [How a rent barber's pay is calculated](/ayuda/nomina/como-se-calcula-la-renta).

## A negative net pay

When the rent is higher than what the barber generated in the period, the **Net pay** shows a minus sign. It's not an error: it's what the barber owes the barbershop. [My net pay is negative](/ayuda/nomina/mi-pago-neto-sale-negativo) explains it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the rent charged weekly or monthly?**
The **Rent base** is a single value in the barber's record. How it's split according to the period you have in **Settings › Payroll** is still to be confirmed with the team.

**Where do I change Carlos's rent?**
In **Team**, on his row, **Actions › Edit Rent barber**, **Payments** tab.

**Does a rent barber also show up on the commission tab?**
No. Each barber is on just one tab, based on their type. If you need to change their type, see [Commission barber or rent barber: which to choose](/ayuda/equipo/comision-o-renta).
