# Payroll for commission barbers: every column

**In short:** the **Commission barber** tab has one row per barber and ten columns. The first three count what they sold, the next three what they're owed for it, and the last ones adjust **Taxes** and **Tips** until you reach the **Net pay**.

![The commission barbers table with the All row and one row per barber](/assets/es/nomina/nomina-barberos-por-comision/tabla.png)

## The columns

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Member** | The barber, with their photo or initial. The first row, **All**, is the team total |
| **Services** | How many services they charged for in the period |
| **Service sales** | What those services added up to |
| **Product sales** | What they sold in products |
| **Service commission** | What they're owed for the services |
| **Product commission** | What they're owed for the products |
| **Total commissions** | **Service commission** plus **Product commission** |
| **Taxes** | The taxes applied to them in the period |
| **Tips** | The tips they received ([Tips in payroll](/ayuda/nomina/propinas-en-la-nomina)) |
| **Net pay** | What they're paid |

An example: Carlos charged for 1 service of $50.00. With a 20% commission he gets $10.00 in **Service commission**, $10.00 in **Total commissions** and, with no taxes or tips, $10.00 of **Net pay**.

## The All row

It adds up each column for all the barbers on the tab, at the selected location and period. It's the total you have to pay in commissions, not what the barbershop sold.

## Where the percentages come from

From each barber's record, **Compensation › Service** tab: **Basic commission**, **Returning client commission**, **Walk-in client commission**, **No-preference client commission** and **New client commission**. And from **Compensation › Product** for products. [A commission barber's record: compensation](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-compensacion) covers it. What gets subtracted before the percentage is applied is in [How a commission is calculated](/ayuda/nomina/como-se-calcula-una-comision).

## Frequently asked questions

**A barber has sales but Service commission is $0.00.**
Check their record, **Compensation › Service**. If all five fields are at 0.00, there's no percentage to apply.

**Is Service sales what the barbershop charged?**
It's what the services they performed added up to, and that were already charged in the period. Anything still **To collect** doesn't show.

**Why doesn't the table fit on the screen?**
There are ten columns. Scroll right to see **Tips** and **Net pay**, or download it in Excel ([Download payroll](/ayuda/nomina/descargar-la-nomina)).
