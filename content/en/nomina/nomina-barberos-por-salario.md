# Payroll for salary barbers

**In short:** the **Salary barber** tab is the shortest: seven columns. It doesn't count services because the barber earns a fixed **Salary**; it does count what they sold in products, their commission on them, taxes and tips, up to the **Net pay**.

![The salary barbers table with the Salary column and the Net pay](/assets/es/nomina/nomina-barberos-por-salario/tabla.png)

## The columns

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Member** | The barber. The first row, **All**, is the salaried team's total |
| **Product sales** | What they sold in products in the period |
| **Product commission** | What they're owed for those products |
| **Taxes** | The taxes applied to them |
| **Tips** | The tips they received |
| **Salary** | Their fixed salary for the period |
| **Net pay** | What they're paid |

## Where the salary comes from

A barber lands on this tab when, in their record under **Compensation**, the **Compensation type** is **Salary** (the other options are **Commission** and **Scale commission**). [A commission barber's record: compensation](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-compensacion) covers it.

> [!NOTE]
> The fields that appear when you choose **Salary** (where the amount is typed and whether it's per week, every two weeks or per month) haven't been seen on screen yet. It will be filled in with a test barber.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a salary barber earn commission on their haircuts?**
This table has no services column. Only **Product commission** is added.

**Where do I change Carlos's salary?**
In **Team**, on his row, **Actions › Edit Commission barber**, **Compensation** tab.

**Why is the tab empty?**
Because no barber at the selected location has **Compensation type › Salary**. Also check the **Date range** and the **Location**.
