# Tips in payroll

**In short:** the **Tips** column on all three **Payroll** tabs adds up the tips each barber received in the period. They're recorded when the appointment is charged, only for barbers with **Can receive tips** turned on, and the options the cashier sees are set in **Settings › Tips**.

## Where they come from

1. When you charge an appointment, at checkout the app asks “Would you like to add a tip?” with options like **$10.00**, **$15.00**, **$20.00**, **No tip** and **Custom** ([Charge with a tip](/ayuda/calendario/cobrar-con-propina)).
2. The tip stays on the account. In **Transactions** you see it added up in the period's **TIPS** card.
3. In **Payroll**, each barber has their own **Tips** column with what they received in the selected range.

## Who can receive them

In the barber's record, **Options** tab, there's the **Can receive tips** switch (Enabled or Disabled). If a barber never has tips in payroll, check that switch first.

## Where the options are set

In **Settings › Tips**: the **Tip percentage** (several options and **Add new tip option**), the **Default selection** and **Show at checkout**. Clients can also enter an amount of their own.

> [!NOTE]
> Whether the **Tips** column is added to the **Net pay** as is, or goes through some deduction, is still to be confirmed with the Barberlytics team. In the screenshots seen, all the tips were at $0.00.

## Frequently asked questions

**Carlos got a cash tip and it doesn't show up.**
Only what was recorded at checkout in the app counts. If the charge was made with **Pay appointment** without going through checkout, check whether the **Tip** field in the summary stayed at $0.00.

**Does the tip count as a sale?**
No. It goes in its own column, separate from **Service sales** and **Product sales**.

**Where do I see the tips for the whole barbershop?**
In **Transactions**, the **TIPS** card for the selected range ([The five Transactions cards](/ayuda/transacciones/las-cinco-tarjetas-de-transacciones)), and in **Metrics**, “% of tips.”
