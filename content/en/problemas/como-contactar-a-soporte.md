# How to contact support from inside the app

**In short:** we haven't found a support button inside the app. The contact is on the sign-in screen: the help button, at the bottom right, opens the **Contact us** form. Sign out, or open the sign-in screen in another tab, and write to us from there.

## Where the contact is

We went through every section and all 17 settings in **Settings**, and there was no support button, chat or email. What does exist:

| Where | What it is |
|---|---|
| Sign-in screen, bottom right | The help button: a black circle with a question mark |
| **Forgot your number?** › **I can't receive the email** | Takes you to the same form |

![The help button, at the bottom right of the sign-in screen](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/contactar-a-soporte-desde-la-entrada/boton-de-ayuda.png)

## Steps

1. Open the sign-in screen. If you're signed in, you can open `dash.barberlytics.com/authenticate` in another tab without signing out, or tap your account icon › **Sign out**.
2. Tap the help button. **Contact us** opens: “Fill in the following information so we can help you as soon as possible.”
   ![The Contact us form](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/contactar-a-soporte-desde-la-entrada/formulario.png)
3. Fill in **Full name\***, **Company\***, **Email\***, **Phone\*** (with the country) and **Tell us what happened\***. All five are required.
4. Tap **Send**.

The form's details are in [Contact support from the sign-in screen](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/contactar-a-soporte-desde-la-entrada).

## What to include in “Tell us what happened”

- Your barbershop and your location, and which profile you sign in with (owner, barber…).
- The exact screen and the button you tapped.
- What you expected and what happened, with the text of the message if there was one.
- When it started and whether it always happens or only sometimes.
- For a number that doesn't add up: the date, the client or the barber, and the figure ([A number doesn't add up](/ayuda/problemas/un-numero-no-cuadra)).

> [!NOTE]
> It isn't confirmed what happens after **Send** (a confirmation message, how they reply to you, how long it takes) or whether opening the sign-in screen in another tab signs you out. If the team has an additional support channel (email, WhatsApp), it will be added here once they confirm it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there a chat inside the app?**
We haven't seen one.

**Is this help center support?**
No. These are written answers; to talk to a person, use **Contact us**.

**I can't even sign in.**
That's exactly what the help button on the sign-in screen is for. See [I can't sign in](/ayuda/problemas/no-puedo-entrar).
