# The app is slow or won't load

**In short:** first reload the screen, then check your connection, and if it's still the same, sign out and sign back in. If one specific screen always fails, write to us and tell us which one.

## What to try, in order

1. **Reload the screen.** In **Transactions** there's a **reload** icon next to the magnifying glass that fetches the data again. On the other screens, reload the browser.
2. **Check your connection.** Open another page on the same phone or computer. If that doesn't load either, it's the connection, not the app.
3. **Come back in from the list.** Some records only load if you open them from their list: a barber's record opened by typing the address in the browser comes up empty. Go to **Team** or **Clients** and tap **Actions › View**.
4. **Sign out and sign back in.** Your account icon › **Sign out**, and sign in again with your phone number and the code ([Sign out](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/cerrar-sesion)).
5. **Try another browser or device.** If it works on the computer but not on your phone, tell us that when you write to us.

## If it's one specific screen that's slow

- **Metrics** with **All locations** or a long period (**Month**) loads more data. Try one location and **Today**.
- **Transactions** by default fetches from the 1st of the month to today. Shorten the range with **FROM** and **TO** and tap **Apply**.
- **Clients** shows 10 per page (“Rows per page”). Use the search box instead of paging through.

> [!NOTE]
> These are the steps you can take from the app itself. We haven't seen a service status screen or a maintenance notice in Barberlytics; if a status page exists, it will be linked here once the team confirms it.

## When to write to us

If it's still slow after these steps, or a screen won't load, write to us with:

- Which screen it is (for example, “Payroll, Rent barber tab”).
- Since when it's been happening.
- Whether it happens on your phone, your computer, or both.
- A screenshot of what you see.

Where to write is in [How to contact support](/ayuda/problemas/como-contactar-a-soporte).

## Frequently asked questions

**Did I lose what I was typing?**
If you reload before tapping **Save** or **Create**, the form is emptied. Type it again.

**Is it my phone?**
Close the other browser tabs and open the app again. If it runs fine on another device, tell us the model of yours when you write to us.
