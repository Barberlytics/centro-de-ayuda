# I can't sign in to Barberlytics

**In short:** to sign in you only need your phone and the code you receive. If you can't get in, check in this order: the country flag, the full number, the code, and whether your number is registered. If nothing works, write to us with the help button.

## What to check, in order

1. **The country flag.** It's set to the United States (+1) by default. If your number is from Colombia, choose +57 ([Choose your phone's country](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/elegir-el-pais-del-telefono)).
   ![The sign-in screen with the Phone field](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/ingresar/pantalla-de-entrada.png)
2. **The full number.** The field only accepts digits; letters can't be typed. If a digit is missing, the screen says “This field is too short.”
3. **Tap Sign in** and wait for the code on your phone.
4. **The code.** Type it in **Code\*** and tap **Confirm**. If it didn't arrive, tap **Resend code** ([Confirm your number with the code](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/confirmar-el-codigo)).
   ![The Confirm number screen with the Code field, Confirm and Resend code](/assets/es/acceso-y-cuenta/confirmar-el-codigo/pantalla.png)
5. **Don't remember which number you signed up with?** Tap **Forgot your number?** and type your email: “We will send an email to the email address registered in barberlytics with your phone number or the option to change your phone number.” ([Forgot your number](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/olvidaste-tu-numero)).
6. **Not getting the email either?** On that screen, tap **I can't receive the email**: it takes you to the **Contact us** form.
7. **Write to us.** The help button, at the bottom right, opens **Contact us**. Fill in the five fields and tap **Send** ([Contact support from the sign-in screen](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/contactar-a-soporte-desde-la-entrada)).

## Errors you may see

| What the screen says | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| “This field is required” | Phone, Code, Email or any empty Contact us field | Fill it in |
| “This field is too short” | Phone with too few digits | Type the full number, with the right country |
| “This field has an invalid format” | Misspelled email in Forgot your number | Check the @ and the dot |

## What isn't there

There's no password on the sign-in screen, and the button to create an account isn't on this screen: signing up a new barbershop is in [Register your barbershop on Barberlytics](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/registrar-tu-barberia). If you're on the team and have never been given access, your owner has to add you in **Team › Actions › Create team member** with your phone number.

> [!NOTE]
> These steps were checked on the sign-in screen without sending any real number. What can only be seen with a phone: the message if the code is wrong or expires, how many attempts you get, the message if the number isn't registered, what happens after **Resend code**, and how long the **Forgot your number?** email takes. ## Frequently asked questions

**I have two profiles and I got into the one I didn't want.**
Signing in is the same for both. Once you're in, switch profiles from your account icon ([Switch roles](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/cambiar-de-rol)).

**I changed phones.**
If the number is the same, sign in as usual. If the number changed, use **Forgot your number?**: the email includes “the option to change your phone number.”

**The screen is in English.**
The globe at the top right switches between **ES** and **EN**.
