# How your products are organized

**In short:** in **Products** you have two tabs: **Products** and **Categories**. Every product belongs to a category. There is one catalog for your whole barbershop: you create each product once and turn it on at each location that sells it with **Activate barbershop products**.

## How to get there

In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Products**. The screen says “Here you can keep full control of your products.” and the **Actions** button is at the top right.

> [!NOTE]
> With the commission barber profile, **Services & Products** doesn't show up in the menu. With the default permissions, the receptionist can see products but can't create or edit them. See [Permissions table by role](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol).

## The two tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Products** | The “product list”, with how many you have (“5 Products”), the **Categories** filter, a search box and a table with each product's name, category and price. [The product list](/ayuda/productos/la-lista-de-productos) explains it. |
| **Categories** | The “Category list”, with how many you have (“4 Categories”), a search box and, for each category, how many products it holds (“Waxes · 1 Product”, “Shampoos · 0 Product”). |

![The Categories tab in Products with the list of categories and how many products each one has](/assets/es/productos/como-se-organizan-tus-productos/categorias.png)

### Categories

A category groups similar products. You can group them by type (“Waxes”, “Shampoos”, “Beard oils”) or by brand, whichever you prefer. When you create a product, **Category** is required. Categories are also the tabs your team sees when selling: in **Create quick sale**, products are grouped by category.

### Products

A product is something you sell: “Matte wax”, “Beard oil”. Each one has a name, a **Sale price**, a category and, if you want, a purchase price, a SKU code, photos, a brand and a distributor. See [Create a product](/ayuda/productos/crear-un-producto).

## Create it once, turn it on by location

Your products are a single catalog for the whole barbershop. Each location turns on the ones it sells:

1. At the top, choose the location.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Activate barbershop products**. The “Activate product” screen says “Select the products you want to import by turning them on with the switch. Click “Save” once you've made your choices.”
   ![The Activate product screen with one switch per product and the Save button](/assets/es/sucursales/activar-productos-en-una-sucursal/pantalla.png)
3. Turn on the switch for each product that location sells.
4. Tap **Save**.

> [!TIP]
> “I can't see a product at my location” almost always means “it isn't turned on here”. See [I can't see a service or product in my location](/ayuda/sucursales/no-veo-un-servicio-o-producto-en-mi-sucursal).

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to create the product at each location?**
No. You create it once and turn it on at the locations where you sell it.

**Where do I sell a product?**
From the calendar: with **Actions › Create quick sale**, or when you charge an appointment with **Add products**. See [Sell a product](/ayuda/productos/vender-un-producto).

**Where do I see how many units I have left?**
The product form has no stock field. What does exist is covered in [Inventory: units and low-stock alerts](/ayuda/productos/inventario-y-unidades).
