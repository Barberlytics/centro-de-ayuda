# Create a product category

**In short:** in **Products**, tap **Actions** › **Create Category**, type the **Name** and tap **Create**. Create categories before products: when you create a product, the category is required.

## Steps

1. In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Products**.
2. Tap **Actions** and choose **Create Category**. The “Create Category” page opens.
   ![The Create Category form with Name, Description and the Create button](/assets/es/productos/crear-una-categoria-de-productos/formulario.png)
3. Type the **Name**, for example “Waxes”.
4. If you want, type a **Description**.
5. Tap **Create**.

## The fields

| Field | Required | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Yes | What the category is called. It's the tab your team sees when selling from **Create quick sale**, so use clear words. |
| **Description** | No | Free text to explain what goes in that category. |

Unlike service categories, there is no **Private / Public** switch here: visibility is decided product by product ([Public or private product and availability for sale](/ayuda/productos/producto-publico-o-privado)).

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows right after **Create** (a message, which tab it goes back to) hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed when a test category is created.

> [!TIP]
> Group the way your team sells, not the way you buy. If Carlos looks for “the wax” and not “the brand”, make the category “Waxes”.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many products does each category have?**
The **Categories** tab shows it: each category displays its count, for example “Waxes · 1 Product”.

**Can I move a product to another category later?**
Yes. In the list, go to **Actions › Edit**, change the **Category** and tap **Save**. See [Edit or delete a product](/ayuda/productos/editar-o-borrar-un-producto).

**Can I delete a category that has products?**
What happens to its products hasn't been checked yet. Before deleting it, move its products to another category.
