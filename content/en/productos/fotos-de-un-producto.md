# Photos of a product

**In short:** a product has one main photo, at the top of the form, and a **Photos - 0/5** block for up to five more photos. You add them when you create the product or when you edit it, in the **General** tab.

## Where they are

![The General tab of Create product with the main photo at the top and the Photos - 0/5 block below](/assets/es/productos/crear-un-producto/general.png)

| Block | What it is |
|---|---|
| Photo (top of the form) | The product's main image. |
| **Photos - 0/5** | Up to five more photos. The counter shows how many you have. |

## Steps

1. In **Products**, tap **Actions › Create product**, or **Actions › Edit** on the row of a product that already exists.
2. In the **General** tab, tap the photo at the top to set the main one.
3. Under **Photos - 0/5**, add the rest, up to five.
4. Tap **Continue** and then **Save**.

> [!NOTE]
> Uploading a photo wasn't run when the screen was read. Which formats and sizes it accepts, how to remove a photo and where the photos show up afterward (in online booking, when selling) hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed with a test product.

> [!TIP]
> A photo of the product as it sits in your display case, on a clean background, helps your team recognize it quickly when selling and helps the client know what they're taking home.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a photo required?**
No. None of the photo fields are required.

**Can I add more than five?**
The block says **Photos - 0/5**: five is the maximum, in addition to the main one.

**Do the photos show in online booking?**
If the product is **Public**, it shows in the booking flow. Which photos it shows hasn't been checked yet. See [Public or private product and availability for sale](/ayuda/productos/producto-publico-o-privado).
