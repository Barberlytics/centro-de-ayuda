# The product list

**In short:** the **Products** tab shows the “product list” in a table: each product's name, category and price. You can filter by category, search by name and, on each row, tap **Actions** to **View**, **Edit** or **Delete**.

## How to get there

1. In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Products**.
2. Tap the **Products** tab. At the top it says how many you have, for example “5 Products”.
   ![The product list with the Categories filter, the search box and the table](/assets/es/productos/la-lista-de-productos/productos.png)

## Filter and search

- **Categories**: a selector that starts at **All categories**. Choose one to see only its products.
- The **search box**: type part of the name (“Wax”) and the list narrows down.

## What's in each column

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The product's name, for example “Matte wax”. |
| **Category** | The category it belongs to, for example “Waxes”. |
| **Price** | The **Sale price**, what the client pays. The purchase price doesn't show in the list. |
| **Action** | The **Actions** button for that row. |

The list has no units or stock column. See [Inventory: units and low-stock alerts](/ayuda/productos/inventario-y-unidades).

## Each row's actions

Tap **Actions** on the product's row:

![The Actions menu of a row with View, Edit and Delete](/assets/es/productos/la-lista-de-productos/acciones-fila.png)

| Option | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **View** | The **View product** screen, read-only, with the General and Advanced tabs. |
| **Edit** | The same form as creating, with **Save**. |
| **Delete** | Removes the product. |

The last two are explained in [Edit or delete a product](/ayuda/productos/editar-o-borrar-un-producto).

## Frequently asked questions

**Why doesn't a product I know exists show up?**
Almost always it's because it isn't turned on at the location you have selected at the top. Turn it on from **Actions** › **Activate barbershop products** ([Activate products in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-productos-en-una-sucursal)).

**Where do I create a new product?**
With **Actions** › **Create product**, on this same screen. See [Create a product](/ayuda/productos/crear-un-producto).

**Can the receptionist see this list?**
With the default permissions, yes: the receptionist can see products and categories, but can't create, edit or delete them.
