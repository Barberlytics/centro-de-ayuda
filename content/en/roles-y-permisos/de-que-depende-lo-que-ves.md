# What decides what you see: your profile, your location and your permissions

**In short:** three things change your screen. The **profile** you signed in with, the **location** you picked at the top and the **permissions** you have.

## 1. Your profile

If you have two profiles, for example **Business** and **Barber comission**, each one shows a different menu. With the commission barber profile, **Services & Products**, **Payroll** and **Transactions** disappear, the calendar shows only your column and Settings shrinks to two options.

See it in [Profiles and roles](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/perfiles-y-roles).

## 2. The location you picked

The location name at the top decides whose calendar, clients, team and metrics you see. Inside a location you only see what is **turned on** there: barbers, services and products.

See it in [How your barbershop is organized](/ayuda/sucursales/como-se-organiza-tu-barberia).

## 3. Your permissions

Each person has a permissions table: **View**, **Create**, **Edit**, **Delete** and **Active** for each feature. If you don't have **View** on a feature, that section doesn't show in your menu. If you don't have **Edit** or **Delete**, you won't be able to use those options.

See it in [How permissions work](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/como-funcionan-los-permisos).

## How to tell which one it is

| What's happening | What to check first |
|---|---|
| A whole section is missing from the menu | Your profile and your permissions |
| A barber, service or product doesn't show up | That it's turned on in the location you picked |
| The numbers are for one person only | That you're on the barber profile |
| A client's detail shows asterisks | That's normal: the phone number is masked |

## Frequently asked questions

**I asked my receptionist to sign in and they don't see the same as I do. Is something wrong?**
No. Each role has its own permissions. See the [permissions table](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol).
