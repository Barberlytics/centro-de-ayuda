# The five roles and what each one is for

**In short:** Barberlytics has five roles. When you create someone on your team you pick one, and in **Settings › Permissions** you see and adjust what each one can do.

## The roles

| Role | How the app describes it |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | “This role has all the permissions normally granted to the barbershop owner.” |
| **Location administrator** | “This role has everything needed to manage a location. It is usually assigned to the location administrator.” |
| **Receptionist** | “This role has permissions to book appointments and check in and check out. It is usually assigned to the barbershop's secretaries.” On the Permissions screen it appears as **Receptionist - front desk** |
| **Commission barber** | “This role is for barbers who are paid commissions.” |
| **Rent barber** | “This role is for team members who are rented a workspace” |

## Where you pick them

When you use **Team › Actions › Create team member**, the screen shows one card per role with its description. You pick one and tap **Create**.

## Where you see their permissions

In **Settings › Permissions** there is one tab per role: **Location administrator**, **Commission barber**, **Rent barber**, **Receptionist - front desk** and **Owner**. The full table is in [Permissions table: what each role can do](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol).

> [!NOTE]
> An owner can also be a commission barber. In the **Managers** list they appear as “Owner - Commission barber”.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I create a new role, for example “Shift manager”?**
In the screens reviewed, only these five roles show up.

**Can reception see the barbers' pay?**
No. That section is for the owner only. See the [permissions table](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol).
