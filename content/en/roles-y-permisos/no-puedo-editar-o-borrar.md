# I can't edit or delete something

**In short:** each feature has its own **View**, **Create**, **Edit** and **Delete** permissions. If your profile is missing one, that option isn't available.

## What to check

1. Look in the [permissions table](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol) to see what your role gets on that feature. For example, the commission barber only has **View** on **Block calendar**.
2. If your role does allow it, ask the owner to check your permissions in your record, on the **Permissions** tab.

> [!NOTE]
> It isn't documented yet how each option looks on screen when you don't have the permission: whether it is hidden or shown turned off.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the owner end up without permissions?**
No. The Permissions screen says owner accounts have full access to the system.
