# I can't see a menu section

**In short:** the menu only shows the sections your profile is allowed. If one is missing, check which profile you signed in with and what permissions you have.

## What to check, in order

1. **Which profile are you on?** Open your account menu, at the top right. If you have two profiles, such as **Business** and **Barber comission**, the commission barber one doesn't show **Services & Products**, **Payroll** or **Transactions**. Switch to the other profile ([Switch roles](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/cambiar-de-rol)).
2. **Are you on your phone?** On a narrow screen the menu becomes a bar at the bottom with a few buttons. Tap **More** to see all the sections.
3. **Does your role include it?** Look at the [permissions table](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol). For example, the rent barber doesn't have Team or Services, and reception doesn't see Expenses.
4. **Did someone take the permission away?** If **View** is taken away on a feature, that section stops showing. Ask the owner to check it in your record, on the **Permissions** tab.

## Who can fix it

According to the [permissions table](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol), the roles with **Permissions** by default are the owner and the location administrator.

## Frequently asked questions

**I used to see Team and now I don't.**
Most likely **View** was taken away from you on **Team**. Ask the owner to check it.

**Is it an app error?**
Normally not. It's how the app protects information based on the role.
