# Profiles and roles: what they are and how they differ

**In short:** a **role** is a set of things a person can do. A **profile** is the role you sign in with. One person, with the same phone number, can have **more than one profile**.

## The idea

Think of an owner who also cuts hair. It is one person with one phone number, but they have two profiles:

- **Business:** for running the barbershop.
- **Barber comission:** for serving clients as a commission barber.

In your account menu you see your name once for each profile, with the profile name underneath. The one you're using has a check mark.

## What changes when you switch profiles

It takes you to the **Calendar**. From there, the menu and the screens change:

| | Business | Barber comission |
|---|---|---|
| Menu | All sections | No **Services & Products**, **Payroll** or **Transactions** |
| Calendar | All barbers | Only your column |
| Metrics | Five tabs, with the whole team's data | Three tabs, with your data |
| Settings | The barbershop's settings | Only **Time format** and **Delete account** |

It doesn't ask you to sign in again.

## The five roles that exist

Owner, Location administrator, Receptionist, Commission barber and Rent barber. Each one is explained in [The five roles](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/los-cinco-roles).

## Frequently asked questions

**I don't see two profiles in my menu.**
Then your account has just one.

**How do I switch profiles?**
See [Switch roles](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/cambiar-de-rol).

**Who decides what each role can do?**
The owner, in **Settings › Permissions**. See [How permissions work](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/como-funcionan-los-permisos).
