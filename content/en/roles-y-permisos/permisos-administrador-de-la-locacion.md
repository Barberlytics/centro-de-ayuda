# What the location administrator can do and see

**In short:** the administrator manages one location: almost everything the owner can, without the **Barber payments section** or **Order barbers' calendars**.

## How the app describes it

“This role has everything needed to manage a location. It is usually assigned to the location administrator.”

## What they can do by default

- **Bookings and clients:** Book appointments, Block calendar and Clients with all permissions; **Client information** and **Import clients** active.
- **Team:** Team, Managers, Levels and Get in line with all permissions.
- **Metrics:** **Business key performance indicators** active.
- **Inventory and Services:** all permissions.
- **Business:** Closing dates; Payroll (View, Create, Edit); Permissions and Account settings (View, Edit); Expenses.
- They don't have: **Barber payments section**, **Order barbers' calendars** or **Client download**.

> [!NOTE]
> This comes from the factory permissions table. A specific person's permissions can be different: the owner can adjust them in their record. This screen has not yet been seen with an account in this role.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the administrator change permissions?**
By default they have **Permissions** with View, Edit and Active.
