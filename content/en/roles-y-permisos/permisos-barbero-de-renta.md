# What the rent barber can do and see

**In short:** the rent barber has only three blocks: Bookings and clients, POS and, under Business, Account settings and Expenses.

## How the app describes it

“This role is for team members who are rented a workspace”

## What they can do by default

- **Bookings and clients:** Book appointments, Block calendar and Clients with all permissions; **Import clients** and **Client download** active.
- **POS:** **Pricing** active.
- **Business:** Account settings with View only; Expenses with all permissions.
- Team, Metrics, Inventory and Services don't show up.

> [!NOTE]
> This comes from the factory permissions table. A specific person's permissions can be different: the owner can adjust them in their record. This screen has not yet been seen with an account in this role.

## Frequently asked questions

**How is this different from a commission barber?**
In how they are paid (the rent barber pays a base amount and the barbershop applies discounts) and in their permissions. See [A barber's record: permissions](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-permisos).
