# What the commission barber can do and see

**In short:** the commission barber books appointments, sees and creates clients, sees the team and records expenses. They don't touch catalogs, payroll or permissions.

## How the app describes it

“This role is for barbers who are paid commissions.”

## What they can do by default

**By default:**
- **Bookings and clients:** Book appointments with all permissions; **Block calendar** with View only; Clients with View and Create.
- **Team:** **View** only.
- **Business:** Account settings with View only; Expenses with all permissions.

**What you see when you sign in with this profile:**
- Menu: Home, Calendar, Clients, Team, Expenses and Settings. Services & Products, Payroll and Transactions don't show up.
- Calendar: only their own column, with the Actions Create appointment, Create quick sale, Walk-in sale and Payment list.
- Metrics: three tabs (Business, Clients, Barbers) with their own numbers.
- Clients: the list with the phone number masked.
- Team: tabs Barbers, Levels and Working hours (no Managers).
- Settings: only **Time format** and **Delete account**.

> [!NOTE]
> The profile details were checked by signing in with a test account. A person's permissions can differ from the factory ones.

## Frequently asked questions

**I'm the owner. How do I see what my barber sees?**
Switch to the **Barber comission** profile from your account menu, if your account has it. See [Switch roles](/ayuda/acceso-y-cuenta/cambiar-de-rol).
