# What the owner can do and see

**In short:** the owner has full access to the system. It is the only role with the **Barber payments section** and **Order barbers' calendars**.

## How the app describes it

“This role has all the permissions normally granted to the barbershop owner.” And in Permissions: “Owner accounts have full access to the system.”

## What they can do by default

- **Bookings and clients:** everything, including **Client download**.
- **Team:** Team, Managers, Levels, Get in line and **Order barbers' calendars** with all permissions; **Barber payments section** active.
- **Metrics:** **Business key performance indicators** active.
- **Inventory and Services:** everything.
- **Business:** Closing dates, Payroll, Permissions, Account settings and Expenses.
- With the full menu: Home, Calendar, Clients, Services & Products, Team, Expenses, Payroll, Transactions and Settings.

> [!NOTE]
> The owner's full menu was seen with the test account.
