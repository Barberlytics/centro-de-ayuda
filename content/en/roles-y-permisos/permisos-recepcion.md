# What reception can do and see

**In short:** reception handles appointments and clients, sees products and metrics, and doesn't see expenses or team.

## How the app describes it

“This role has permissions to book appointments and check in and check out. It is usually assigned to the barbershop's secretaries.” In Permissions it appears as **Receptionist - front desk**.

## What they can do by default

- **Bookings and clients:** Book appointments and Block calendar with all permissions; Clients with View, Create and Edit; **Client information** and **Import clients** active.
- **Metrics:** **Business key performance indicators** active.
- **Inventory:** Products and Categories with **View** only.
- **Business:** Account settings with View and Edit.
- They don't have: Team, Services, Expenses, Payroll, Permissions or Closing dates.

> [!NOTE]
> This comes from the factory permissions table. A specific person's permissions can be different: the owner can adjust them in their record. This screen has not yet been seen with an account in this role.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can reception charge?**
It isn't documented which payment features this role includes. Check the calendar with a reception account before assuming it.
