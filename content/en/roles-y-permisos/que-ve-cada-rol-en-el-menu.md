# Which menu sections each role sees

**In short:** the menu is built from each person's permissions. These are the two versions that were checked on screen.

## Owner (Business profile)

Home · Calendar · Clients · Services & Products (with **Services** and **Products**) · Team · Expenses · Payroll · Transactions · Settings.

On your phone, the bottom bar has Home, Calendar, Team, Clients and **More**.

## Commission barber (Barber comission profile)

Home · Calendar · Clients · Team · Expenses · Settings.

**Services & Products**, **Payroll** and **Transactions** don't show up. On your phone, the bottom bar has Home, Calendar, Expenses, Clients and **More**.

## The other roles

The real menu for the location administrator, reception and the rent barber has not been checked yet. To know which sections they get, look at their row in the [permissions table](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol): a section shows up when the person has **View** or **Active** on it.

> [!NOTE]
> If you take **View** away from a person on a feature, that section disappears from their menu. For example, without **View** on **Team**, they don't see Team.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I check what my receptionist sees?**
There is no preview by role yet. Compare their permissions in their record, on the **Permissions** tab.
