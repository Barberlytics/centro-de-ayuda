# How your services are organized: categories, services and combos

**In short:** in **Services** you have three tabs: **Categories**, **Services** and **Combos**. Every service belongs to a category, and a combo puts several services together for a single price. There is one catalog for your whole barbershop: you create each service once and turn it on at each location with **Activate services**.

## How to get there

In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Services**. The screen says “Here you can keep full control of your services.” and the **Actions** button is at the top right.

> [!NOTE]
> With the commission barber profile, **Services & Products** doesn't show up in the menu. The owner and the location admin see it. See [What each role sees in the menu](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/que-ve-cada-rol-en-el-menu).

## The three tabs

![The Categories tab with the list of categories, the search box and each row's actions](/assets/es/servicios/como-se-organizan-tus-servicios/categorias.png)

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Categories** | The **Category List**, with how many you have (“5 Categories”) and a search box. Each category is a row with **…**, the eye (view), pencil (edit) and trash (delete) icons, and an arrow **›** that expands it to show its services. |
| **Services** | The **Service listing**: a **Categories** filter, a search box and a table with the service, its category, its **Base price** and its **Status**. [The service list](/ayuda/servicios/la-lista-de-servicios) explains it. |
| **Combos** | The **Combo list**, with how many you have (“4 Combos”) and the same table as the services one. |

### Categories

A category groups similar services: “Cuts”, “Beard”, “Color”. It's useful for two things:

- Sorting the list and filtering it.
- Ordering what your client sees when booking online: categories show up in the order you set in **Booking order** ([The order of services when booking](/ayuda/servicios/orden-de-agendamiento)).

When you create a service, **Category** is required. Services left without a category end up in **Uncategorized**.

### Services

A service is what you charge for doing: “Classic haircut”, “Beard”, “Kids' haircut”. Each one has a name, a **Base price**, a length in minutes and a category. See [Create a service: general details](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-servicio).

### Combos

A combo puts two or more services together with its own **Combined price** and its own length: “Haircut + beard” for less than the two add up to. See [Create a service combo](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-combo).

## Create it once, turn it on by location

Your services are a single catalog for the whole barbershop. Each location turns on its own:

1. At the top, choose the location.
2. Tap **Actions** › **Activate services**. The screen says “Select the services you want to import by turning the service on with the switch. Click 'Save' once you've made your choices.”
   ![The Activate services screen with one switch per service and the Save button](/assets/es/sucursales/activar-servicios-en-una-sucursal/pantalla.png)
3. Turn on the switch for each service that location offers.
4. Tap **Save**.

> [!TIP]
> “I can't see a service at my location” almost always means “it isn't turned on here”. See [I can't see a service or product in my location](/ayuda/sucursales/no-veo-un-servicio-o-producto-en-mi-sucursal).

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to create the service at each location?**
No. You create it once and turn it on at the locations where you offer it.

**Can a service be in two categories?**
No. When you create it you choose one **Category**. If you need it to show up in two places, create a combo or a second service.

**Who can create services?**
With the default permissions, the owner and the location admin. The receptionist and the barbers don't have the Services feature. See [Permissions table by role](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/tabla-de-permisos-por-rol).
