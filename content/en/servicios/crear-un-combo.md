# Create a service combo

**In short:** a combo puts two or more services together with its own **Combined price** and **Combo length**, for example “Haircut + beard” at 75 when separately they add up to 80. You create it with **Actions › Create combo**: name, the services, the price, the category and **Continue**.

## Steps

1. In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Services**.
2. Tap **Actions** and choose **Create combo**. “Create service combo” opens with the **General**, **Team** and **Advanced** tabs.
   ![The Create service combo form: name, the list of services, the totals and the Combo Details](/assets/es/servicios/crear-un-combo/formulario.png)
3. Type the **Name**, for example “Haircut + beard”.
4. Under “Select your services”, tap **Add service** and choose the first one. Repeat for each service in the combo. Below, **Services Total** and **Total Length** keep adding up what they cost and last separately.
5. In **Combo Details**, type the **Combined price** (what you charge for the combo) and the **Combo length**.
6. If you want, type a **Description**.
7. Leave it as **Public** so it shows in online booking, or switch it to **Private**.
8. Choose the **Category**.
9. Check **This service accepts gift cards** if it can be paid with one.
10. Tap **Continue**.

## The fields

| Field | Required | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Yes | What the combo is called in the list and when booking. |
| **Add service** | — | Adds a service from the catalog to the combo. |
| **Services Total** | — | What the services would add up to separately. Read-only. |
| **Total Length** | — | How long the services would last separately. Read-only. |
| **Combined price** | Yes | What you charge for the combo. It can be lower than the total. |
| **Combo length** | No | How much time the calendar blocks. It can differ from the total length. |
| **Description** | No | Free text with what it includes. |
| **Private / Public** | — | Whether the combo shows in online booking. |
| **Category** | Yes | The category it appears in. |
| **This service accepts gift cards** | No | Whether the combo can be paid with a gift card. |

## What View combo shows

In the **Combos** tab, tap **Actions › View** on the combo's row.

![The View combo screen with Details, the Additional Services and the totals](/assets/es/servicios/crear-un-combo/ver-combo.png)

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Name and **Description** | The combo's name and its description. |
| **Details** | **Visibility**, **Color** (for example “Yellow”), **Combined price** (for example $75.00), **Combo length** (for example 60 Minutes) and **Category**. |
| **Additional Services** | Each service in the combo with its length and price: “Service 1: Classic haircut · 30 min · $50.00”, “Service 2: Beard · 15 min · $30.00”. |
| **Services Total** and **Total Length** | The sum of the services separately (for example $80.00 and 45 min). |

The combo's price can be lower than the sum, and its length different. That's what makes it a combo.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the combo have to be cheaper than the individual services?**
No. You set the **Combined price**. It's usually lower, but the app doesn't require it.

**Can I put a combo inside another combo?**
**Add service** lets you pick services. Whether it accepts combos hasn't been checked yet.

**Where do I see the combo's color?**
In **View combo**, under **Details**. In the create form it wasn't seen where it's chosen; it will be completed when a test combo is created.
