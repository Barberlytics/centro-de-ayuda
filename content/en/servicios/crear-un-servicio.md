# Create a service: general details

**In short:** tap **Actions** › **Create Service**, fill in the **General** tab (at least **Service name**, **Base price** and **Category**) and tap **Continue**. That makes the **Team** and **Advanced** tabs available.

## Before you start

- Have the category the service goes in already created ([Create a service category](/ayuda/servicios/crear-una-categoria-de-servicios)).
- Know the price and how many minutes it takes. The length is what the calendar uses to reserve the chair.

## Steps

1. In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Services**.
2. Tap **Actions** and choose **Create Service**. The form opens with three tabs: **General**, **Team** and **Advanced**. Only **General** is active; the other two become available when you continue.
   ![The General tab of Create Service with all its fields and the Continue button](/assets/es/servicios/crear-un-servicio/general.png)
3. If you want, upload a photo of the service.
4. Type the **Service name**, for example “Classic haircut”.
5. Type the **Service cost** (what it costs you) and the **Base price** (what you charge). [Service cost and base price](/ayuda/servicios/costo-y-precio-de-un-servicio) explains them.
6. Type a **Description** if you want the client to know what's included.
7. Decide whether the service is **Private** or **Public**: “This option makes your service visible in online booking”. See [Public or private service](/ayuda/servicios/servicio-publico-o-privado).
8. Type the **Service length (minutes)**, for example 30.
9. Choose the **Taxes** that apply, if you have any set up.
10. Choose the **Category**.
11. Tap **Continue**. Go on to [Create a service: who does it](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-servicio-equipo).

If you change your mind, tap **Cancel** and nothing is saved.

## The fields

| Field | Required | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Photo | No | An image of the service. |
| **Service name** | Yes | What it's called in the list, in the calendar and in online booking. |
| **Service cost** | No | What it costs you to do it (supplies, for example). The client doesn't see it. |
| **Base price** | Yes | What you charge for the service. It's the price in the list and the starting point for each barber's price. |
| **Description** | No | Free text with what the service includes. |
| **Private / Public** | — | Whether the service shows in online booking (**Public**) or not (**Private**). |
| **Service length (minutes)** | No | How long it takes. The calendar sets that time aside when booking. |
| **Taxes** | No | A selector with the taxes set up in **Settings › Taxes**. |
| **Category** | Yes | A selector with your categories. |

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows right after **Continue** (whether it saves the service right away or waits until the end) hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed when a test service is created.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Creating the service doesn't turn it on at every location. After creating it, turn it on at each location that offers it with **Actions › Activate services** ([Activate services in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-servicios-en-una-sucursal)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between cost and price?**
The **Service cost** is what it costs you; the **Base price** is what you charge. See [Service cost and base price](/ayuda/servicios/costo-y-precio-de-un-servicio).

**Can I set a different price for each barber?**
Yes, in the **Team** tab, after **Continue**. See [Create a service: who does it](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-servicio-equipo).

**What if the service is done at two locations?**
Create it once and turn it on at both. The catalog belongs to the whole barbershop.
