# Create a service category

**In short:** in **Services**, tap **Actions** › **Create category**, type the **Name**, decide whether it's **Private** or **Public** and tap **Create**. Create categories before services: when you create a service, the category is required.

## Steps

1. In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Services**.
2. Tap **Actions** and choose **Create category**. The “Create category” page opens.
   ![The Create category form with Name, Description, the Private / Public switch and the Create button](/assets/es/servicios/crear-una-categoria-de-servicios/formulario.png)
3. Type the **Name**, for example “Cuts”.
4. If you want, type a **Description**.
5. Leave the switch on **Public** if you want the category to show in online booking. Set it to **Private** so only your team sees it.
6. Tap **Create**.

## The fields

| Field | Required | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Yes | What the category is called. It's what your client sees when booking, so use clear words: “Cuts”, “Beard”, “Color”. |
| **Description** | No | Free text to explain what goes in that category. |
| **Private / Public** | — | “This option makes your service visible in online booking”. When **Public**, the category shows when booking online; when **Private**, it doesn't. |

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows right after **Create** (a message, which tab it goes back to) hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed when a test category is created.

> [!TIP]
> Think of categories as the sections of your menu. A few clear ones help your client more than many with two services each. The order they see them in is set in [The order of services when booking](/ayuda/servicios/orden-de-agendamiento).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I create the service first and the category afterward?**
When you create a service, **Category** is required. If you don't have any yet, create the category first. Services without a category end up in **Uncategorized**.

**How do I rename a category?**
In the **Categories** tab, on the category's row, tap the pencil icon (edit). The trash icon deletes it and the eye shows it.

**Does a private category hide its services?**
The category's switch controls whether the category shows in online booking. Each service also has its own switch. See [Public or private service](/ayuda/servicios/servicio-publico-o-privado).
