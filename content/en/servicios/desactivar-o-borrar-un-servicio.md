# Turn off or delete a service

**In short:** to delete a service, in the list tap **Actions › Delete** on its row. To stop offering it without deleting it you have two options: turn it off at a location with **Activate services**, or set it to **Private** to take it out of online booking.

## Three ways to stop offering a service

| You want | What to do | What it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| A location to stop offering it | **Actions › Activate services** and turn off its switch. See [Activate services in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-servicios-en-una-sucursal). | The service stays in the catalog and at the other locations. |
| Clients not to see it when booking online | **Actions › Edit**, **General** tab, set the switch to **Private**, **Save**. See [Public or private service](/ayuda/servicios/servicio-publico-o-privado). | Your team can still book and charge it. |
| It to disappear from the catalog | **Actions › Delete** on the service's row. | Nothing: it's deleted for the whole barbershop. |

## Delete a service

1. In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Services**. Go to the **Services** tab.
2. Find the service and, on its row, tap **Actions**.
   ![The Actions menu of a row with View, Edit and Delete](/assets/es/servicios/la-lista-de-servicios/acciones-fila.png)
3. Tap **Delete**.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting applies to the whole barbershop, not just the location you have selected. If the service is offered at another location, turn it off with **Activate services** instead of deleting it.

> [!NOTE]
> What happens after **Delete** (whether it asks you to confirm, what happens to appointments already booked with that service and to its history in Metrics) hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed with a test service.

## About the “Active” status

The list's **Status** column shows **Active**. On the row there are only **View**, **Edit** and **Delete**, and in the form no switch was seen for making a service inactive.

> [!NOTE]
> How a service is made inactive and what the list shows when it is hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed once it's confirmed with the team.

## Frequently asked questions

**I deleted a service by mistake. Can I get it back?**
Whether it can be recovered hasn't been checked yet. The safest thing is to create it again with the same details and turn it on again at each location.

**Does deleting the service delete the appointments it already had?**
That hasn't been checked yet. Until it's confirmed, before deleting check in the calendar that there are no appointments left with that service.

**I just want Carlos to stop doing it.**
Don't delete the service. In **Actions › Edit**, **Team** tab, uncheck Carlos's box and tap **Save**. See [Create a service: who does it](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-servicio-equipo).
