# Edit a service

**In short:** in the service list, tap **Actions › Edit** on the service's row. The same form as creating opens, with the **General**, **Team** and **Advanced** tabs and a **Save** button in each one. Change what you need and tap **Save** in the tab you changed.

## View before editing

If you only want to look, tap **Actions › View**. **View service** opens, read-only, with the **Actions** button and the three tabs.

![The View service screen with the General tab: description, details and data](/assets/es/servicios/editar-un-servicio/ver.png)

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Name and **Description** | The service's name and its description. |
| **Details** | **Visibility** (Public or Private), **Category** and **Creation date**. |
| **Data** | **Amount** (the base price, for example $30.00) and **Service length (minutes)** (for example 15 Minutes). |

## Steps to edit

1. In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Services**. Go to the **Services** tab.
2. Find the service and, on its row, tap **Actions** › **Edit**.
   ![The Edit Service form with the General tab and the Save button](/assets/es/servicios/editar-un-servicio/editar.png)
3. Change what you need in the matching tab:

| What you want to change | Tab | Field |
|---|---|---|
| The name, price, length, category, whether it's public | **General** | The same ones as in [Create a service: general details](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-servicio) |
| Which barbers do it and their price or length | **Team** | The ones in [Create a service: who does it](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-servicio-equipo) |
| The days and hours it can be booked, multi-service, prep time | **Advanced** | The ones in [Create a service: advanced options](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-servicio-avanzado) |

4. Tap **Save** in that tab.

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows after **Save**, and whether a price change affects appointments already booked or only new ones, hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed with a test service.

> [!TIP]
> If you're going to change a barber's price on several services, go to their record instead (**Team › Actions › View**, **Services** tab): there you have all the services together.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I move a service to another category?**
Yes. In **General**, change the **Category** and tap **Save**.

**Does editing a service change it at every location?**
The service is one for the whole barbershop. A change to its name, base price or length is seen by every location where it's turned on.

**How do I take a service away without deleting it?**
See [Turn off or delete a service](/ayuda/servicios/desactivar-o-borrar-un-servicio).
