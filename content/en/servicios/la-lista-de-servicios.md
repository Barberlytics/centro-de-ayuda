# The service list

**In short:** the **Services** tab shows the **Service listing** in a table: the service, its category, its **Base price** and its **Status**. You can filter by category, search by name and, on each row, tap **Actions** to **View**, **Edit** or **Delete**.

## How to get there

1. In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Services**.
2. Tap the **Services** tab.
   ![The Service listing with the Categories filter, the search box and the table](/assets/es/servicios/la-lista-de-servicios/servicios.png)

## Filter and search

- **Categories**: a selector with **…** to see only the services in one category.
- The **search box**: type part of the name (“Haircut”) and the list narrows down.

## What's in each column

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Service** | The service's name, for example “Classic haircut”. |
| **Category** | The category it belongs to, for example “Cuts”. |
| **Base price** | The service's price before any per-barber or per-client-type price. [Service cost and base price](/ayuda/servicios/costo-y-precio-de-un-servicio) explains it. |
| **Status** | **Active** when the service is in use. |
| **Action** | The **Actions** button for that row. |

## Each row's actions

Tap **Actions** on the service's row:

![The Actions menu of a row with View, Edit and Delete](/assets/es/servicios/la-lista-de-servicios/acciones-fila.png)

| Option | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **View** | The **View service** screen, read-only, with the General, Team and Advanced tabs. |
| **Edit** | The same form as creating, with **Save** in each tab. See [Edit a service](/ayuda/servicios/editar-un-servicio). |
| **Delete** | Removes the service. See [Turn off or delete a service](/ayuda/servicios/desactivar-o-borrar-un-servicio). |

## The combo list

The **Combos** tab shows the **Combo list** with how many you have (“4 Combos”) and the same table: the combo, its category, its price and its status, with the same **Actions** menu on each row. For example, “Haircut + beard · Combos · 75.00 · Active”.

![The Combo list with the same table as the services one](/assets/es/servicios/la-lista-de-servicios/combos.png)

## Frequently asked questions

**Why doesn't a service I know exists show up?**
Almost always it's because it isn't turned on at the location you have selected at the top. Turn it on from **Actions** › **Activate services** ([Activate services in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-servicios-en-una-sucursal)).

**The price I see here isn't what Carlos charges.**
The list shows the **Base price**. Each barber can have their own **Service price** in the Team tab. See [Create a service: who does it](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-servicio-equipo).

**Do the barbers see this list?**
With the commission barber profile, no: **Services & Products** doesn't show up in their menu.
