# The order of services when booking

**In short:** in **Services**, tap **Actions › Booking order**. You'll see your categories in a numbered list. Reorder them and tap **Save**. That's the order in which your client sees the categories when booking online.

## Steps

1. In the side menu, open **Services & Products** and tap **Services**.
2. Tap **Actions** and choose **Booking order**. “Service ordering for online bookings” opens with the numbered list of **Categories**: 1, 2, 3…
   ![The Service ordering for online bookings screen with the numbered list of categories and the Cancel and Save buttons](/assets/es/servicios/orden-de-agendamiento/pantalla.png)
3. Move each category to the position you want. The first one in the list is the first one the client sees.
4. Tap **Save**. If you don't want to change anything, tap **Cancel**.

## What it orders and what it doesn't

| What | Ordered here |
|---|---|
| The **categories** in online booking | Yes |
| The services inside each category | How wasn't seen |
| The categories in the **Categories** tab on your screen | No: this screen is for online booking |

> [!NOTE]
> What the app shows after **Save**, and whether the order of the services inside a category can also be changed, hasn't been checked yet. It will be completed with a test and by looking at online booking as a client.

> [!TIP]
> Put what you sell most first. If nine out of ten clients book a haircut, make “Cuts” category 1. **Uncategorized** (services without a category) usually goes last.

## Frequently asked questions

**I changed the order and it looks the same on my screen.**
That's normal. This order is the one for online booking, not the one in your **Categories** tab.

**A category doesn't show up in the list to order.**
Check that the category exists in the **Categories** tab. If it's **Private**, the client doesn't see it; see [Public or private service](/ayuda/servicios/servicio-publico-o-privado).

**Is the order per location or for the whole barbershop?**
That hasn't been checked yet. The service catalog belongs to the whole barbershop, so the order most likely does too.
