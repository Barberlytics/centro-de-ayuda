# Public or private service: what the client sees when booking

**In short:** every service has a **Private / Public** switch. When **Public**, the service appears when your client books online. When **Private**, only your team sees it from the calendar. Categories and combos have the same switch.

## Where it is

In the service's **General** tab, below the **Description**, with the phrase “This option makes your service visible in online booking” and the **Private / Public** switch.

![The General tab of Create Service with the Private / Public switch](/assets/es/servicios/crear-un-servicio/general.png)

In **View service**, the **Details** block shows the **Visibility** (“Public” or “Private”), next to the **Category** and the **Creation date**.

## What changes

| Option | Client's online booking | Your team's calendar |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | The service appears in its category | Can be booked |
| **Private** | The service doesn't appear | Can be booked |

## When to leave a service on Private

- A service you only offer if the client asks for it in the chair (“Eyebrow design”).
- A special price you don't want to advertise (“Complimentary haircut”).
- A new service you're still trying out with the team.

## Three switches, three levels

The same switch is in three places:

| Where | What it hides |
|---|---|
| The **category** ([Create a service category](/ayuda/servicios/crear-una-categoria-de-servicios)) | The whole category in online booking. |
| The **service** | That service. |
| The **combo** ([Create a service combo](/ayuda/servicios/crear-un-combo)) | That combo. |

For your client to see a service when booking online, the service has to be **Public**, be in a visible category and be turned on at the location ([Activate services in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-servicios-en-una-sucursal)).

## Frequently asked questions

**I set the service to Public and the client doesn't see it.**
Check three things: that the category is also public, that the service is turned on at that location and that some barber at that location has it checked in the **Team** tab.

**Does private mean it can't be charged?**
No. Private only removes it from online booking. Your team still books and charges it.

**Can I change it later?**
Yes. In the list, go to **Actions › Edit**, **General** tab, and **Save**. See [Edit a service](/ayuda/servicios/editar-un-servicio).
