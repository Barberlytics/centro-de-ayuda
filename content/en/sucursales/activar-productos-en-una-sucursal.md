# Activate products in a location

**In short:** in **Products › Actions › Activate barbershop products** you choose, with one switch per product, which ones the location you have selected at the top sells, and you tap **Save**.

## Before you start

The product catalog is **one for the whole company**, and each location turns on the ones it sells. It's the same pattern as the team and the services ([Catalogs belong to the company and are activated per location](/ayuda/sucursales/catalogos-compartidos-entre-sucursales)).

## Steps

1. At the top, choose the location.
2. Tap **Products** in the menu. The screen says “In this section you can have full control of your products.”
3. Tap **Actions** and choose **Activate barbershop products**.
   ![The Products Actions menu](/assets/es/productos/como-se-organizan-tus-productos/menu-acciones.png)
4. “Activate product” opens. Read the instruction: “Select the products you want to import by turning them on with the switch. Click ‘Save’ once you've made your choices.”
   ![The Activate product screen with the product list and one switch per product](/assets/es/sucursales/activar-productos-en-una-sucursal/pantalla.png)
5. In the “product list” (“6 Products”), turn on the switch for each product that location sells.
6. Tap **Save**. To leave without changes, **Cancel**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you leave without tapping **Save**, the switches aren't applied.

> [!NOTE]
> This article was written by reading the screen. We still need to tap **Save** in a test location to confirm what you see right afterward and whether the product shows up right away in **Create quick sale**.

## What activating doesn't do

- **It doesn't decide who sells it.** The product's **Availability for sale** switch does that: “This feature allows barbers to sell these products.”
- **It doesn't make it visible online.** **Private / Public** decides that: “This option makes your product visible in the booking flow.” ([Public or private product](/ayuda/productos/producto-publico-o-privado)).
- **It doesn't activate the services.** They have their own screen ([Activate services in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-servicios-en-una-sucursal)).

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I stop barbers from selling a product?**
Edit the product and turn off **Availability for sale**.

**I activated the product and I don't see it when I sell.**
Make sure you're in the same location where you activated it. See [I can't see a service or product in my location](/ayuda/sucursales/no-veo-un-servicio-o-producto-en-mi-sucursal).

**Is inventory per location?**
The product form has no units field. How inventory is tracked per location isn't documented yet ([Inventory and units](/ayuda/productos/inventario-y-unidades)).
