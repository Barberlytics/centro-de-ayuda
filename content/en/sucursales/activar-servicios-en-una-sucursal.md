# Activate services in a location

**In short:** in **Services › Actions › Activate services** you choose, with one switch per service, which ones the location you have selected at the top offers, and you tap **Save**.

## Before you start

The service catalog is **one for the whole company** (30 services in the test account), and each location turns on its own. [Catalogs belong to the company and are activated per location](/ayuda/sucursales/catalogos-compartidos-entre-sucursales) explains it.

## Steps

1. At the top, choose the location.
2. Tap **Services** in the menu. The screen says “In this section you can have full control of your services.”
3. Tap **Actions** and choose **Activate services**.
   ![The Services Actions menu](/assets/es/servicios/como-se-organizan-tus-servicios/menu-acciones.png)
4. “Activate services” opens. Read the instruction: “Select the services you want to import by turning on the service with the switch. Click ‘Save’ once you've made your choices.”
   ![The Activate services screen with the services list and one switch per service](/assets/es/sucursales/activar-servicios-en-una-sucursal/pantalla.png)
5. In the “Services list” (“30 Services”), turn on the switch for each service that location offers.
6. Tap **Save**. To leave without changes, **Cancel**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you leave without tapping **Save**, the switches aren't applied.

> [!NOTE]
> This article was written by reading the screen. We still need to tap **Save** in a test location to confirm what you see right afterward and whether the activated services show up right away when you create an appointment.

## What activating doesn't do

- **It doesn't change the price.** The price is the catalog's. Each barber's own prices are set in their record, **Services** tab ([A barber's record: services](/ayuda/equipo/ficha-de-un-barbero-servicios)).
- **It doesn't make it visible online.** The service's **Private / Public** switch decides that: “This option makes your service visible in online booking” ([Public or private service](/ayuda/servicios/servicio-publico-o-privado)).
- **It doesn't activate the products.** Products have their own screen ([Activate products in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-productos-en-una-sucursal)).

## Frequently asked questions

**How many services can I activate?**
As many as you want from your company's catalog.

**I activated the service and I don't see it when I book.**
Make sure you're in the same location where you activated it and that the barber has it in their record. See [I can't see a service or product in my location](/ayuda/sucursales/no-veo-un-servicio-o-producto-en-mi-sucursal).

**What about combos?**
The screen shows the “Services list.” Whether combos are activated here or separately hasn't been checked yet.
