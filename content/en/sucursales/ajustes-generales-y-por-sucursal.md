# General settings and per-location settings: where each one is changed

**In short:** the **Settings** options belong to the whole company. Each location's own details (currency, language, time zone) are edited in **Settings › Locations**. Several screens say they can be adjusted by location, but where isn't documented yet.

## Company-wide settings

They look the same no matter which location you've selected at the top:

- **Team:** Permissions, Payroll, Commissions.
- **Sales:** Taxes, Credit card usage fee, Tips, Prices, Payment integration.
- **Notifications:** Notification center.
- **Account settings:** Business details, Billing details and invoices, Online booking, Business closure periods, Payment devices, Locations and Company.

## Each location's details

In **Settings › Locations › Actions › Edit**: Currency, Language, Time zone, address and phone. See [What changes from one location to another](/ayuda/sucursales/que-es-propio-de-cada-sucursal).

## What the screens say

In **Commissions**, **Taxes**, **Tips** and **Business closure periods**, the app says: “This is a general setting, but it can be adjusted for each location in the corresponding section.” And in **Permissions**: “they can be customized individually by location.”

> [!NOTE]
> With an owner account, these screens look identical in every location. From what we know so far, the per-location adjustment appears when you sign in as someone with permission to edit that location. We still need to check this.
