# Delete a location

**In short:** in **Settings › Locations**, the row's **Actions** menu has **Delete**. If you just want to take the location out of use, use **Deactivate** instead of deleting it.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting usually can't be undone. This option wasn't run while writing this article: it isn't confirmed whether the app asks you to confirm, what happens to that location's clients, appointments, Transactions accounts and Payroll, or whether it can be recovered. Don't delete a location that has history without checking with support first.

## Where it is

1. Tap **Settings** and open **Locations**.
2. On the location's row, tap **Actions**.
   ![A location's Actions menu with View, Edit and Delete](/assets/es/sucursales/empresa-y-sucursales/acciones-fila.png)
3. Choose **Delete**.

## Before you delete

1. **Download what you need.** That location's **Payroll**, by cycle, with **Download** ([Download payroll](/ayuda/nomina/descargar-la-nomina)).
2. **Collect what's pending.** In **Transactions**, choose the location at the top and filter by **Bill status › To collect**.
3. **Move your team.** Activate the people in the location where they'll keep working ([Activate the team in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-equipo-en-una-sucursal)).
4. **Think about deactivating.** Inside **View › Actions** is **Deactivate**, which takes it out of use without removing it ([Deactivate a location](/ayuda/sucursales/inactivar-una-sucursal)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Does deleting the location delete my barbers?**
People belong to the company, not to the location. What happens to their activation when you delete the location isn't confirmed. To remove a person, use **Delete** on their row in **Team** ([Delete a team member](/ayuda/equipo/borrar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo)).

**What about my clients?**
Each client shows up with their **Location** in the **Clients** list. What happens to them when you delete the location is still to be confirmed.

**I deleted a location by mistake.**
Write to support as soon as you can with the location's name and the date.
