# How your barbershop is organized: company, locations, team and catalogs

**In short:** you have **one company** and, inside it, one or more **locations**. Your team, your services and your products are created **only once** for the whole company, and then you **activate** them in each location where they're used.

## The map

| Piece | Who it belongs to | What you do with it |
|---|---|---|
| **Company** | There's only one. The general settings live there | You see it in **Settings › Company** |
| **Locations** | Each location has its own currency, time zone, language and schedule | They're created and edited in **Settings › Locations** |
| **Team** | The whole company | Created once and **activated** in each location where the person works |
| **Services** | The whole company | Created once and **activated** in each location where they're offered |
| **Products** | The whole company | Created once and **activated** in each location where they're sold |
| **Permissions, taxes, tips, commissions, payroll and notices** | The whole company | Changed in **Settings** |

## The rule you'll use most

**Create once, activate per location.** It's the same rule for the team, the services and the products.

That's why a new location starts out empty: you won't see anyone from the team, or any services or products, until you activate them there. And that's why, if something “doesn't show up” in a location, it's almost always because it still needs to be activated.

## What you see on each screen

- **At the top, the location's name** decides whose data you see. Switch locations from there, or choose **All locations** to see everything together.
- **Calendar, Clients, Team and Metrics** change with the location you chose.
- **Settings** looks the same in any location, because it belongs to the company.

> [!NOTE]
> What you see also depends on your profile and your permissions. Read [What decides what you see](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos/de-que-depende-lo-que-ves).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I have more than one location?**
Yes. They're created in **Settings › Locations** with the **Create Location** button. How many your plan allows isn't documented yet.

**Can a barber work in two locations?**
Yes. They're a single person on the company's team, and you activate them in both locations.

**Do services have the same price in every location?**
The catalog is a single one, and the currency belongs to each location. Whether the price can vary from one location to another isn't documented yet.
