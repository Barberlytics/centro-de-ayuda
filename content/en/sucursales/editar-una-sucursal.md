# Edit a location's details

**In short:** in **Settings › Locations**, open **Actions** on the location's row and choose **Edit**. It's the same form as creating, with a **Save** button.

## Steps

1. Tap **Settings** and open **Locations**.
2. In the “Locations list,” tap **Actions** on the location's row.
   ![A location's Actions menu with View, Edit and Delete](/assets/es/sucursales/empresa-y-sucursales/acciones-fila.png)
3. Choose **Edit**. You can also get there from **View** › **Actions** › **Edit Location**.
   ![The Edit Location form with the location's details](/assets/es/sucursales/editar-una-sucursal/formulario.png)
4. Change what you need.
5. Tap **Save**.

> [!NOTE]
> This article was written by reading the form, without saving changes. What the app shows after **Save** and what the client sees from each field (for example, the address in emails) will be completed with a test location.

## What isn't edited here

- **Who works at the location** is changed in **Team › Actions › Activate team in this location** ([Activate the team in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-equipo-en-una-sucursal)).
- **Opening hours** are in **Settings › Business details** (**Start time**, **End time**) and each barber's schedule is in **Team › Working hours**.
- **The services and products** it offers are activated in **Services** and **Products**.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I set my location's logo?**
The form has a **Photos** section (0/5). Where each photo shows up isn't documented yet.

**Can I change the currency when I already have appointments?**
The field can be edited. What happens to existing prices and accounts isn't documented: check with support first.

**Does editing the location change the company?**
No. The company's details are in **Settings › Company** ([Company and locations](/ayuda/sucursales/empresa-y-sucursales)).
