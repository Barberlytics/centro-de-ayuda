# Company and locations: how they differ

**In short:** the **company** is the whole business, with its ID and its phone number; you have just one. Each **location** is a shop where you serve clients, with its own currency, time zone, language and activated team. Both live in **Settings**, in the **Account settings** group.

## How they appear in Settings

| Card | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Locations** | “Manage the business's locations: create them, edit them and see their details.” |
| **Company** | “View and manage the company's details.” |

## The locations list

Tap **Settings › Locations**. The screen says “In this section you can have full control of your locations” and shows the “Locations list” with how many you have (“3 Locations”), a search box and the **Create Location** button.

![The locations list with its columns and the Create Location button](/assets/es/sucursales/empresa-y-sucursales/lista.png)

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The location's name, for example “Sucursal Norte” |
| **Team** | How many people are activated in it |
| **Country**, **State**, **City** | Where it is |
| **Date create** | When it was created |
| **Status** | **Active** |
| **Actions** | Each row's menu |

On each row, **Actions** has **View**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

![A location's Actions menu with View, Edit and Delete](/assets/es/sucursales/empresa-y-sucursales/acciones-fila.png)

- **View** opens the detail: clients, team, license and the profile ([See a location's details](/ayuda/sucursales/ver-el-detalle-de-una-sucursal)).
- **Edit** opens the form with all its details ([Edit a location's details](/ayuda/sucursales/editar-una-sucursal)).
- **Delete** removes it ([Delete a location](/ayuda/sucursales/borrar-una-sucursal)). Inside **View**, **Actions** also offers **Deactivate** ([Deactivate a location](/ayuda/sucursales/inactivar-una-sucursal)).

## The company list

Tap **Settings › Company**. The screen is called “Companies,” says “In this section you can have full control of your companies.” and shows the “Company list” (“1 Company”) with the columns **Company**, **ID**, **Phone**, **Status** and **Action**, whose menu has **View** and **Edit**.

![The company list with one row](/assets/es/configuracion/empresa/lista.png)

The details are in [Company: your company's details](/ayuda/configuracion/empresa).

## What belongs to each

| | Company | Location |
|---|---|---|
| How many | One | One or several |
| Its own details | Name, ID, phone | Name, ID, address, phone, **Currency**, **Language**, **Time zone**, photos |
| What it groups | The locations, the team, the catalogs and the Settings options | The people, services and products **activated** in it; its clients and its calendar |

The selector at the top lists the locations and **All locations**; when you choose one, the calendar, clients, team and metrics switch to that location. The **Settings** screens look the same in any of them because they belong to the company.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between deactivating and deleting a location?**
Both options exist: **Deactivate** inside **View › Actions** and **Delete** in the row's menu. What each one keeps isn't documented yet. Don't delete a location if you're not sure.

**Can I have more than one company?**
The test account's list shows “1 Company.” Whether the plan allows more isn't documented.

**How do I switch locations to work in another one?**
With the location name at the top. See [Switch locations](/ayuda/navegacion/cambiar-de-sucursal).
