# The team belongs to the company and is activated in each location

**In short:** everyone on your team belongs to the company. In **Team** you only see the people who are **activated in the location** you chose at the top.

## How it works

- A person is created **only once**.
- Then you **activate** them in each location where they work.
- If you activate them in two locations, it's the same person in both: they aren't duplicated.
- The **Team** list changes depending on the location you chose.

For example: if your company has 21 commission barbers and you only activated 10 in one location, that location shows 10.

## Where it's activated

In **Team**, tap **Actions** and choose **Activate team in this location**. There you'll find one switch per person, split into **Barbers** and **Managers**, and inside each one into **Commission** and **Rent**.

The full guide is in [Activate the team in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-equipo-en-una-sucursal).

## Frequently asked questions

**I can't see a barber who does work with me.**
Most likely they aren't activated in this location. See [I can't see a barber in my location](/ayuda/sucursales/no-veo-a-un-barbero-en-mi-sucursal).

**Can a barber work in two locations?**
Yes: activate them in both.

**Does switching locations change the team I see?**
Yes. Each location shows only the people it has activated.
