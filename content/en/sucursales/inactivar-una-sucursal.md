# Deactivate a location

**In short:** the option is inside the location: **Settings › Locations › Actions › View** and, on that screen, **Actions › Deactivate**. It's different from **Delete**, which is in the row's menu.

> [!WARNING]
> This option wasn't run while writing this article. What an inactive location keeps (its clients, its appointments, its history in Transactions and Payroll, its license) and whether it can be activated again isn't confirmed yet. Before deactivating a location that has history, check with support.

## Where it is

1. Tap **Settings** and open **Locations**.
2. On the location's row, tap **Actions** and choose **View**.
   ![The View location screen with its Actions button](/assets/es/sucursales/ver-el-detalle-de-una-sucursal/pantalla.png)
3. Tap **Actions**, at the top right, and choose **Deactivate**.

## Deactivate or delete

| | Where it is | What it seems meant for |
|---|---|---|
| **Deactivate** | Inside **View**, in **Actions** | Taking the location out of use without removing it from the list |
| **Delete** | In the **Actions** menu on the list row | Removing it ([Delete a location](/ayuda/sucursales/borrar-una-sucursal)) |

The **Status** column in the “Locations list” shows **Active** for locations in use. Whether an inactive location shows up there with a different status still needs to be checked.

## Before deactivating

- Make sure no appointments are still booked at that location: choose it at the top and look at the **Calendar**.
- Make sure nothing is left **To collect** in **Transactions**.
- Download the last cycle's **Payroll** ([Download payroll](/ayuda/nomina/descargar-la-nomina)).
- If its team is moving to another location, activate them there ([Activate the team in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-equipo-en-una-sucursal)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Does deactivating a location deactivate its team?**
It isn't confirmed. People belong to the company; to take someone out of use, there's the **Deactivate** option on their row in **Team** ([Deactivate a team member](/ayuda/equipo/inactivar-a-un-miembro-del-equipo)).

**Can I activate it again?**
We didn't see a reactivate option. Still to be confirmed.

**Is an inactive location's license still charged?**
Still to be confirmed with the team.
