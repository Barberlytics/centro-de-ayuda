# I can't see a barber in my location

**In short:** the team belongs to the whole company, but each location shows only the people it has **activated**. If a barber doesn't show up, activate them in that location.

## What to do

1. Look at the location's name at the top. Make sure it's the location where you expect to see the barber.
2. Go to **Team**, tap **Actions** and choose **Activate team in this location**.
3. Find the person under **Barbers** (or **Managers**) and, inside, under **Commission** or **Rent**.
4. Turn on their switch and tap **Save**.

Full details in [Activate the team in a location](/ayuda/sucursales/activar-equipo-en-una-sucursal).

## If they're already activated and still don't show up

- In the **Calendar**, check the **Team\*** filter. See [A barber doesn't show up on the calendar](/ayuda/calendario/un-barbero-no-aparece-en-el-calendario).
- If you signed in with a barber profile, in the calendar you only see **your** column.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to create the barber again?**
No. They already exist in your company. You only need to activate them in the location.

**Can I activate the same barber in two locations?**
Yes.
