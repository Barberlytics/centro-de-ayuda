# I can't see a service or product in my location

**In short:** services and products belong to the whole company, but each location shows only the ones it has **activated**. If one is missing, activate it in your location.

## A service

1. At the top, check that you're in the right location.
2. Go to **Services**, tap **Actions** and choose **Activate services**.
3. Turn on the service's switch and save.

## A product

1. Go to **Products**, tap **Actions** and choose **Activate barbershop products**.
2. Turn on the product's switch and save.

## If it still doesn't show up

- It may not exist yet. Create it with **Create Service** or **Create product**.
- With the commission barber profile, **Services & Products** doesn't appear in the menu.

## Frequently asked questions

**I opened a new location and it's empty.**
That's normal: a new location comes with no team, services or products until you activate them.

**Does activating a service change it in the other locations?**
Activation is per location, so it doesn't touch the others.
