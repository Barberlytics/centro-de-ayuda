# What changes from one location to another: currency, time zone, language and schedule

**In short:** each location has its own **currency**, **time zone** and **language**. That's why you can have one location in Colombia and another in the United States without mixing up times or prices.

## What's set at each location

You see them when you edit the location, in **Settings › Locations › Actions › Edit**:

| Detail | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Currency\*** | The currency that location's prices are shown in |
| **Language\*** | The language that location uses with its clients |
| **Time zone\*** | The time its calendar is shown in |
| **Country\***, **State\***, **City\***, **Address**, **Postal code**, **Phone\*** | Where it is and how to contact it |

The fields with an asterisk are required.

## What belongs to the whole company

These settings are in **Settings** and look the same no matter which location you've chosen at the top: permissions, taxes, tips, commissions, the payroll pay cycle, notifications and closure periods.

> [!NOTE]
> Several screens say “This is a general setting, but it can be adjusted for each location in the corresponding section.” Where that per-location adjustment is made isn't documented yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**My location is in another country. What do I set?**
When you create or edit it, choose that location's **Country**, **Currency**, **Language** and **Time zone**.

**Does changing a location's currency change the prices it already had?**
It isn't documented yet. Before you change it, check with support.
