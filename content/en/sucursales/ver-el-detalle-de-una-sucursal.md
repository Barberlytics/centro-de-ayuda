# See a location's details: clients, team and license

**In short:** in **Settings › Locations**, **Actions › View** opens the location. At the top you see three figures, **Clients**, **Team** and **License**, and below them its profile. The **Actions** button on that screen offers **Edit Location** and **Deactivate**.

## How to get there

1. Tap **Settings** and open **Locations**.
2. On the location's row, tap **Actions** and choose **View**.
   ![The View location screen with the three figures and the profile](/assets/es/sucursales/ver-el-detalle-de-una-sucursal/pantalla.png)

## The three figures

| Figure | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Clients** | How many clients that location has | 33 |
| **Team** | How many people are activated in it | 11 |
| **License** | The date the location's license expires | 28/AUG/2027 |

## The profile

Below, the location's details: **Name**, **ID**, **Country**, **State**, **City**, **Address**, **Language**, **Phone**, **Postal code** and **Description**, with its logo or photo. To change them, tap **Actions › Edit Location** ([Edit a location's details](/ayuda/sucursales/editar-una-sucursal)).

## The Actions menu

- **Edit Location**: opens the form with all the fields.
- **Deactivate**: takes the location out of use ([Deactivate a location](/ayuda/sucursales/inactivar-una-sucursal)).

> [!NOTE]
> What the **License** includes, what happens when it expires and how it relates to **Billing details and invoices** (where the test account shows “Your next invoice will be available on AUG 28, 2027,” the same date) is still to be confirmed with the team. It isn't confirmed either whether **Clients** counts only those who have had appointments there.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does Team say 11 if I see 10 barbers in Team?**
The figure adds up everyone activated in the location, including managers and rent barbers. The **Barbers** tab in Team separates **Commission barber** and **Rent barber**.

**Where do I renew the license?**
What you pay Barberlytics is in **My profile** and in **Settings › Billing details and invoices** ([Billing details and invoices](/ayuda/configuracion/detalles-de-facturacion-y-facturas)). Whether each location's license is renewed there is still to be confirmed.

**I typed the location's address in the browser and it comes up empty.**
Always come in from the list, with **Actions › View**.
