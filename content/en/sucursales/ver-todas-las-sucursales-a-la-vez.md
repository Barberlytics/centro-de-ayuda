# See all locations at once

**In short:** tap the location's name at the top, and at the end of the list choose **All locations** to see the data for all of them together.

## Steps

1. Tap the location's name at the top.
2. If the list includes **All locations**, choose it.

To go back to a single one, repeat step 1 and choose that location. The details are in [Switch locations](/ayuda/navegacion/cambiar-de-sucursal).

## What to expect

- The **Settings** screens look the same in any location, because they belong to the whole company.
- If you only have a barber profile, you see the data for your own work.

> [!WARNING]
> In the **Calendar**, the list of locations includes only the locations, without **All locations**. Which screens show that option isn't confirmed yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Which screens combine their data when you choose **All locations**, and which ones ask you to pick one, isn't documented yet. For a single location's metrics, the location filter is inside **Metrics**.

## Frequently asked questions

**I want to compare two locations.**
Choose each one separately and compare the figures in **Metrics**.
