# Search for a transaction

**In short:** next to the **Transactions** title there's a **magnifying glass** icon. Tap it, type in **Search** and the table narrows down. If the bill doesn't show up, it's almost always outside the **FROM** / **TO** range.

## Steps

1. Tap **Transactions** in the menu.
2. Tap the **magnifying glass** next to the title. The **Search** field appears.
   ![The Search field open next to the Transactions title](/assets/es/transacciones/buscar-una-transaccion/buscador.png)
3. Type what you're looking for.
4. If it still doesn't show up, widen **FROM** and **TO** and tap **Apply** ([Filter Transactions](/ayuda/transacciones/filtrar-transacciones)).

> [!NOTE]
> What the field searches by (client name, phone, invoice number, barber) couldn't be checked during the reading session. It will be confirmed with the screenshot.

## The refresh button

Next to the magnifying glass there's a **refresh** icon. It reloads the table with the filters you have. Use it when you just charged in another tab and the bill still shows **TO COLLECT**.

## The header's magnifying glass is something else

The magnifying glass at the very top, next to the location name, opens the appointment **Search** screen. It's for finding an appointment, not a bill. For bills, use the magnifying glass in **Transactions**.

## Frequently asked questions

**I'm searching for Laura and she doesn't show up.**
Check the range: by default you only see from the 1st of the month through today. If her appointment was last month, move **FROM**.

**Can I search by invoice number?**
That's not confirmed. If it doesn't work, sort the table by **Invoice** by tapping the column title.

**I want to see all of a client's bills.**
Open their record in **Clients**, **Payment methods** tab, where their transactions are ([A client's payments](/ayuda/clientes/pagos-de-un-cliente)).
