# How to read Transactions: bills, payments and what's pending

**In short:** **Transactions** is the barbershop's cash register. At the top, five cards with what was billed, collected, still to collect, tipped and refunded in the period. Below, a table with every bill: date, invoice, client, who served, status, total, collected and payment method. By default you see from the 1st of the month through today.

![The full Transactions screen: header, filters, the five cards and the table of bills](/assets/es/transacciones/como-leer-transacciones/pantalla.png)

## What you see, top to bottom

1. The **Transactions** title with a **magnifying glass** icon (opens the search) and a **refresh** one ([Search for a transaction](/ayuda/transacciones/buscar-una-transaccion)).
2. The header: the location, the range and how many bills there are, in the form “Mi Barbería · 01 SEP – 25 SEP 2026 · 38 bills.”
3. The **FROM**, **TO**, **BILL STATUS** and **APPOINTMENT STATUS** filters, with the **Apply** button ([Filter Transactions](/ayuda/transacciones/filtrar-transacciones)).
4. The five cards: **BILLED**, **COLLECTED**, **TO COLLECT**, **TIPS** and **REFUNDED** ([The five cards](/ayuda/transacciones/las-cinco-tarjetas-de-transacciones)).
5. The table of bills.

## The table

Each row is a bill: an appointment, a walk-in sale or a quick sale. You can sort it by tapping a column title.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Day and time of the bill, for example “25 SEP 10:16 AM” |
| **Invoice** | The invoice number. “—” if it doesn't have one yet |
| **Client** | The client's name and phone. “Walk in” if they aren't registered ([Bills for unregistered clients](/ayuda/transacciones/clientes-walk-in-en-transacciones)) |
| **Served by** | The barber and how many services (“1 service”) |
| **Status** | **TO COLLECT** or **COLLECTED** ([Bill statuses](/ayuda/transacciones/estados-de-una-cuenta)) |
| **Total** | What the bill is worth |
| **Collected** | How much of that total has already been paid |
| **Method** | How it was paid, for example “CASH.” “—” if it hasn't been charged yet ([Payment methods you can charge](/ayuda/transacciones/metodos-de-pago-aceptados)) |

Each row expands to show the bill's details ([See a bill's details](/ayuda/transacciones/ver-el-detalle-de-una-cuenta)).

## Frequently asked questions

**How much did I collect today?**
Set **FROM** and **TO** to today's date, tap **Apply** and look at the **COLLECTED** card.

**What's left to collect?**
The **TO COLLECT** card gives you the total and how many open bills there are. To see them one by one, filter by **BILL STATUS › To collect**.

**Is this where I see what I pay Barberlytics?**
No. That's in **My profile** and in **Settings › Billing details and invoices**. **Transactions** has your barbershop's payments from your clients.
