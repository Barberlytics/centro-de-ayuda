# A bill ended up uncollected

**In short:** a bill that's **TO COLLECT** is an appointment, walk-in sale or quick sale that nobody closed with a payment. Find it in **Transactions** with **BILL STATUS › To collect**, or in the calendar with **Actions › Payment list**, and charge it from the appointment with **Pay appointment**.

## Find it

1. Tap **Transactions** in the menu.
2. In **BILL STATUS**, pick **To collect** and tap **Apply**. The **TO COLLECT** card tells you how many open bills there are.
3. Look at the **Date**, the **Client** and **Served by** to know which appointment it is.

You can also filter by **APPOINTMENT STATUS › Completed**: a completed appointment with a **TO COLLECT** bill is the one that slipped through.

## Charge it

1. Go to the **Calendar** and open it on the bill's date ([Move between dates](/ayuda/calendario/moverte-entre-fechas)).
2. Tap the appointment's card and, in **NEXT STEP**, tap **Pay appointment** ([Charge an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cobrar-una-cita)).
3. Pick the method and confirm.
4. Go back to **Transactions** and tap **refresh**. The bill should show as **COLLECTED** with its **Method**.

> [!TIP]
> When you close out the day, open **Actions › Payment list** in the calendar. The **To collect** tab says: “Here you'll find all your appointments pending payment” ([The payment list](/ayuda/calendario/lista-de-cobros)).

> [!NOTE]
> A button to charge directly from the **Transactions** row wasn't seen. If the expanded detail has one, it will be added here once it's documented ([See a bill's details](/ayuda/transacciones/ver-el-detalle-de-una-cuenta)).

## Why it matters

As long as a bill is **TO COLLECT**, it doesn't count in **COLLECTED** or in the barber's payroll. If Carlos is missing an appointment on his settlement, it's almost always here ([Payroll doesn't match what I expect](/ayuda/nomina/la-nomina-no-coincide-con-lo-que-espero)).

## Frequently asked questions

**The client left without paying and won't be back.**
An option to write a bill off wasn't seen. Cancel the appointment ([Cancel an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cancelar-una-cita)) and check in **Transactions** how the bill ends up.

**The bill is from a quick sale, not an appointment.**
It doesn't show up on the calendar as a card. How to close a quick sale that was left open is still to be confirmed.

**Can I charge a bill from a month ago?**
Yes. Open the calendar on that date and charge the appointment like any other. In **Transactions**, widen **FROM** to see it.
