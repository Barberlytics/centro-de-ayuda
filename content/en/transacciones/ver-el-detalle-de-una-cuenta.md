# See a bill's details

**In short:** each row in the **Transactions** table expands when you tap it. That's where the bill's details are.

## Steps

1. Tap **Transactions** in the menu.
2. Find the bill. If you need to, adjust the filters ([Filter Transactions](/ayuda/transacciones/filtrar-transacciones)) or use the magnifying glass ([Search for a transaction](/ayuda/transacciones/buscar-una-transaccion)).
3. Tap the row. It expands below, without leaving the table.
   ![A Transactions row expanded with the bill's details](/assets/es/transacciones/ver-el-detalle-de-una-cuenta/fila.png)
4. Tap the row again to close it.

> [!NOTE]
> Exactly what the expanded detail includes (the services and products, the tax, the tip, the payment by method) couldn't be seen during the reading session. This article will be filled in with the screenshot.

## What you already see on the row without expanding

**Date**, **Invoice**, **Client** (name and phone), **Served by** (barber and number of services), **Status**, **Total**, **Collected** and **Method**. [How to read Transactions](/ayuda/transacciones/como-leer-transacciones) describes it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the detail open another screen?**
No. It expands within the same row.

**Can I charge the bill from the detail?**
A charge button wasn't seen there. Charge from the appointment in the calendar ([Charge an appointment](/ayuda/calendario/cobrar-una-cita)).

**I want to see the whole appointment, not just the bill.**
Open the calendar on the bill's date and tap the appointment's card ([Open an appointment and see its details](/ayuda/calendario/abrir-una-cita)).
